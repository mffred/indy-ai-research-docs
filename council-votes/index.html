How this was made

This page is built from two sets of public records the Council posts on indy.gov: the meeting minutes and the full-council roll-call votes, for every meeting from January 2021 through September 2026.

  • Who voted how comes from the roll-call sheets where they exist. They're printed by the Council's voting system, one page per vote, but posted as scanned images, so the names were read with OCR and checked against the totals printed on each sheet. Where a sheet couldn't be read cleanly, or the city didn't post one, the vote comes from the minutes instead.
  • Titles, sponsors and rezoning details come from the minutes, where each proposal is read into the record, with the meeting agendas and the city's Council Proposal Search filling gaps. The proposal search also supplies each proposal's committee, who initiated it and a link to its full text.
  • Cross-check against the minutes: where both the minutes and a readable roll-call sheet cover the same vote, they list every councilor's vote identically 648 times out of 739 (88%). Most differences are the minutes still listing a councilor who had left, or naming someone "not voting" whom the sheet shows abstaining. Where they differ, this page follows the sheet, which the voting system prints.
  • Cross-check against the city's database: the city's own proposal database records the final yes-no count for most proposals. It matches the count on this page for 1,215 of 1,223 final votes (99%). The 8 that don't match are marked "Needs review", with the city's figure shown.
  • Routine group votes. Many proposals, most rezonings and ceremonial resolutions, are approved together in one motion or by unanimous consent. They're hidden by default because a group vote doesn't mean each councilor weighed in on each item. Tick "Include routine group votes" to see them.
  • Topics are assigned by matching keywords in each title, not by a person reading every proposal. A proposal can have several topics and some will be wrong; use the flag on any vote to tell us.
  • Names follow the records, which use surnames. First names are shown where the records or news coverage confirm them.

In all: 2,121 recorded votes on 1,956 proposals across 75 meetings, 1,144 of them routine group votes. 26 are marked as needing review.

Known gaps: the city posted roll-call sheets for only 5 of 2022's meetings (the minutes cover the rest); its link to the May 4, 2026 roll call is broken; and minutes for August and September 2026 aren't posted yet, so those two meetings come from the roll-call sheets alone and some titles are missing. Votes taken by voice, with no roll call, aren't included.

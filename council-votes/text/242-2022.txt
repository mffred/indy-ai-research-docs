Proposal No, 242, 2022

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2022 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 22, 2021) by an additional appropriation of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) for the purposes of the Marion County Information Services Agency.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To. provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2022 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Information Services Agency.

SECTION 2. The Marion County Information Services Agency requests an additional appropriation in the
Enhanced Access fund totaling thirty-four thousand dollars ($34,000) In character three and two-hundred sixteen thousand dollars ($216,000) in character four for the purposes of financing the cost of implementing closed captioning to all live and post-produced programming for Channel 16:

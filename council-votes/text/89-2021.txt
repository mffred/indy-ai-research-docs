CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 6, 2021
Proposal No. 89, 2021

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2021 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 20, 2020) by appropriating a total of nineteen million dollars ($19,000,000) for the purposes of the City
Non-Departmental.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended by the increase hereinafter stated for purposes of the City Non-
Departmental.

SECTION 2. The City Non-Departmental requests an additional appropriation totaling nineteen million dollars ($19,000,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund in character three for the purposes of funding design and site due diligence:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL

Consolidated 149,000,000 19,000,000
County General

SECTION 3. The City expects revenues totaling nineteen million dollars ($19,000,000) to reimburse the
Consolidated County General Fund from the issuance of future bonds.

SECTION 4. Upon approval of this and other pending proposals, the following unappropriated fund balances are projected to remain at the end of 2021:

Fund Projected 2020 Year-End Projected 2021 Year-End
Balance Balance

SECTION 5. This ordinance shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

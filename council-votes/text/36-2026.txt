CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2026
Proposal No. 36, 2026

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Linda Osborne to the Greater Virginia Avenue Corridor Economic
Improvement Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Greater Virginia Avenue Corridor Economic Improvement Board, the
Council appoints:

Linda Osborne

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending March 2, 2029. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6th day of April, 2026, at 7:14 p.m.

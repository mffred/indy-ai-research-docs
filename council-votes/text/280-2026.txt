CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO.    , 2026 
 
A SPECIAL ORDINANCE approving the issuance of “Indianapolis -Marion County Public Library General 
Obligation Bonds (2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long -Term Capital 
Improvement and Equipping Project)” in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Nineteen 
Million Dollars ($19,000,000). 
 
WHEREAS, on July 27, 2026, the Library Board (the “Library Board”) of the Indianapolis -Marion 
County Public Library, Indiana (the “Public Library”), adopted Resolution 26 -2026 (the “2026- 2027 
Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement and Equipping Project 
Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public 
Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis -Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds  (2026-2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement and 
Equipping Project), Series 2026 (or such other designation determined by the Public Library prior to the  time of sale or issuance) ” (collectively, the “ 2026-2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility 
Long-Term Capital Improvement and Equipping Project  Bonds”), in an original aggregate principal  amount not to exceed Nineteen Million Dollars ($19,000,000) , for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to  pay for the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement  and Equipping Project (as defined in the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long-
Term Capital Improvement and Equipping Project Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay  the costs of issuance of the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long-Term Capital 
Improvement and Equipping Project Bonds; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Library Board has requested the approval of the City -County Council of the issuance  of the 2026 -2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement and 
Equipping Project Bonds , pursuant to Indiana Code § 36- 3-6-9(c), as amended, and the City -County 
Council now finds that the issuance of the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility 
Long-Term Capital Improvement and Equipping Project Bonds should be approved; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1.  For the purpose of providing funds for the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and 
Multi-Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement and Equipping Project , including, but not limited to, the  costs of the issuance of the 2026- 2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long-Term Capital

Improvement and Equipping Project Bonds, the City-County Council does hereby approve the issuance of  the 2026-2027 Irvington Branch Renovation and Multi -Facility Long- Term Capital Improvement and

SECTION 2.  Any and all prior actions taken by any officer of the Public Library, the City or any member  of the Library Board or the City-County Council in connection and consistent with this Ordinance is hereby  ratified and approved.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 29, 2021
Proposal No. 333, 2021

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2021 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No.20, 2020) by an additional appropriation of two million one hundred forty-one thousand nine hundred dollars ($2,141,900) and the transfer of appropriation of seven hundred thousand dollars ($700,000) for purposes of the Department of Parks and Recreation.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since the adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended by the character transfers and character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of the Department of Parks and Recreation.

SECTION 2. The Department of Parks and Recreation requests additional appropriations totaling two million one hundred forty-one thousand nine hundred dollars ($2,141,900) in the Parks General Fund in Characters
Three and Four and the transfer of appropriations in the Parks General Fund totaling seven hundred thousand dollars ($700,000) from Character One to Character Four to finance land acquisition costs, the purchase of capital equipment, and for the design, construction and inspection of capital infrastructure projects:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR4 | CHARS TOTAL
Parks General Fund (700,000) 870,000 2,171,900 2,141,900

SECTION 3. In support of the additional appropriation provided in Section 2, additional revenues totaling one hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($175,000) are to be donated to the Department of Parks and
Recreation in contribution for land acquisition costs.

SECTION 4. In support of the additional appropriation provided in Section 2, funds totaling one million nine hundred sixty-six thousand nine hundred dollars ($1,966,900) are to be transferred from the Consolidated
County General Fund to the Parks General Fund.

SECTION 5. Upon approval of this and other pending approvals, the following unappropriated fund balances are projected to remain at the end of 2021:

Fund 2020 Year-End Balance Projected 2021 Year-End Balance
Parks General Fund 4,247,983 1,786,292

SECTION 6. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8"" day of November, 2021, at 8:20 p.m.

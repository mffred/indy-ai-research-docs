GENERAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION 2025 
Proposal No. 276, 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to direct the Office of Public Health and Safety to provide a  report to the City-County Council regarding the appropriations of the round III Elevation Grant program to  the Indianapolis Foundation.  
 
WHEREAS, Fiscal Ordinance No. 8, 2025 amended the City -County Annual Budget for 2025 (City -
County Fiscal Ordinance No. 6, 2024). As part of Fiscal Ordinance 8, 2025, the Office of Public Health and 
Safety sought an additional appropriation totaling two mil lion dollars ($2,000,000.00) in the Consolidated 
County General Fund in character three for the purposes of funding Elevation Grants provided to the 
Indianapolis Foundation, and  
 
WHEREAS, the City -County Council approved the appropriation requested by the Office of Public 
Health and Safety, but required that the Office of Public Health and Safety return to the City-County Council  with a detailed proposal regarding the Indianapolis Foundation’s scope of work; now, therefore  
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1. The Office of Public Health and Safety is directed to appear before the City -County Council  regarding the appropriation of the round III Elevation Grant program approved in Fiscal Ordinance No. 8,  2025. 
 
SECTION 2. The City-County Council will assign this General Resolution to the public safety and criminal  justice committee, which will hear the Office of Public Health and Safety’s presentation of its detailed  proposal regarding the Indianapolis Foundation’s scope of work.  
 
SECTION 3. It is acknowledged and recognized that no further action is required by the committee or the 
City-County Council. 
 
The report was received by the City-County Council this 22nd day of September, 2025, at 8:30 p.m.

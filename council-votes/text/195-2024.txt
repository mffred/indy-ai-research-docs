 I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan 
Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on May 15, 2024, following  a public hearing as required by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof  published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of  general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan Development 
Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment prayed for in  petition: 
 
2024-ZON-025  7500 East 30th Street (Approximate Address)   
Prop No. 195, 2024 Warren Township, Council District #9 
*DENIAL UPHELD Calumet Civil Contractors, Inc., by John Cross 
   UVV    6/3/2024 Rezoning of 68.45 acres from the C-S (FF) (FW) district to the C-
S (FF) (FW) district to provide for uses permitted under 2006-
ZON-110, in addition to commercial and building contractors and  outdoor storage and operations. 
 
 I FURTHER CERTIFY that the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members recommended denial of 2024-
ZON-025 to the City-County Council.

nd day of May 2024.

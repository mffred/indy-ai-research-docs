CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 7, 2025 
Proposal No. 90, 2025 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Volunteer Groups that Support Animal Welfare in the City.  
 
WHEREAS, throughout this month it has been brought to our attention that there is a strong presence  of individuals and groups who devote their time, money, and even their lives to protecting and caring for  animals; and 
 
WHEREAS, these volunteer groups provide essential services including shelter, rescue,  rehabilitation, and advocacy for the welfare of animals throughout the City; and  
 
WHEREAS, the tireless efforts of these organizations have not only saved countless animal lives but  also enriched the quality of life in our community by fostering a culture of compassion and responsibility;  and 
 
WHEREAS, while many dedicated organizations work ceaselessly to advance animal welfare, it is  impossible to name them all, yet each deserves our deepest gratitude and recognition; and  
 
WHEREAS, the collective impact of these volunteer groups has strengthened community bonds and  improved public awareness of the importance of caring for all living creatures: now, therefore;  
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1: The Council hereby recognizes and expresses profound gratitude to all volunteer groups  and individuals for their outstanding contributions to animal welfare in our City.  
 
SECTION 2: The Council extends its congratulations and sincere thanks to each organization and  individual whose unwavering commitment continues to make a significant difference in the lives of  animals and the community at large. 
 
SECTION 3: This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon its adoption and in accordance with all  applicable local ordinances. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3rd day of March, 2025, at 7:23 p.m.

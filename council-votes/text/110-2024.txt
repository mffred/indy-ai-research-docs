CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 11, 2024
Proposal No. 110, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Lawrence Central Bears Girls Basketball team.

WHEREAS, on February 24", 2024, the Lawrence Central Bears Girls Basketball team beat the Lake
Central Indians, 55-28, in the Class 4A Final; and

WHEREAS, the team, consisting of Seniors Katie Castro, Daneijah Jourdyn Wynn, and Demetria
Dempsey; Juniors Mariah Garrett, Jaylah Lampley, Melaya Hughes, Janiyah Russell and Laila Abdurraqib;
Sophomores Lola Lampley, and Anijah McKenzie; coached by Head Coach Jannon Lampley, Assistant
Coaches Bryce Stone, Holly Coulter, and Terrence Johnson, and Statistician Todd Coulter; accompanied by Lawrence Central Athletic Director Mosi Barnes, Assistant Athletic Director Mike Connors, Lawrence
Central Principal Franklyn Bush, and MSD of Lawrence Township Superintendent Dr. Shawn Smith; made history; and

WHEREAS, the Lawrence Central Bears kept their opponents to the lowest score ever in the history of
Indiana State 4A basketball championships, where they also brought home the first State Championship win for Lawrence Central Girls Basketball ever; and

WHEREAS, Coach Jannon Lampley became the first African American woman to head coach a state championship win in the more than 120 year history of the Indiana High School Athletic Association; and

WHEREAS, the individual players, who excel both on the court and academically, have garnered many, many awards in their own right, now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City of Indianap celebrates the Lawrence Central Girls Basketball Team’s commanding win at the Indiana State 4A Basketball Championship.

SECTION 2. The City of indianapolis is proud of the Lawrence Central Girls Basketball team for their accomplishments both on and off the court

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of April, 2024, at 7:27 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.   , 2020 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION calling on City government, through the Department of Public Works, to paint a  message condemning anti -police sentiment on Shelby Street, at an appropriate location between its  intersection with Cottage Avenue and its intersection with Olive Street. 
 
WHEREAS, Policing is a dangerous profession as evidenced by the U.S. Bureau of Labor occupational  fatality statistics demonstrating that police and sheriff’s patrol officers suffered 13.7 job-related fatalities per  100,000 full-time equivalent workers in 2018, compared with 3.5 for the U.S. workforce as a whole and 0.7  for professional and related occupations; and 
 
WHEREAS, nationally, there has been many reports and videos circulated on social media of police  officers being targeted for violence or ambushed and short-sighted calls to defund police departments; and 
 
WHEREAS, locally, four Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department officers have been murdered in  the line of duty since department was created in 2007 and there have been numerous occurrences of IMPD  officers having suspects shoot firearms at them in 2020; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Indianapolis economy is boosted by conventions and tourism wherein safety is a  necessity for visitors to spend money at locally owned businesses; and  
 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis has recently hosted major American sporting events, and such would no t be  possible without proactive policing and traffic control measures; and  
 
WHEREAS, the vast majority of IMPD officers fulfill their duty to protect and serve the Indianapolis  citizens daily typically with no recognition for their unseen heroics, kind gestures or extra-efforts to promote  the peace and remove criminal element from neighborhoods; and 
 
WHEREAS, governments may, and regularly do, also engage in official government speech on  important matters; and  
 
WHEREAS, government has an obligation to exp ress its condemnation of anti -police sentiment, in  official government speech, with a visible message—a message that will be clearly visible outside the walls  of this Council Chamber; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-4-  14.

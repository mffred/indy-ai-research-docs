CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 15, 2023
Proposal No. 88, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the grand opening of Hannah’s Memorial Playground.

WHEREAS, in September, 2021, seven-year-old first grader Hannah Crutchfield was taken from us in a tragic car crash at George W. Julian School 57 in Irvington that injured her mother and a George Julian crossing guard; and

WHEREAS, George Julian students created the idea to revamp the playground in Hannah's honor.
With support from parents, school families, faculty, and the PTSA, the George Julian family embarked on a playground project to serve children from kindergarten through 8" grade; and

WHEREAS, a massive community fundraising effort began, ultimately raising approximately $240,000 from local businesses, charities, labor, foundations and more than 740 individuals to cover the cost of new equipment, several murals, and clean-up of the former playground areas; and

WHEREAS, at the grand opening and ribbon-cutting celebration for Hannah’s Memorial Playground on
Saturday, February 25, 2023, Hannah’s mother, Cassandra Crutchfield, expressed how brave and adventurous her daughter was, and what a loving and playful spirit she had; and

WHEREAS, Hannah's Memorial Playground introduces new ways for children to play on equipment that brings all students together in a safe and welcoming space, allowing George Julian students a place to process the pain and grief associated with the loss of their special friend; and

WHEREAS, Hannah’s Memorial Playground at George Julian is now open to the entire community, where generations of Irvington children and families gather to play on the playground, enjoy the basketball courts, or harvest vegetables from the school’s community garden; and

WHEREAS, the playground serves as a legacy to Hannah Crutchfield, a vibrant space that will inspire students and their Irvington neighbors for years to come. It is also a testament of how the George Julian family, the Indianapolis Public Schools community, and the people of the Eastside will always hold Hannah and her family in our hearts; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council recognizes the grand opening of Hannah’s Memorial
Playground in Irvington, created in memory of seven-year-old first grader Hannah Crutchfield.

SECTION 2. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of March, 2023, at 7:43 p.m.

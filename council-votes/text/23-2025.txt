COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 35, 2025 
Proposal No. 23, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s appointment of Christopher Bailey as the Chief of 
Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department and until a successor is appointed and  confirmed. 
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 279-221 of the “Revised code of the 
Consolidated City and County,” the appointment of the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan 
Police Department is subject to the confirmation by the City-County Council; and  
 
 WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of 
Christopher Bailey to serve as the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department at  his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2025; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. Christopher Bailey is confirmed by the City- County Council to serve as the Chief of 
Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department at the pleasure of the mayor, for a term ending 
December 31, 2025, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.  
 
SECTON 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC §  36-3-4-14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3 rd day of March, 2025, at 7:28 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.       , 2024 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2024 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 14, 2023) by a transfer of appropriation of three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000), a reduction of  appropriation of nine hundred twelve thousand three hundred fifty dollars ($912,350)  and an additional  appropriation of seven million sixty-two thousand three hundred fifty dollars ($7,062,350) for the purposes of  the Marion County Sheriff. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION 
COUNTY, INDIANA:  
 
SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the increases and decreases hereinafter stated  for purposes of the Marion County Sheriff. 
 
SECTION 2. The Marion County Sheriff  requests a transfer of appropriation totaling  three hundred  thousand dollars ($300,000) in the County General Fund from character three to character one, a reduction  of appropriation totaling  nine hundred twelve thousand three hundred fifty dollars ($912,350)  in Medical 
Care for Inmates Fund  in character three, an additional appropriation totaling six million one hundred  seventeen thousand dollars  ($6,117,000) in Public Safety Income Tax Fund in character one, an  additional appropriation totaling seven hundred ninety -five thousand three hundred fifty dollars ($795,350)  in County General Fund in character one, and an additional appropriation totaling one hundred fifty  thousand dollars ($150,000) in Federal Grants -County Fund in character three for the purposes of funding  personnel costs and additional grant awards:

SECTION 5. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does not  intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the  appropriation for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or  project, or both, and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city -county council immediately upon  receipt of any information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

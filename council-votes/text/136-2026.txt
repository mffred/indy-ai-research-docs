Proposal No. 136, 2026

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No.12, 2025) by a transfer of three hundred thousand ($300,000) for the purpose of Marion County Forensic
Services Agency.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County- Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Forensic Services Agency.

SECTION 2. The Marion County Forensic Services Agency requests a transfer of appropriation totaling three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000) in the County General Fund from character three to character two for the purpose of purchasing laboratory supplies.

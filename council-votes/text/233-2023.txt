CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 15, 2023
Proposal No. 233, 2023

A GENERAL ORDINANCE approving the issuance of: (a) “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library
General Obligation Bonds (2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023A" in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000); (b) “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Nora
Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023B” in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000); and (c) “Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update
Project), Series 2023C’ in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred
Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000).

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board (the “Library Board”) of the Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library, Indiana (the “Public Library”), adopted Resolution 43-2023 (the “2023 Central
Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update
Project), Series 2023A” (collect ely, the “2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project
Bonds”), in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand
Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Central Library
Renovation and Facility Update Project (as defined in the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility
Update Project Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023
Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 44-2023 (the “2023 Nora Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion County Pu
Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023B" (collectively, the “2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds’), in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and
Facility Update Project (as defined in the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project
Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023 Nora Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds, and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 45-2023 (the *2023 Pike Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion County Public
Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023C” (collectively, the “2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds’) (the 2023
Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds, the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and
Facility Update Project Bonds and the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds, collectively, the "2023 Renovation and Facility Improvement Bonds’), in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project (as defined in the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds;
and

WHEREAS, the Library Board has requested the approval of the City-County Council of the issuance of each of the 2023 Renovation and Facility Update Bonds, pursuant to Indiana Code § 36-3-6-9(c), as amended, and the City-County Council now finds that the issuance of each of the 2023 Renovation and
Facility Update Bonds should be approved; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. For the purpose of providing funds for the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility
Update Project, including, but not limited to, the costs of the issuance of the 2023 Central Library
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds, the City-County Council does hereby approve the issuance of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds in one or more series,

SECTION 3.. For the purpose of providing funds for the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility
Update Project, including, but not limited to, the costs of the issuance of the 2023 Pike Branch

SECTION 4. Any and all prior actions taken by any officer of the Public Library, the City or any member of the Library Board or the City-County Council in connection and consistent with this Ordinance is hereby ratified and approved.

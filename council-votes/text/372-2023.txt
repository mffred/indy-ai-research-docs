CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 37, 2023
Proposal No. 372, 2023

ASPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. and October 16, 2023 as “AKA
Day at the City Hall.”

WHEREAS, Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, incorporated, was founded on January 15, 1908 with a mission to promote unity and friendship among college women, to study and help alleviate problems concerning girls and women in order to improve their social stature, to maintain a progressive interest in college life, and to be of “Service to All Mankind;” and

WHEREAS, in 1915, the sorority hosted its first political action conference with Illinois Congressman and human rights activist Martin B. Madden as the speaker; and

WHEREAS, through the years, the sorority's function has become more complex, branching out to engage members worldwide in advocacy and service projects to improve socioeconomic, racial, political, and environmental conditions; and

WHEREAS, Alpha Kappa Alpha's diverse, collaborative work throughout the United States, the
Caribbean, Europe, Africa, and United Arab Emirates integrates a high level of personal engagement with the strong bonds of sisterhood and a productive network of community partners; and

WHEREAS, on October 16, 2023, Central indiana members of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority,
Incorporated®, will participate in “AKA Day at the City Hail,” and

WHEREAS, the event is an opportunity for members to speak with local lawmakers, engage in the local
City-County Council meeting, advocate on a local level for critical issues including mental health and wellness, Black maternal health, infrastructure, economic development, criminal justice reform, etc.; and,

WHEREAS, the Central Region is home to 95 empowered and engaged chapters serving Illinois,
Indiana, Kentucky, Minnesota, Southeastern Missouri and Wisconsin; and

WHEREAS, Indiana is home to 20 Aipha Kappa Alpha chapters 8 which are located on college and university campuses and have 12 active alumnae groups in communities throughout the state; and

WHEREAS, there are nearly 900 active members of Alpha Kappa Alpha in the city of Indianapolis, representing the Kappa, Alpha Mu Omega, and Chi Chi Omega chapters, respectively; and

WHEREAS, there are many members of Alpha Kappa Alpha currently serving in Marion County and
Central indiana, working for their community in local government; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 7. The Indianapolis City-County Council declares October 16, 2023 as Alpha Kappa Alpha
Sorority, incorporated, Day at the City Hail in the City of Indianapolis.

SECTION 2. A copy of this resolution shall be transmitted to Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc., Alpha Mu
Omega and Chi-Chi Omega Chapters in recognition of all indianapolis chapters.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4 This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

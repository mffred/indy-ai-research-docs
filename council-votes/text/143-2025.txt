CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 58, 2025
Proposal No. 143, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Craig Mince to the Indianapolis City Market Corporation Board of
Directors.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis City Market Corporation Board of Directors, the Council reappoints:

Craig Mince
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2026. The person reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the

term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9th day of June, 2025, at 7:37 p.m.

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Nicole Martinez-LeGrand.

WHEREAS, Nicole Martinez-LeGrand is native Hoosier and Mexican-American who is a direct descendant of Indiana's pioneering Mexican colony that began in Northwest Indiana the late 1910s. She has served the City of Indianapolis as a community builder for the Mid North Quality of Life Plan convened by The Children's Museum of Indianapolis in the early 2010s and recently as a member of Board of Public
Works in 2022; and

WHEREAS, in 2016, the Indiana Historical Society sought to fill in a crucial historical gap in its 186- year-old collection, the missing narratives and voices of Indiana's largest growing demographics, Latino and Asians. Since that time Nicole had been leading their multicultural collecting initiatives as the Indiana
Historical Society's Multicultural Collections Curator, through statewide outreach and collecting. The collections comprise of audio recorded interviews, genealogical research and hand selected family photographs and documents. All previously unseen in an institution setting; and

WHEREAS, since 2018, there were two exhibits, accompanying online exhibits, and traveling exhibits for both Latino and Asian communities carefully detailing their history in Indiana. Nicole worked in coordination with Daniel Gonzales, Indiana Historical Society's Director of Exhibition Curation; and

cei

WHEREAS, in 2022 the debut publication, Hoosier Latinos: A Century of Struggle Service and Success, outlines the personal experiences of Latinos across Indiana through transcribed interviews and personal photos donated to the Indiana Historical Society. This book and research uncover some of Indiana's pioneering individuals, families, businesses and organizations. It is the first book to address Midwestern
Latino identity across the state and not a select region. By covering over 100+ years of history in Indiana, it combats the common deficit narrative of Latino's as recent arrivais. They have been a long part of
Indiana's democratic society enriching the quality of life of all Hoosiers.

WHEREAS, Nicole serves as a steward of Indianapolis Latino history through her contributions to the digital Encyclopedia of Indianapolis, highlighting long forgotten history such as the early founding of the
Mexican Consulate in Indianapolis dating back to 1907, Indianapolis's Lost Barrio and Farm worker advocacy during the early years of Chicano Civil Rights era. Nicole is the Multicultural Collections Curator at the Indiana Historical Society where she regularly contributes her research to the Indiana Historical
Society Blog; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Nicole Martinez-LeGrand.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily thanks Nicole Martinez-LeGrand for the many hours of research and contributions to the Indiana Historic Society her dedicated service to the City of Indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with iC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9" day of January, 2023, at 7:51 p.m.

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Percy Bland, Jr. to the Metropolitan Board of Zoning-Appeals, Division
I

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Metropolitan Board of Zoning Appeals, Division I, the Council appoints:
Percy Bland, Jr.

appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure-of the Council’ Upon expiration of the term, the

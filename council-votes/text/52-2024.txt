CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2024
Proposal No. 52, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Dr. Jessica L. Braidden for her steadfast resolve to effect positive change within communities.

WHEREAS, Dr. Jessica L. Braidden stands as a beacon of resilience, influence, and unwavering dedication, embodying the essence of an entrepreneurial luminary, an esteemed educator, and a community leader, consistently empowering individuals through her transformative journey marked by healing, growth, and monumental achievements; and

WHEREAS, her unyielding commitment to community service and empowerment has garnered widespread admiration, notably culminating in the prestigious Women's Organization of the Year award, a testament to her tireless endeavors with Deborah's Place, reflecting her steadfast resolve to effect positive change within communities; and

WHEREAS, Dr. Braidden's personal narrative, marked by triumph over adversity and the pursuit of healing, serves as an inspiration to countless souls, offering hope through her candid sharing of struggles and victories, emphasizing the profound importance of healing, self-compassion, and personal growth; and

WHEREAS, her entrepreneurial prowess shines through her successful ventures, including impactful nonprofit initiatives and influential media platforms, each a testament to her unwavering commitment to education, empowerment, and the creation of pathways for others to thrive; and

WHEREAS, Dr. Braidden's profound dedication to education and mentorship resonates through her ownership of esteemed medical training institutions, passionately guiding and empowering students toward impactful careers in healthcare, while extending mentorship to aspiring entrepreneurs and professionals, thereby fostering a legacy of knowledge and empowerment; and

WHEREAS, her indelible mark within the media industry and her literary journey stand as testaments to her resilience and unwavering pursuit of her writing passion despite obstacles, inspiring others through her relentless commitment to realizing her dreams; and

WHEREAS, may Dr. Braidden be celebrated as a guiding light, an exemplar of resilience, and an embodiment of empowerment for generations to come; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes Dr. Jessica L. Braidden for her steadfast resolve to effect positive change within communities.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis City-County Council greatly appreciates Dr. Braidden’s commitment to consistently empower individuals through non-profit initiatives, influential media platforms, education and mentorship, entrepreneurship, and inspirational leadership.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5" day of February, 2024, at 7:34 p.m.

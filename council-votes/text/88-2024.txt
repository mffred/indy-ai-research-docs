COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2024 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 23, 2024 
Proposal No. 88, 2024 
 
A PROPOSAL FOR A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving a schedule of regular council meetings for the year  2024. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. In proposal 365, 2023, t he City-County Council approved the following schedule of regular  meetings for the year 2024: 
 
 (1) Monday, January 8, 2024 (8) Monday, August 12, 2024 
 (2) Monday, February 5, 2024 (9) Monday, September 9, 2024 
 (3) Monday, March 4, 2024 (10) Monday, September 23, 2024 
 (4) Monday, April 8, 2024 (11) Monday, October 7, 2024 
 (5) Monday, May 13, 2024 (12) Monday, November 4, 2024 
 (6) Monday, June 3, 2024 (13) Monday, December 2, 2024 
 (7) Monday, July 8, 2024 
 
SECTION 2. The City-County Council hereby modifies the above schedule by moving the April meeting  date from April 8, 2024, to April 1, 2024. 
 
SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36- 3-4- 14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 4th day of March, 2024, at 7:19 p.m.

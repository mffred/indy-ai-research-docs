CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
             
I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development 
Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on September 7, 2022, a public hearing as required  by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and  “Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment  prayed for in petition: 
 
2022-ZON-086 8135 BROOKVILLE ROAD (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 351, 2022 WARREN TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #18  
R.O. No. 112, 2022 SUNBELT RENTALS, INC., by Murray Clark  
Rezoning of 3.70 acres from the C-4 district to the I-2 district to provide for  heavy equipment sales, service and repair. 
 
2022-ZON-087 15 AND 25 NORTH HIGHLAND AVENUE (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
Prop No. 352, 2022 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #17  
R.O. No. 113, 2022 APOGEE INDY, LLC, by Mark and Kim Crouch  
Rezoning of 0.17 acre from the SU-1 (TOD) district to the D-8 (TOD) district. 
 
2022-CZN-836 4800 EAST RAYMOND STREET (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 353, 2022 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #18  
R.O. No. 114, 2022 CONTINENTAL BROADCAST GROUP, LLC, by David E. Dearing 
Rezoning of 15.41 acres from the SU-5, D-2 and D-A (FW) (FF) districts to  the SU-5 (FW) (FF) district to provide for a radio broadcast station. 
 
2022-CZN-844 2545 EAST PLEASANT RUN PARKWAY NORTH DRIVE 
Prop No. 354, 2022 (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
R.O. No. 115, 2022 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #12  
INDIANAPOLIS BOND BANK, by Andrew B. Buroker  
Rezoning of 17.92 acres from the I-4 (FF) district to the SU-9 (FF)  district to provide for Marion County Forensic Laboratory and 
Coroner’s office. 
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance  prayed for in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended  2022-ZON-086, 2022-ZON-087, 2022-CZN-836, and 2022-CZN-844 to the City-County Council  for adoption to the end that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety from fire,  flood, and other danger may be secured, that property values may be preserved and that the  public health, comfort, morals and convenience and general welfare may be preserved. 
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the records of the Metropolitan Development Commission  of Marion County, Indiana, with regard to the above ordinance is as indicated on the attached  chart, which is incorporated herein by reference and made a part hereof.

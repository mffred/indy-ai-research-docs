COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2022 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2022 
Proposal No. 5, 2022 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s appointment of Brian D. Madison as the Director of the 
Department of Business and Neighborhood Services  for a term ending December 31, 2022, and until a  successor is appointed and confirmed. 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-3 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and 
County,” a mayoral appointment of Director of the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services is  subject to the approval of the City-County Council; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Brian D. 
Madison to serve as the Director of the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services at his pleasure  for a term ending December 31, 2022; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  Brian D. Madison is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Director of  the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services  for a term ending December 31, 2022, and until a  successor is appointed and confirmed. 
 
SECTION 2.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4- 14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7th day of February, 2022, at 7:40 p.m.

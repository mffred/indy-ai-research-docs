CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 12, 2025
Proposal No. 280, 2025

A SPECIAL ORDINANCE approving the issuance of “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library General
Obligation Bonds (2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update
Project), Series 2025” in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000).

WHEREAS, on July 28, 2025, the Library Board (the “Library Board”) of the Indianapolis-Marion County
Public Library, Indiana (the “Public Library”), adopted Resolution 25-2025 (the “2025-2026 Multi-Facility
Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-
Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital
Maintenance and Equipment Update Project), Series 2025” (collectively, the “2025-2026 Multi-Facility
Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds’), in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project (as defined in the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term
Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, the Library Board has requested the approval of the City-County Council of the issuance of the 2025-2026 MultiFacility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds, pursuant to Indiana Code § 36-3-6-9(c), as amended, and the City-County Council now finds that the issuance of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project
Bonds should be approved; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. For the purpose of providing funds for the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital
Maintenance and Equipment Update Project, including, but not limited to, the costs of the issuance of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds, the City-
County Council does hereby approve the issuance of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital
Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds in one or more series, as general obligation bonds of the Public Library, anticipated to be designated as “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library General
Obligation Bonds, Series 2025,” In an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Fifteen n
Dollars ($15,000,000), bearing interest at a rate or rates not to exceed six percent (6.00%) and having a final maturity no later than six (6) years from the date such 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital
Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds are issued.

SECTION 2. Any and ail prior actions taken by any officer of the Public Library, the City or any member of the Library Board or the City-County Council in connection and consistent with this Ordinance is hereby ratified and approved.

SECTION 3. This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code §§ 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15, 36-3-4-16 and 36-3-4-17, each as amended.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 22nd day of September, 2025, at 8:31 p.m.

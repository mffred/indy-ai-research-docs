CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 5, 2024
Proposal No. 289, 2024

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2024 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 14, 2023) by appropriating a fotal of one million and two hundred thousand dollars ($1,200,000) for the purposes of the Office of Finance and Management.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the increases hereinafter stated for purposes of the Office of
Finance and Management.

SECTION 2. The Office of Finance and Management requests an additional appropriation totaling one million, two hundred thousand dollars ($1,200,000) in the City Cumulative Capital Improvement Fund in character three for the purpose of funding a debt service reserve for anticipated debt issuance for the Animal Shelter.

Improvement

SECTION 3. In support of appropriation provided in Section 2, revenues totaling $1,200,000 are to be transferred from the Cumulative Capital Improvement Fund to the City Cumulative Capital Improvement
Fund.

SECTION 4. Upon approval of this and other pending proposais, the following unappropriated fund balances are projected to remain at the end of 2024:

Fund 2023 Year-End Balance, Projected 2024 Year-End Balance
City Cumulative Capital Improvement $15,242,418 $14,972,003

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 23 day of September, 2024, at 8:46 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 14, 2025
Proposal No. 191, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Department of Public Works for its recent awards and recognition by ACEC Indiana.

WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works was awarded the State Finalist Award, which recognizes those entries that demonstrate initiative and ingenuity in the field of engineering. Some projects were the 79 and Dean Road intersection improvements and Michigan Street Complete Streets, Phase | and II; and

WHEREAS, the Department of Pu Works was also awarded the Merit Award. Merit Awards are presented for projects that deserve special recognition for engineering excellence that meet the needs of the client and benefit public welfare. Broad Ripple Avenue improvement project and Spades Park Pogues
Run Greenway Glow Stone Trail were some of the projects; and

WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works also received the Honors Award, which showcases the engineering achievements based on criteria such as uniqueness and originality, technical complexity, social and economic value, and public awareness. The Emerson Avenue Corridor was recognized as one of the reasons for this award; and

WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works also received the 2025 ACPA Excellence in Concrete
Award, as well as several Merit Awards; including: Keystone Avenue South Stormwater Outfall,
Lochmueller Group, Nowland Avenue Historical Bridge Rehabilitation, RQAW/DCCM, PA-24-008 Fall
Creek Trail Phase 2, Bollinger Lach & Associates, Incorporated, and Indiana MS4 Partnership; and

WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works has received numerous awards for their work in the City of Indianapolis; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Department of Public Works for its many years of service to the Indianapolis community.

SECTION 2. The Council congratulates the Department of Public Works for their many accomplishments and wishes them continued future success.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9 day of June, 2025, at 7:34 p.m.

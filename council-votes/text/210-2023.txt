COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2023 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 63, 2023 
Proposal No. 210, 2023 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION renominating Mark Young for appointment to the Economic Development 
Commission. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Economic Development Commission, the Council renominates:  
 
Mark Young 
 
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending January 31, 2027.  The person  appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the  appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the  holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14 th day of August, 2023, at 8:05 p.m.

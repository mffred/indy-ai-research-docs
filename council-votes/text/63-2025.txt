COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
 
 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 41, 2025 
Proposal No. 63, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Brett Thomas to the Citizens’ Police Complaint Board. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Citizens’ Police Complaint Board, the Council reappoints:  
 
Brett Thomas 
 
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2027.  The  person reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the  term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to  exceed the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3 rd day of March, 2025, at 7:30 p.m.

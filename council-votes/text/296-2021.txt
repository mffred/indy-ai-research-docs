CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 89, 2021
Proposal No. 296, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Anthony Bridgeman to the Board of Business and Neighborhood
Services.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Business and Neighborhood Services, the Council reappoints:
Anthony Bridgeman

SECTION 2. The reappcintment made by this resolution is fora term ending December 31, 2021. The person

appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the

holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was. passed by the City-County Council this 18 day of Qctober, 2021, at 7:10 p.m.

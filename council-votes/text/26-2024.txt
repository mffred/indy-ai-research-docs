CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 1, 2024
Proposal No, 26, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing January 21-27, 2024 as National Coroner/Medicolegal Death
Investigator Professionals Week.

WHEREAS, the Marion County Coroner's Office employs Deputy Coroners certified as Medicolegal
Death Investigators (MDI’s), Forensic Autopsy Techs, and other support staff who provide services in
Indianapolis, Indiana and Marion County. They are the cornerstone of medicolegal death investigations.
They are professionals, who represent decedents, their families and their communities to ensure that suspicious, violent, unattended, unexplained and unexpected deaths that occur in Marion County are investigated with great integrity, and professionalism; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Coroner, Dr. Leeandrea Sloan, and her team ensure that every citizen is represented by a qualified and experienced medicolegal death investigator and staff fo ensure knowledgeable, comprehensive and compassionate investigations into the deaths of the community’s citizens and visitors; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Coroner’s Office aims to attain the highest quality medicolegal death investigation standards, which are consistent with national standards; and are an indispensable component of the public health and law enforcement systems, as they speak for those who can no longer speak for themselves; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby recognizes the week of January 21-27, 2024 as National
Coroner/Medicolegal Death Investigator Professionals Week

SECTION 2. The Council honors the work of the Coroners, Deputy Coroner, and Medicolegal Death
Investigations Professionals who serve Marion County citizens every day.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3- 4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8 day of January, 2024, at 7:21 p.m.

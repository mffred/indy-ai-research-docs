CITY‑COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.       , 2024 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City ‑County Annual Budget for 2024 (City -County Fiscal 
Ordinance No. 14, 2023) by an additional appropriation and transfer of appropriation totaling sixteen  million four hundred seventy thousand dollars ($16,470,000) for the purposes of the Department of Public 
Works. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY ‑COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF 
MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County- Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter  stated for purposes of the Department of Public Works. 
 
SECTION 2. The Department of Public Works  requests the transfer of appropriations totaling one  million six hundred ten thousand dollars ($1,610,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund from  character one to character three, additional appropriations totaling one million seven hundred fifty  thousand dollars ($1,750,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund in characters two and three, and  additional appropriations totaling one million five hundred dollars ($1,500,000) in the Capital Asset 
Lifecycle and Development Fund in character three to finance additional supply and contractual needs in  the operations and maintenance of the City-County fleet:

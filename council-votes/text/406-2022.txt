A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2022 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 22, 2021) by transferring and appropriating a total of nine million eighty-seven thousand nine hundred ten dollars ($9,087,910) for the purposes of those departments and agencies listed below.

CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.     , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION congratulating MacAllister Machinery Company, Inc., on the  occasion of its 80th anniversary, and recognizing its contributions to the economic vitality and civic life of 
Indianapolis and Marion County. 
 
WHEREAS, on June 2, 1945, E. W. MacAllister  accepted appointment as the exclusive Caterpillar  dealer for much of Indiana and founded MacAllister Machinery Company, Inc.; and  
 
WHEREAS, MacAllister Machinery has grown over eight decades into one of the nation’s leading 
Caterpillar dealers, providing heavy equipment sales, rentals, service, and parts that support construction,  infrastructure, agriculture, utilities, and public works across Indianapolis and the State of Indiana; and  
 
WHEREAS, MacAllister Machinery’s headquarters at 6300 Southeastern Avenue anchors significant  employment and investment on Indianapolis’s southeast side, reflecting the company’s long -term  commitment to doing business in our community; and 
 
WHEREAS, through the leadership and example of the MacAllister family, including the late P. E. 
MacAllister, the company has been a steadfast philanthropic partner to Indianapolis, supporting arts,  culture, parks, and numerous civic initiatives that enrich quality of life for residents across Marion County;  and 
 
WHEREAS, MacAllister Machinery marked its 80th anniversary on June 2, 2025, celebrating a record  of service, innovation, workforce development, and customer support that continues to power Central 
Indiana’s growth; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Council congratulates MacAllister Machinery Company, Inc., on its 80th anniversary  and extends sincere appreciation for its eight decades of investment, job creation, and community  partnership in Indianapolis. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Council recognizes the MacAllister family and the company’s employees for their  ongoing contributions to the economic vitality and civic life of Marion County.

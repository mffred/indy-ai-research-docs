A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing John Leslie (Wes) Montgomery.

WHEREAS, John Leslie (Wes) Montgomery, world famous Jazz guitarist and composer, was born in indianapolis, Indiana on March 6, 1923, and passed away June 15, 1968; and

WHEREAS, Wes, widely regarded as the greatest jazz guitarist of all time, was a self-taught musical prodigy who was renowned for his unique technique of plucking the strings with the side of his thumb and for his extensive use of octaves, which gave him a unique sound and set him apart from every other guitarist; and nonetheless, his ability to play anyplace with his instrument was made possible by his inability to read or write music; and

WHEREAS, not being able to read or write music freed him from being constrained by conventional playing techniques and allowed him to perform his instrument anyplace. Wes developed a new method for playing the instrument and rose to the top of every musical genre. Wes revolutionized jazz forever by rising to become a well-known jazz guitarist; and

WHEREAS, because of his distinctive style, Wes—who has been dubbed the “Father of Smooth
Jazz"—became one of the most significant figures in introducing newcomers fo Jazz. His soft tones and capacity to personalize any song are praised by musicians around. Distinguished Professor of Music and
Chairman of the Jazz Department at Indiana University's Jacobs School of Music, Dr. David Nathaniel was quoted as saying “He (Wes) was as great a teacher as he was a player’; and

WHEREAS, in addition to winning Grammys for "Going out of my head” (1966), "Willow weep for me” (1969), the Grammy Hall of Fame Award, and "The Incredible Jazz Guitar of Wes Montgomery” (1988),
Wes also garnered numerous other honors and awards, including the Recording Industry Association of
America (RIAA) Gold Album for "A day in the life"; and Wes Montgomery is now a representation of the greatness and brightness of Indiana history and Hoosier pride due to his originality and genius; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council recognizes world-famous jazz guitarist John Leslie (Wes) Montgomery for his many musical accomplishments.

SECTION 2. The Mayor is invited fo join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6"* day of March, 2023, at 7:25 p.m.

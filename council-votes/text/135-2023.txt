CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 20, 2023
Proposal No. 135, 2023

SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the United Brotherhood of Carpenters.

WHEREAS, working men and women are the backbones of this country and they deserve the support of their elected officials; and

WHEREAS, tax fraud, low wages, and exploitive practices in the construction industry cause profound hardship for many workers and their families; and

WHEREAS the right to a good paying job with benefits to support your family is a fundamental part of a strong middle class, and a strong middie class makes our city, state, and America strong; and

WHEREAS, the United Brotherhood of Carpenters and the Indiana/Kentucky/Ohio Regional Council of
Carpenters have a long history of fighting for the rights of workers, and of upholding a standard of living for their members that should be an example to all employers; and

WHEREAS, we support the efforts of the Carpenters union and its membership by advocating for and supporting legislation that will, among other things, eliminate tax fraud in the construction industry, end the exploitation of workers, and strengthen laws that help protect a worker's right to organize; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the United Brotherhood of
Carpenters and their efforts on behalf of their workers.

SECTION 2. The Council encourages the United Brotherhood of Carpenters to continue to support and advocate for their members to uphold their rights and standard of living.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8° day of May, 2023, at 8:01 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 37, 2026 
Proposal No. 241, 2026 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Allein Patton Hickman to the Greater Virginia Avenue Corridor  
Economic Improvement Board. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Greater Virginia Avenue Corridor  Economic Improvement Board, the 
Council appoints:  
 
Allein Patton Hickman 
 
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending September 14, 2029.  The  person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term,  the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed  the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14th day of September, 2026, at 7:13 p.m.

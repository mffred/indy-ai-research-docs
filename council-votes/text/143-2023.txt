COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2023 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 50, 2023 
Proposal No. 41, 2023 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A COUNCIL RESOLUTION confirming Mayor Joseph H. Hogsett’s appointment of Marcia 
Lewis as Executive Director of the Indianapolis Housing Agency.  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 285-421 of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” the 
Mayor’s appointment of the Executive Director of the Indianapolis Housing Agency is subject to confirmation  by the City-County Council; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the M ayor has appointed Marcia Lewis to be the Executive Director of the Indianapolis 
Housing Agency for a term of two (2) years; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Office of the Mayor has submitted the appointment of Marcia Lewis as Executive Director  of the Indianapolis Housing Agency for City-County Council confirmation pursuant to ordinance; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  Marcia Lewis is hereby confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Executive Director of  the Indianapolis Housing Agency. 
 
SECTION 2. Marcia Lewis’s term as Executive Director began on January 8, 2023 and will expire on January  7, 2025. 
 
SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and in compliance with 
Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6 th day of March, 2023, at 7:51 p.m.

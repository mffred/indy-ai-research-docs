ing Danny Camden, Indiana’s 2022 Career and Technical Education

WHEREAS, Danny Camden was once a student in the Automotive Service program that he now leads.
He has taken the program from a low-level tire and lube shop to an ASE-certified program that offers multiple certifications and a technical certificate from Ivy Tech; and

WHEREAS, over time, Danny has strengthened curriculum, instruction, and employer relationships within his program. He has single-handedly recruited and received financial and in-kind support from organizations such as Andy Mohr and Cummins Sales and Service; and

WHEREAS, in addition to supporting students in his program, Danny provides career and technical educators with support as a locai teacher-leader, a statewide officer for IATTI, a presenter at the Indiana-
ACTE conference, and a national ACTE vice president representing trade and industry; and

WHEREAS, amidst the challenges of the COVID 19 pandemic, Danny even formed a relationship with an organization called Good 360 that led to him securing storage space and developing logistical/inventory management systems, so that Indiana schools with automotive service programs can now receive material donations from the excesses of automotive parts suppliers; and

WHEREAS, Danny is a teacher first, and he is also a teacher-leader in every sense. His positive demeanor and his concrete efforts toward enhancing career and technical education programs set him apart as a contributor within the state; and

WHEREAS, Danny has been recognized as the Indiana Association of Career and Technical Education 2022 Teacher of the Year. This award recognizes the finest career and technical education teachers who have demonstrated innovation in the classroom, commitment to their students, and dedication to improving
CTE in their institutions and communities. Danny is a worthy recipient because of his collaborative efforts to increase student opportunities in his Area 31 program, his co-leadership of the career and technical student organization known as SkillsUSA, his statewide leadership of the automotive service instructor association, and his service as Trade and Industrial Vice President on the national board of the Association for Career and Technical Education: now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Danny Camden and congratulates him on being named 2022 Career and Technical Education Teacher of the Year.

SECTION 2. The Council further thanks Mr. Camden for his dedication to educating students and serving his community and the City of Indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of February, 2023, at 7:19 p.m.

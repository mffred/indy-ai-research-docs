CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 33, 2022
Proposal No. 458, 2022

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Thomas Ridley on his 100" Birthday.

WHEREAS, Thomas Ridley was born on December 9, 1922, to Thomas Howard Ridley and Evelyn
Hazelwood in Indianapolis, IN; and

WHEREAS, the six-member family resided.at 735 Center Street, a location in one of the communities near Indiana Avenue, which he came to adore; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley enlisted in the military in 1943 and served across Europe until January 1946;
and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley re-enrolled at Crispus Attucks upon his return to Indianapolis in 1946 to finish

his high school studies, graduating with the Class of 1947; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley wed Mary Louise Ridley, who had aiso grown up close to Indiana Avenue, in 1948. After marriage, the Ridieys relocated to the near northside, where they finally made their home at 3317 Ralston Avenue; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley spent more than three decades working for the United States Postal Service, eventually moving up the ranks fo assume a managerial position; and

WHEREAS, the Ridleys had always dreamed of returning to Indiana Avenue, where they had both spent their formative years. They seized the chance in 1996 to return to Ransom Place along with Mrs.
Ridley's sister, Nancy Johnson, when the lots on the 800 and 900 biocks of Paca Street were made

available for purchase. On the land they all cherished, they bought a corner lot and constructed a brand- new duplex; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley became regarded as a great historian of the Indiana Avenue area when he authored and published his memoir, "From the Avenue’, at the age of 90; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Ridley will turn 100 years old on December 9, 2022, and jazz music will play down
Indiana Avenue in celebration of the occasion; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Thomas Ridley on his 100!
Birthday.

SECTION 2. The indianapolis City-County Council proudly offers its admiration, congratulations and very best wishes to Thomas Ridley upon this momentous occasion.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5" day of December, 2022, at 7:26 p.m.

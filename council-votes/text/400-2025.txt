CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the organizations and volunteers addressing  hunger during SNAP disruptions, and expressing the Council’s gratitude for their service. 
 
WHEREAS, thousands of Indianapolis residents have experienced disruptions to the Supplemental 
Nutrition Assistance Program (“SNAP”) benefits during the 2025 federal government shutdown,  significantly increasing demand for emergency food assistance across Marion County; and  
 
WHEREAS, in response, the City of Indianapolis joined with corporate and nonprofit partners to  commit hundreds of thousands of dollars in emergency funding  to Gleaners Food Bank of Indiana to  expand distribution to local food -pantries across the broader Central Indiana region, underscoring a  community-wide mobilization of resources.   
 
WHEREAS, also under the city’s coordination and leadership, dozens of distribution events  were  scheduled in November with no sign -up or ID required, making meals, produce, and other resources  accessible to all residents including those facing benefit interruptions.   
 
WHEREAS, local hunger -relief organizations and networks —including food banks, food pantries,  community centers, grassroots community groups, neighborhood associations, faith -based organizations  and mutual-aid networks—together with thousands of volunteers, have sorted, packed, transported, and  distributed food resources for neighbors in need; and 
 
WHEREAS, volunteers have repeatedly demonstrated extraordinary commitment by serving on short  notice, extending hours, and working in higher -than-normal volume situations to ensure no family goes  hungry during this period; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City -County Council has a long -standing precedent for recognizing anti -hunger  organizations and volunteers for their critical role in protecting the health and dignity of Indianapolis  families; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Council commends and thanks the organizations, staff, and volunteers who have  worked tirelessly to address hunger and food insecurity during the SNAP disruptions caused by the  federal government shutdown of 2025. 
 
SECTION 2. The Council recognizes partners such as Gleaners Food Bank of Indiana, Indy Hunger 
Network, Second Helpings, faith -based hunger -relief sites, neighborhood food -pantries, mutual -aid  networks, corporate donors, and all community volunteers for expanding service and collaborating across  sectors to reach residents quickly. 
 
SECTION 3. The Council encourages all residents, businesses, and institutions with the ability to do so  to continue donating time, funds, and food to local partners addressing hunger ; reinforcing the spirit of  community that defines Indianapolis while SNAP benefits are disrupted and as recovery continues.  
 
SECTION 4. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.  
 
SECTION 5. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3- 4-14.

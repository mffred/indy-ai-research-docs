CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 41, 2023
Proposal No. 18, 2023

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Brandon Herget as the Director of the
Department of Public Works for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-3 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and
County,” a mayoral appointment of Director of the Department of Public Works is subject to the approval of the
City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Brandon Herget fo serve as the Director of the Department of Public Works at his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2023; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Brandon Herget is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Director of the Department of Public Works for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of February, 2023, at 8:07 p.m.

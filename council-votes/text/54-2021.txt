CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 4, 2021
Proposal No. 54, 2021

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2020 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 235, 2020) by additional appropriations of ten million, ($10,000,000) for purposes of the City Non-
Departmental.

WHEREAS, the COVID-19 pandemic has required public-health measures that, though necessary, have caused significant hardship for Marion County’s small businesses, and

WHEREAS, the Indy Chamber has developed a Rapid Response Hub to provide aid to small businesses in Central Indiana facing such hardships; and

WHEREAS, that program is operated through the Neighborhood Self Employment Initiative, Inc. d/b/a
Business Ownership Initiative ("BOP), and

WHEREAS, a particular focus of that programming involves the rapid provision of small-business loans to Marion County small businesses eligible for participation in the Federal Small Business Administration’s
Paycheck Protection Program, and

WHEREAS, that program incentivizes small businesses to keep their workers on the payroll by making loans partially or fully forgivable if a recipient keeps its employees on the payroll; and

WHEREAS, keeping Marion County residents employed is vital to preserving the County's economic well- being, lessens the already significant burdens facing social-services agencies, and protects the tax base; and

WHEREAS, BO!’ relationship with Bankable allows it to offer Paycheck Protection Program loans as a
Community Development Financial Institution; and

WHEREAS, BOI could provide loans to Marion County small businesses that would, in turn, be purchased by the federal Small Business Administration under the Paycheck Protection Program if it had the upfront capital to fund those loans; and

WHEREAS, the City has the ability to provide that up-front capital to protect countless Marion County jobs in a manner that will see its investment be fully reimbursed as the Small Business Administration purchases the Paycheck Protection Program loans made by BOI; and

WHEREAS, ensuring that Marion County small businesses and their employees have access to this federal program in a way that, after reimbursement, will be entirely budget neutral is sound public policy; and

WHEREAS, that policy is particularly sound because BOI is able to get Paycheck Protection Loans to smaller businesses and businesses operated by individuals in historically disadvantaged demographic groups—businesses largely going unserved by the Paycheck Protection Program loans being processed by traditional banks; and

WHEREAS, IC § 10-14-3-17(j)(5)(H) provides that, in the event of a declared disaster emergency under
IC § 10-14-3-12, a political subdivision may “waive procedures and formalities otherwise required by law pertaining fo . . . the appropriation and expenditure of public funds”; and

WHEREAS, IC § 5-14-1.5-5 likewise provides that notice requirements for public meetings shall not apply when a meeting is called in response to an emergency that threatens disruption of a government service;
now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended by the character increase hereinafter stated for purposes of the City Non-Departmental.

SECTION 2. The City Non-Departmental, requests an additional appropriation in Character Three totaling ten Million ($10,000,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund:

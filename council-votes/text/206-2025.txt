CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 12, 2025
Proposal No. 206, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring State Senator La Keisha Jackson.

WHEREAS, The Honorable La Keisha Jackson faithfully served the citizens of Indianapolis for over a decade as a member of the Indianapolis-Marion County City-County Council, representing the Far Eastside (Districts 14 and 15), and worked tirelessly to uplift her community and improve the quality of life for residents; and

WHEREAS, during her Council tenure, Councilor Jackson provided impactful leadership as Chair of the Municipal Corporations Committee and as a member of key committees, helping shape policies on development, public safety, and finance; and

WHEREAS, Councilor Jackson led transformative efforts on the Far Eastside, including the redevelopment of Oaktree Apartments, cleanup of Towne & Terrace, major infrastructure investments, and the expansion and improvement of Grassy Creek Park, enhancing access to green space and recreation for local families; and

WHEREAS, a steadfast advocate for equity, she promoted economic inclusion, supported minority- owned businesses, expanded affordable housing, and spearheaded anti-violence and food access initiatives; and

WHEREAS, beyond the Council, Jackson has served over 20 years as CEO of Pathway Resource
Center, mentoring youth, connecting families to resources, and making a lasting impact on over 100,000 residents; and

WHEREAS, in 2024, Jackson was elected to the indiana State Senate representing District 34, where she continues to champion transportation, housing, food security, and economic justice for her constituents;
now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. That the Indianapolis City-County Council hereby honors and thanks La Keisha Jackson for her distinguished service, celebrates her lasting contributions to the city, and extends best wishes for her continued leadership in the Indiana State Senate.

SECTION 2. That a copy of this resolution be presented to Senator Jackson as a token of the Council's high regard and appreciation.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9" day of June, 2025, at 7:18 p.m.

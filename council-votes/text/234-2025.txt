CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 76, 2025
Proposal No. 234, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Laura Larimer to the Information Technology Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Information Technology Board, the Council appoints:

Laura Larimer
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending September 8, 2027. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8th day of September, 2025, at 7:09 p.m.

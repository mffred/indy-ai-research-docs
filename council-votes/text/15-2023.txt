CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 38, 2023
Proposal No. 15, 2023

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Martine Romy Bernard-Tucker as the
Director of the Office of Public Health and Safety for a term ending December 31, 2023.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 202-712 of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” a mayoral appointment naming the Director of the Office of Public Health and Safety is subject fo the approval of the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Martine Romy
Bernard-Tucker to serve as the Director of the Office of Public Health and Safety at his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2023; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Martine Romy Bernard-Tucker is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as the Director of the Office of Public Health and Safety for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of February, 2023, at 8:05 p.m.

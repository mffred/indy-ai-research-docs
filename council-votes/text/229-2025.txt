CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 15, 2025
Proposal No, 229, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION commemorating the Indiana Pacers’ extraordinary 2025 postseason performance.

WHEREAS, the 2024-2025 Indiana Pacers began the season with a record of 16 wins and 18 losses and carried modest expectations from fans and analysts across the National Basketball Association; and

WHEREAS, under the leadership of Head Coach Rick Carlisle, the Pacers emphasized defense and conditioning, sparking a turnaround that saw the team go 34-14 to close the regular season and 15~8 in the playoffs; and

WHEREAS, the Pacers set numerous franchise and league records during the season, including scoring 162 points in a single game, making 27 three-pointers in one contest, and seeing point guard Tyrese
Haliburton break the franchise record with 10 three-pointers in a single game against the Washington
Wizards; and

WHEREAS, the Pacers became the first team in NBA history to feature eight players each scoring 200 or more points in a single postseason, a testament to the team’s exceptional depth and cohesion; and

WHEREAS, indiana earned the fourth seed with 50 wins, then defeated the Milwaukee Bucks, toppled the top-seeded Cleveland Cavaliers in five games, and outlasted the New York Knicks in a dramatic seven- game Eastern Conference Finals; and

WHEREAS, the Pacers thrilled fans by repeatedly overcoming adversity—erasing 20-point deficits, rallying from multiple seven-point deficits in final minutes, and forcing overtime to win after trailing by 14 points with under three minutes left; and

WHEREAS, despite falling to the Oklahoma City Thunder in a hard-fought Game Seven of the NBA
Finals, the Pacers’ extraordinary run defied expectations, inspired a city, and reignited statewide pride in
Indiana basketball; and

WHEREAS, the Pacers’ extraordinary run was made possible not only by the players, but also by the dedicated efforts of the coaching staff, front office, medical and training teams, and all those behind the scenes whose commitment and leadership fueled the team’s success; and

WHEREAS, power forward Pascal Siakam captured the spirit of the season, saying, “We're not a team everyone wants to watch. For a while, indy was like, hey, nobody cares. And we made people care—that's something to be proud of. Just to see how the city was so alive, and we feel blessed that we were the people that gave them that.”; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes the 2024-2025 Indiana Pacers basketball team for their remarkable playoff run for the NBA Championship, and thanks them for bringing such pride and joy to the
Indianapolis community.

SECTION 2. The Council wishes a speedy recovery for Point Guard Haliburton and much continued success for a team many will now be watching in the 2025-2026 NBA Season.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

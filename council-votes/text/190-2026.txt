CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to direct the City-County to require changes to the Office of 
Public Health & Safety (OPHS) as a result of the audit completed by the Office of Audit & Performance.  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Indiana Code (IC) § 36- 3-4-23, the City -County Council of the Consolidated 
City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana (Consolidated City -County) may create or terminate  agencies of the Consolidated City -County and transfer to or f rom those agencies any powers, duties,  functions, or obligations; and  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 202-711 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County   (Revised Code) , the Office of Public Health and Safety (OPHS) was created under the Mayor’s office; and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 202-712 of the Revised Code, the director of the Office of Public Health  and Safety shall (1) be appointed by the Mayor subject to City -County Council confirmation, (2) serve at  the pleasure of the Mayor, and (3) execute contracts subject to the authority of the mayor and any other  limitations prescribed by law; and  
 
WHEREAS, all officials, appointees, and employees of the Consolidated City -County must follow the 
Ethics portion, §293-101 through § 293-216, of the Revised Code; and 
 
WHEREAS, more specifically, all officials, appointees, and employees of the Consolidated City-County  are subject to the conflicts of interests for contracts provisions spelled out in Section 293-211 of the Revised 
Code; and 
 
WHEREAS, Section 293- 302 of the Revised Code requires that the Office of Corporation Counsel  properly train all officials, appointees, and employees on the terms and conditions of the Ethics Code; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Office of Public Health and Safety was audited by the Mayor’s Office of Audit & 
Performance with a report published on April 9, 2026; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Audit Report found that there were significant issues with the OPHS’s contracts and  recommended ongoing staff training, a centralized system for managing and tracking contracts, steps to  address noncompliance for contracts, and ethics training to follow ethics requirements from state and  municipal law; and

WHEREAS, the City -County Council having considered the findings from the Audit Report, have  created the following requirements: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The City -County Council hereby calls upon the director of OPHS to create a centralized  system for tracking and managing contracts and invoices or otherwise utilize currently -existing processes  or systems of the City -County enterprise. Additionally, the Council hereby calls for ongoing training for all  staff of the office to ensure a general understanding of how to comply with the duties given to the OPHS. If  the director does not comply with these requirements, the City -County Council will call upon the Mayor’s  office to replace the director. 
 
SECTION 2.  The City-County Council hereby calls upon the Office of the Corporate Counsel to provide in- person ethics training for the staff of OPHS, within the next six months, followed by monitoring OPHS to  ensure all current and new employees comply with the current online training requirements.  
 
SECTION 3.  The City -County Council hereby calls upon the Mayor’s office and the Office of Corporation 
Counsel to modify the ordinance to increase the training of ethical requirements for all of city and county  departments and agencies.  
 
SECTION 4.  The City -County Council hereby calls upon the Office of Finance and Management to  establish a monitoring system for OPHS that will ensure compliance with both city and state accounting  policies and procedures. 
 
SECTION 5.  The City -County Council hereby calls upon the OPHS to take the any and all steps needed  to address the findings made in the Audit Report and to fully cooperate with the other departments and  agencies listed herein.  Until this is done, the City -County Council will freeze any additional funding that  goes to OPHS.

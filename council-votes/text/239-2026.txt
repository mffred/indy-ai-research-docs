CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 16, 2026
Proposal No. 239, 2026

A GENERAL RESOLUTION designating East 19" Street, from Dr. Andrew J. Brown Avenue to Hillside
Avenue, as the Pastor David Lee Memorial Way.

WHEREAS, the late Pastor David Lee served faithfully as a guiding spiritual leader and pillar of the Zion
Hill Missionary Baptist Church, Inc., dedicating his life to serving the community of Indianapolis; and

WHEREAS, Pastor Lee's leadership, compassion, and commitment to faith and service have left an enduring legacy on the congregation and the broader community; and

WHEREAS, it is fitting and appropriate to honor the memory of Pastor David Lee by designating a portion of East 19th Street to commemorate his impact on the city and its residents; and

WHEREAS, the stretch of East 19th Street, from Dr. Andrew J. Brown Avenue to Hillside Avenue, is a significant corridor in the community that reflects the values and vision shared by Pastor Lee; and

WHEREAS, it is the belief of Zion Hill Missionary Baptist Church, Inc. and its leadership that the installation of memorial signage will serve as a constant reminder to future generations of the vital work carried out by Pastor Lee; now, therefore:

BE iT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council does hereby honor the memory of Pastor David Lee, pastor of Zion Hill Missionary Baptist Church, by designating East 19'" Street, from Dr. Andrew J. Brown
Avenue to Hillside Avenue, as the Pastor David Lee Memorial Way.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14th day of September, 2026, at 7:43 p.m.

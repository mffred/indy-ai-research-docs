CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 6, 2022
Proposal No. 127, 2022

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for Marion County
Auditor (City-County Fiscal Ordinance No. 22, 2021) appropriating an additional fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) in the County General Fund for purposes of the Marion County Fair Board.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since the adoption of the City-
County Annual Budget for 2022 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance No. 22, 2021) that appropriated funds for
Public Purpose Local Grants (Marion County Fair Board), the City-County Annual Budget for Marion County
Auditor is hereby amended to reflect additional appropriations hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion
County Fair Board to make continued capital improvements to facilities on the fairgrounds.

SECTION 2. Appropriates $50,000 from the Marion County General Fund for the purposes of additional capital improvements to facilities on the fairgrounds.

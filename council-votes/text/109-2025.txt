CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 60, 2025 
Proposal No. 109, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Kenneth Beache to the Marion County Stormwater Management 
Technical Advisory Committee. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  As a member of the Marion County Stormwater Management Technical Advisory Committee,  the Council reappoints: 
 
Kenneth Beache 
 
SECTION 2.  The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2028.  The person  appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council and until his or her successor is  appointed and qualifies, unless the duration of the holdover period for this appointment is limited by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7th day of July, 2025, at 7:36 p.m.

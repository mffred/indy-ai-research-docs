CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 2, 2022
Proposal No. 403, 2021

A GENERAL RESOLUTION approving the declaratory resolution designating the establishment of the State
Ditch — Mars Hill Flood Control Improvement District.

WHEREAS, Indiana Code 36-7-15.6 (the “Act’) authorizes the establishment of flood control improvement districts in special flood hazard areas of the Consolidated City of Indianapolis and Marion County (the “City’) to capture incremental property tax revenue generated by special flood hazard properties to be deposited in a special fund for the planning, design, construction, operation and maintenance of flood control works; and

WHEREAS, on October 27, 2021, following public notice and a public hearing pursuant to Section 9 of the
Act, the Board of Public Works of the City (the “Board”) adopted Resolution 22, 2021, by which it recommended to the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County (the “Commission’) that it establish the State
Ditch - Mars Hill Flood Control Improvement District (the “District”) to finance the planning, design, construction, operation and maintenance of a flood damage reduction project fo be constructed by the
Department of Public Works of the City to provide fiood protection in the District (the “Project’); and

WHEREAS, on November 17, 2021, the Commission, finding that the construction, replacement, repair, maintenance, or improvement of flood control works being constructed as part of the Project is necessary for the general welfare, safety, and security of the District and its inhabitants, adopted Declaratory Resolution 2021-R-049 pursuant to Section 11 of the Act, by which it approved the recommendation of the Board and established the District; now therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Pursuant to Section 11 of the Act, the City-County Council of the Consolidated City of
Indianapolis and of Marion County, Indiana hereby approves the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County’s Declaratory Resolution 2021-R-049 establishing the State Ditch ~ Mars Hill Flood
Control Improvement District.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10% day of January, 2022, at 8:22 p.m.

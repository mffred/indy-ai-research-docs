CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 2, 2021
Proposal No. 52, 2021

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2021 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 235, 2020) by additional appropriations of three million seven hundred thousand dollars ($3,700,000) for purposes of the City Non-Deparimental and County Non-Departmental.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended by the character increase hereinafter stated for purposes of responding to the COVID-19 pandemic.

SECTION 2. The City of Indianapolis Non-Departmental requests an additional appropriation totaling two million three hundred twenty-five thousand dollars ($2,325,000) in the Federal Grants Fund and seven hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($775,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund in Characters
One, Two, Three, and Four to be used for overtime, PPE, cleaning services, various supplies, eligible sub recipient costs, and equipment.

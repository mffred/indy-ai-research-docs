CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
             
I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development 
Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on December 21, 2022, a public hearing as required  by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and  “Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment  prayed for in petition: 
 
2022-ZON-036   4161 AND 4355 SENOUR ROAD (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
Prop No. 21, 2023 FRANKLIN TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT 25 
R.O. No. 1, 2023 BEAZER HOMES OF INDIANA, LLP, by David A. Retherford 
Rezoning of 63.60 acres from the D-A district to the D-3 district. 
 
2022-ZON-117 6105 SOUTHEASTERN AVENUE (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 22, 2023 WARREN TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT 18 
R.O. No. 2, 2023 TIM HEDINGER, by David Gilman 
Rezoning of 2.07 acres from the C-3 and C-S districts to the C-S  district to provide the expansion of a self-storage use and to terminate  all commitments approved under rezoning petition 95-Z-6. 
 
2022-ZON-125  5212 AND 5246 BLUFF ROAD (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
Prop No. 23, 2023 PERRY TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #23 
R.O. No. 3, 2023 BURMESE AMERICAN COMMUNITY INSTITUTE, INC., by Andi M. 
Metzel  
Rezoning of 9.24 acres from the D-A district to the SU-38 district to  provide for a community center. 
 
2022-CZN-843  10505 INDIAN CREEK ROAD SOUTH (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 24, 2023 FRANKLIN TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT 25 
R.O. No. 4, 2023 SIX BROTHERS, INC., by Pat Rooney 
Rezoning of 23.665 acres from the D-A (FF) district to the D-S (FF)  district. 
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance  prayed for in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended  2022-ZON-036, 2022-ZON-117, 2022-ZON-125, and 2022-CZN-843 to the City-County Council  for adoption to the end that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety from fire,  flood, and other danger may be secured, that property values may be preserved and that the  public health, comfort, morals and convenience and general welfare may be preserved.

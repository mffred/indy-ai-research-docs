CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 9, 2025
Proposal No. 111, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Lawrence North Wildcats Girls Basketball Team for their 2025
Class 4A State Championship victory.

WHEREAS, on March 2, 2025, the Lawrence North Wildcats Girls Basketball Team captured the IHSAA
Ciass 4A State Championship with a 65-59 victory over Warsaw Community High School, marking an extraordinary accomplishment for the program; and

WHEREAS, the team, composed of Ashiyah Teague, Ava Cousin, Brooklyn Roberts, Jaida Wheat,
Jamaya Thomas, Kalea Bentley, Keadriah "Keke" Butler, Kya Hurt, Maia Johnson, Naja Winston, Wilma
Gardner, Zion Hunt, and Zo’e Peyton, was led by Head Coach Stephen Thomas and Assistant Coaches
Damone Eubank, Tim Thomas, Lauren Dickerson, Mikale Rogers, Consuelo Sanders, and Tyler
Etherington, with the support of team managers Kennedi Mills, lyanna Clancy, and Estefany Chacon
Gramajo; and

WHEREAS, the team was further supported by Lawrence North Athletic Director Mike Penrose,

Pri al Brett Crousore, and MSD of Lawrence Township Superintendent Dr. Shawn Smith; and

WHEREAS, the Wildcats showcased resilience, skill, and determination throughout the championship game, executing in critical moments to secure the title and reinforcing their reputation as the "Cardiac Cats”;
and

WHEREAS, Jamaya Thomas, Kya Hurt, Keadriah "Keke" Butler, and Naja Winston delivered key performances, demonstrating leadership, poise, and excellence under pressure; and

WHEREAS, this championship victory is a testament to the dedication, hard work, and perseverance of the Lawrence North student-athletes, who have represented their school and community with distinction both on and off the court; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council congratulates the Lawrence North Wildcats Girls Basketball Team on their 2025 Class 4A State Championship and commends their outstanding achievement.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council recognizes the contributions of Head Coach Stephen Thomas,
Assistant Coaches Damone Eubank, Tim Thomas, Lauren Dickerson, Mikale Rogers, Consuelo Sanders, and Tyler Etherington; team managers Kennedi Mills, lyanna Clancy, and Estefany Chacon Gramajo; and the entire Lawrence North community for their commitment to fostering a culture of excellence in athletics and academics.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7 day of April, 2025, at 7:28 p.m.

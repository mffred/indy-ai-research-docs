CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO.       , 2024 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to have the city controller revise the 2025 budget proposal  to add additional funds to DPW dedicated for road and bridge repair by reducing the budget from certain  other departments in an equal increment. 
 
WHEREAS, the city-county council acknowledges that the many of the roads and bridges within the  consolidated city are in dire need of repair or replacement; and 
 
WHEREAS, the city-county council recognizes that the roadway funding formula provides only a portion  of the funds needed to adequately address the annual roadway repair and maintenance needs; and  
 
WHEREAS, city-county council further recognizes that in order to maintain the roads and bridges in a  manner the citizens of the Consolidated City and all of its guests and visitors deserve, hard decisions need to  be made; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Department of Public Work’s 2025 budget shall hereby be increased in the amount of  $225,000,000.00 (Two hundred twenty -five million dollars.)  These funds shall be used exclusively for  road and bridge maintenance and repair work. 
 
SECTION 2.  The controller shall review the 2025 budgets of all city departments and county agencies, other  than those listed below as “Exempt Departments and Agencies,” and shall reduce those budgets in a total  amount equal to the amount added to DPW’s budget, $225,000,000.00 (Two hundred twenty -five million  dollars.) 
 
SECTION 3.  Exempt Departments and Agencies are defined as the Department of Public Works, the 
Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department, the Indianapolis Fire Department, the County Prosecutor, the 
Public Defender, the Metropolitan Emergency Services Agency, and the Office of Public Health and Safety.  
 
SECTION 4.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and in compliance with Indiana 
Code § 36-3-4-14.

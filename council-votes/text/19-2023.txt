CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 2, 2023
Proposal No. 19, 2023

ASPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Shyann Delmar and Mecka Curry for helping IMPD solve a kidnapping case and locating a missing 5-month-old twin baby during the holiday season.

WHEREAS, on December 19, 2022, 5-month-old twin babies went missing from Columbus, Ohio when a woman took their mother’s running car while they were in the back seat; and

WHEREAS, on December 20, the suspected kidnapper ended up in Indianapolis, and Ms, Delmar and
Ms. Curry interacted with her at a gas station on the northwest side and bought toys from her, the woman asked for a ride to the store, so they assisted her and exchanged phone numbers; and

WHEREAS, Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry realized that woman looked similar to the photos posted online of the suspected kidnapper from Columbus, so they took a video of her and created a gameplan to take action. The woman called Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry the next day asking if they would like to buy toys from her again, and they made plans to meet the woman at the store and call the police; and

WHEREAS, Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry called the police while in the car with the woman, and they distracted her by taking her to a few different stores while they got ahold of IMPD. The police conducted a traffic stop on I-65 south and stopped the woman in the car. Ms. Curry showed police a mugshot of the suspected kidnapper; and

WHEREAS, the kidnapper was taken into custody that day and Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry’s actions leading to the traffic stop were praised by detectives in Columbus; and

WHEREAS, Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry knew that one twin was still missing and cold temperatures and a snowstorm were starting, and they found the suspect’s bus map in the backseat, so they drove to a few different bus stops looking for the car; and

WHEREAS, Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry went to Papa John’s on Indiana Avenue and saw the missing
Honda car covered in snow and a baby in a car seat, and they saw police officers nearby in the plaza, so they rushed to tell them about the baby. At 6:40 p.m. that day, Ms. Curry and Ms. Belmar helped locate one of the missing twin babies, and was found safe and in the custody of IMPD; now, therefore

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council firmly believes that our communities are stronger and safer when we all work together.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council commends Ms. Delmar and Ms. Curry for their heroic actions of not only helping IMPD capture the suspect but helped find the missing baby.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9 day of January, 2023, at 7:39 p.m.

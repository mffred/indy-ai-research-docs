CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.      , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County 
Fiscal Ordinance 6, 2024) by an additional appropriation of four million eight hundred twenty thousand five  hundred dollars ($4,820,500) and a transfer of appropriation of sixty-eight thousand dollars ($68,000) for the  purposes of Marion County Information Services Agency. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers here in  after stated for purposes of the Marion County Information Service Agency. 
 
SECTION 2. The MC Information Services Agency requests an additional appropriation totaling four million  three hundred five thousand five hundred ($4,305,500) and  transfer of appropriation totaling sixty-eight  thousand dollars ($68,000) from characters one and two to character three  in the Information Services 
Fund, and an additional appropriation totaling five hundred fifteen thousand dollars ($515,000) in the 
Enhanced Access Fund in character three for the purpose of transitioning Marion County’s property tax  system and other software services.

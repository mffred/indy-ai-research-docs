CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.      , 2025 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 6, 2024) by an additional appropriation of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) and a transfer  of appropriation of one hundred ninety -eight thousand dollars ($198,000) for the purposes of the Marion 
County Prosecutor. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE

City-County Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increases, decreases, and  transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Prosecutor.

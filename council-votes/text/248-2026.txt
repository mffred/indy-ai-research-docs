GENERAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2026 
CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to direct the City -County Council to accept, for the record,  the 2026 Second Quarter Report submitted by the Marion County Sheriff (Sheriff), as required by HEA 1208  (2025), Public Law 54 (2025), and IC 36-8-10-21. 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21 establishes a jail commissary fund (“the fund”); and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(c) requires that the Sheriff deposits all money from the fund in a depository  as designated under IC 5-13-8; and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(d) allows the Sheriff to disburse, without appropriation of the City -County 
Council, money from the fund for specifically-listed items; and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(e) further requires that the Sheriff maintain a record of the fund’s receipts  and disbursements and provide a copy of those records, on a semiannual basis, to the City-County Council;  and 
 
 WHEREAS, this quarterly report also includes, for informational purposes only, correspondence from  the American Correctional Association confirming re-accreditation and the audit results from the inspection  of the Adult Detention Center by the Office of Detention Oversight; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1. The Marion County Sheriff hereby submits to the City -County Council, for the record, its July  9th commissary report, for the quarter ending June 30, 2026, attached hereto as Exhibit A . 
 
SECTION 2. It is acknowledged and recognized that IC 36-8-10-21 requires no further action by the full  council. 
 
Said report was received by the City-County Council this 14th day of September, 2026.

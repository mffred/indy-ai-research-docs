CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 49, 2022
Proposal No, 126, 2022

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Whitley Yates to the Equal Opportunity Advisory Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Equal Opportunity Advisory Board, the Council appoints:

Whitley Yates
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending June 30, 2025. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2"4 day of May, 2022, at 8:17 p.m.

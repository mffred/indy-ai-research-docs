CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 48, 2022
Proposal No. 123, 2022

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Terry Morris to the Information Technology Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Information Technology Board, the Council appoints:
Terry Morris

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2022. The

person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the

term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2" day of May, 2022, at 8:16 p.m.

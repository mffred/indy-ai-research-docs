CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 37, 2024
Proposal No. 51, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Jill Hoffman to the Marion County Stormwater Management
Technical Advisory Committee.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Marion County Stormwater Management Technical Advisory Committee, the Council reappoints:

Jill Hoffman
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of April, 2024, at 7:58 p.m.

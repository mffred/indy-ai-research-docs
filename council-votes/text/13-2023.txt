CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 42, 2023
Proposal No. 13, 2023

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Abbey Brands as the Director of the
Department of Business and Neighborhood Services for a term ending December 31, 2023, and u successor is appointed and confirmed.

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-3 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and
County,” a mayoral appointment of Director of the Department of Business and. Neighborhood Services is subject to the approval of the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Abbey Brandsn to serve as the Director of the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services at his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2023; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Abbey Brands is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Director of the
Department of Business and Neighborhood Services for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-34- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of February, 2023, at 8:07 p.m.

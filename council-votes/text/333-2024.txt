CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF DENIED ZONING PETITION           ) 
 
 I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Interim Administrator, Division of Current Planning, 
Department of Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the 
Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on September  18, 2024, following a public hearing as required by law pursuant to required statutory  legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and Commercial 
Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan 
Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment  prayed for in petition: 
 
 2024-ZON-056  4545 East Michigan Street  
 
PROP NO. 333, 2024 Center Township, Council District #13 
 *DENIAL UPHELD SJM Co. Inc., by Jamilah Mintze 
  25-0  10/07/2024 Rezoning of 0.12-acre from the D-5 district to the C-5 district to  provide for general commercial uses, including an automobile  repair shop. 
 
 I FURTHER CERTIFY that the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members recommended denial of 2024-
ZON-056 to the City-County Council. 
 
 I WITNESS my hand and official seal of the Metropolitan Development 
Commission of Marion County, Indiana, this 24
th day of September 2024.

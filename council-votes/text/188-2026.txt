Proposal No. 188, 2026

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 12, 2025) by an additional appropriation of eight hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($875,000) for the purposes of the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of the
Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department requests an additional appropriation totaling eight hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($875,000) in the City Cumulative Capital Improvement Fund in character four for the purpose of funding the final payment of IMPD’s 2021 vehicle financing agreement.

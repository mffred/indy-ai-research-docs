CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 31, 2023
Proposal No. 230, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve the Second Amendment to the 2015 Restated
Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Retirement Plan.

WHEREAS, the Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Retirement Plan (hereinafter referred to as “Retirement Plan”) was established by the Marion County Sheriffs Office, Indianapolis, Indiana, formerly known as the Marion County Sheriff's Department, effective January 1, 1963, and was restated in 2008, and again in 2015, and amended by a First Amendment in 2018; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Sheriffs Office reserved the right to amend the Retirement Plan as allowed by state law; and

WHEREAS, the Second Amendment to the Retirement Plan shall amend Section 7.02(d) of the
Retirement Plan to provide that the return of contributions from the Retirement Plan to a participant determined to have incurred a disability not in the line of duty shall be based on the participant's actuai years of credited service, rather than the greater of twenty (20) years of credited service or actual years of credited service as the Retirement Plan currently provides. This change shail align the time period, based on actual years of service, over which Retirement Plan participant contributions are returned to non-line of duty disabled’ participants with the time period used to calculate disability benefits under the Marion
County Sheriff's Department Personnel Benefit Pian for such participants; and

WHEREAS, Indiana Code 36-8-10-12 mandates that a department may not establish or modify a retirement plan without approval of the county fiscal body; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Pursuant to the specific authority grant by Indiana Code 36-8-10-12, the “Second
Amendment to 2015 Restated Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Retirement Plan” as attached hereto and incorporated herein, shall be and hereby is approved.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance with Indiana Code 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 11! day of September, 2023, at 7:50 p.m.

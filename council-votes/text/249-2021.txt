CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 7, 2021
Proposal No. 249, 2021

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to authorize the City to submit an application for indiana
Emergency Rental Assistance Funds to the Indiana Housing and Community Development Authority.

WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City-County Council, as the legislative body for the City of
Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana (“City”), held on August 9, 2021, with sufficient notice of the time and place of the meeting having been given and a quorum of the Council being present as required by the
Revised Code of the City, a majority of those members present at the meeting considered, discussed, consented to, and adopted the following resolution; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Mayor, as the City’s Chief Elected Official, is authorized to apply for and receive an award in an amount not to exceed One Hundred Million and 00/100 Dollars ($100,000,000.00) in indiana
Emergency Rental Assistance 1.0 (“IERA 1.0”) funding to provide assistance to eligible households in
Marion County that are at risk of experiencing homelessness or housing instability and is authorized to- administer any resulting grant of IERA 1.0 funding according to the terms and conditions set forth in the
Indiana Housing and Community Development Authority’s and the U.S. Department of Treasury’s published policies, procedures and regulations, as amended from time to time, and as set forth in the City’s application.

SECTION 2. Deputy Mayor of Community Development Jeff Bennett, acting on behalf of the Mayor, is hereby authorized, empowered, and directed to execute, acknowledge, and deliver in the City's name and on its behalf any and all applications, grant agreements, documents, instruments or writings, as are necessary and/or as appropriate to consummate the application and receipt of IERA 1.0 funding.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9 day of August, 2021, at 9:13 p.m.

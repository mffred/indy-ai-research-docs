COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2025 
Proposal No. 4, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s appointment of Wesley Jones as the Director of the 
Office of Audit and Performance for a term ending December 31, 2025. 
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 202-302 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and 
County,” a mayoral appointment of Director of the Office of Audit and Performance is subject to the  approval of the City-County Council; and  
 
 WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of 
Wesley Jones to serve as the Director of the Office of Audit and Performance at his pleasure for a term  ending December 31, 2025; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. Wesley Jones is approved and confirmed by the City- County Council to serve as the 
Director of the Office of Audit and Performance for a term ending December 31, 2025, and until a  successor is appointed and confirmed.  
 
SECTON 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC §  36-3-4-14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3rd day of February, 2025, at 7:57 p.m.

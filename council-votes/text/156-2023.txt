CITY-COUNTY GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 34, 2023
Proposal No. 156, 2023

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE to add additional gun safety measures to Revised Code
Chapter 451, Weapons, conditioned on the repeal or abrogation of state prohibitions or other legal restrictions on local regulation.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Title Ill, Chapter 451 of the "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” regarding public health and welfare, hereby is amended add the following section:

Sec. 451-7. — Gun Safety Measures (Conditional).

(a) The Council recognizes that the provisions of subsections (b) through (e) below are currently unenforceable under the Indiana statute restricting most forms of gun regulation by local governments, Ind.
Code Ch. 35-47-11.1, and by other relevant provisions of state law. Therefore, subsections (b} through (e} below shall not take effect, and shall not be enforced, unless and until the Corporation Counsel certifies in a written notice to the President of the Council that all or part of subsections (b) through (e) have become enforceable and are not preempted by or in conflict with the United States Constitution, the Indiana Constitution, federal statute, and/or Indiana statute. The written notice from the Corporation Counsel shall specify the measure(s) herein that have become enforceable, and such measure(s) shail take effect only after ninety (90) days have elapsed since the Corporation Counsel's written notice and the Council has taken no action to revise or rescind such measure(s).

(b) Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter or the Revised Code, it shall be unlawful in Marion
County to buy, sell, possess, display, carry, brandish, or discharge a semiautomatic assault weapon, as that term is defined in the proposed federal Assault Weapons Ban of 2023 (S.25), Section 2(a), as introduced in the United States Senate on January 23, 2023.

(c) It shall be unlawful in Marion County for a person under twenty-one (21) years of age to purchase a firearm. it shall be unlawful in Marion County to sell a firearm to a person under twenty-one (21) years of age.
As used in this section, the term firearm has the definition set forth in Ind. Code § 35-47-1-5.

(d) No person shall carry a handgun in Marion County without having applied for and received a handgun license by following the license application procedure set forth in Ind. Code § 35-47-2-3. It shail be unlawful for a person to carry a handgun in Marion County with a license that has expired or has been suspended or revoked pursuant to Ind. Code Chapter 35-47-2. As used in this section, the term handgun has the definition set forth in Ind. Code § 35-47-1-6.

(e) Notwithstanding subsection (d) above, no person shall carry a concealed firearm in Marion County. As used in this section, the term firearm has the definition set forth in Ind. Code § 35-47-1-5.”

SECTION 2. The full definition of semiautomatic assault weapon, as referred to in section 1(b) above, is reproduced in Appendix A to this ordinance.

SECTION 3. Should any provision (section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or any other portion) of this ordinance be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid for any reason after having taken effect, the remaining provision or provisions shall not be affected, if and only if such remaining provisions can, without the invalid provision or provisions, be given the effect intended by the Council in adopting this ordinance. To this end the provisions of this ordinance are severable.

SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance with ind. Code § 36-3-4-14, but only after the preconditions stated in Section 1(a) above have been satisfied.

Appendix A — Definition of Semiautomatic Assault Weapon

The term “semiautomatic assault weapon” means any of the following, regardiess of country of manufacture or caliber of ammunition accepted:

(A) A semiautomatic rifle that—
(i) has the capacity to accept a detachable ammunition feeding device; and (ii) has any 1 of the following:
(I) A pistol grip.
(U) A forward grip.

(IH) A folding, telescoping, or detachable stock, or a stock that is otherwise foldable or adjustable in a manner that operates to reduce the length, size, or any other dimension, or otherwise enhances the concealability, of the weapon.

(IV) A grenade launcher.
(V¥) A barrel shroud.
(VI) A threaded barrel.

(B) A semiautomatic rifle that has a fixed ammunition feeding device with the capacity to accept more than 10 rounds, except for an attached tubular device designed to accept, and capable of operating only with, .22 caliber rimfire ammunition.

(C) Any part, combination of parts, component, device, attachment, or accessory that is designed or functions to accelerate the rate of fire of a semiautomatic firearm but not convert the semiautomatic firearm into a machinegun.

(D) A semiautomatic pistol that—
{i} has an ammunition feeding device that is not a fixed ammunition feeding device, and (ii) has any 7 of the following:
(l) A threaded barrel.
(ID A second pistol grip.
(Ill) A barrel shroud,

(IV) The capacity to accept a detachable ammunition feeding device at some location outside of the pistol grip.

(V) A semiautomatic version of an automatic firearm.

(VJ A manufactured weight of 50 ounces or more when unloaded.

(Vi) A buffer tube, stabilizing brace or similar component that protrudes horizontally behind the pistol grip, and is designed or redesigned fo allow or facilitate a firearm to be fired from the shoulder.

(E) Asemiautomatic pistol with a fixed ammunition feeding device that has the capacity to accept more than 10 rounds.

(F) A semiautomatic shotgun that—

(i) has the capacity to accept a detachable ammunition feeding device or a fixed ammunition feeding device that has the capacity to accept more than 5 rounds; and

(ii) has any 1 of the following:
(1) A folding, telescoping, or detachabie stock.

(I) A forward grip.

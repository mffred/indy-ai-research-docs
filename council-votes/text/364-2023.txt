CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 36, 2023
Proposal No. 364, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the career and contributions of Marion County Surveyor, Debra S.
Jenkins.

WHEREAS, Debra S. Jenkins has served in local government for more than 40 years including time spent working in the Marion County Superior Courts as a Court Reporter and time spent working in the
Marion County Recorder's office as the Chief Deputy Recorder and; and

WHEREAS, since her election in 2008, Ms Jenkins has served the Marion County residents as the
County Surveyor, an indiana Constitutional Office first filled in 1819; and

WHEREAS, since being elected, she has implemented various strategies and policies to increase the effectiveness of the office, such as digitizing the 2400 section corners in the county, improving and expanding the digitization of other documents including, but not limited to, subdivision maps, subdivision plats, railway valuation maps, and legal surveys; and

WHEREAS, this digitization of office records has resulted in a substantial ease and efficiencies in storing and sharing these records via email or disc; and

WHEREAS, her office works closely with the Marion County Sheriffs office on determining the actual distance between schools and the work or home locations of sex offenders, works with the State Aicohol and Tobacco Commission to verify liquor license locations, and provides an annual average of 50 no-cost surveying projects to other government departments and agencies; and

WHEREAS, Ms Jenkins guidance has enabled the office to be a more cost-effective size, saving county tax dollars; and

WHEREAS, when encountering the problem of attracting and retaining employees only to have employees leave within months of being trained to go fo the private sector or other higher paying jobs, Ms
Jenkins, working with City HR, the Office of Corporation Counsel, and the Controller's Office, implemented the Higher Education and Longevity or HELP Program where employees can earn surveyor certifications by taking hybrid classes from Vincennes University over the course of 6 -8 years at no cost to the employees if they stay an additional 2 years; and

WHEREAS, the Association of Indiana Counties awarded Marion County the 2023 County
Achievement Award for the Surveyors HELP program; and

WHEREAS, in addition to the HELP program, Ms Jenkins has made it a point to introduce the career of land surveying to high school students, by hiring them in the summer, and provide early career employment opportunities to college students studying land surveying, now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes of Marion County Surveyor, Debra
Jenkins for her years of dedicated public service.

SECTION 2. The Council extends its congratulations to Ms Jenkins for providing enhanced and exemplary services fo the citizens of the Consolidated City of indianapolis - Marion County and other local and state entities.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

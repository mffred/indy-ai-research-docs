Proposal No. 168, 2025

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City County Annual Budget for 2025 (City County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 6, 2024) by additional appropriations totaling one million nine hundred fourteen thousand twenty-five dollars ($1,914,025) for the purposes of the Department of Metropolitan Development.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County- Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the following departments and agencies.

SECTION 2. The City Non-Departmental requests additional appropriations of five hundred ten thousand ($510,000) in the Revenue Bonds Fund in character three for the purpose of affording additional debt service capacity for the HUD Section 108 Loan Program:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL

Revenue Bonds Fund.

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Dan Haake to the Board of Public Works.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Public Works, the Council appoints:

Dan Haake
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2021. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 4, 2025
Proposal No. 166, 2025

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 6, 2024) by additional appropriations totaling five hundred fifty thousand dollars ($550,000) for the purposes of the Marion County Information Services Agency.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County- Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the following departments and agencies.

SECTION 2. The Marion County Information Services Agency requests an additional appropriation of five hundred fifty thousand dollars ($550,000) in the Information Services Fund in character three:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 GHAR 4 CHAR5 TOTAL
Information Services 550,000 550,000

SECTION 3. Upon approval of this and other pending proposals, the projected 2024 year-end and projected 2025 year-end fund balances are as follows:

Fund Projected 2024 Projected 2025
Year-End Balance Year-End Balance
Information Services 5,187,624 5,543,945

SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9" day of June, 2025, at 8:56 p.m.

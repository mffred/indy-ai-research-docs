COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2023 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 51, 2023 
Proposal No. 66, 2023 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Dr. Eugene White to the Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library 
Board of Trustees. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  As a member of the Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library Board of Trustees, the Council  appoints: 
 
Dr. Eugene White 
 
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025.  The person  appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term, the  appointee may serve until his or her successor  is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the  holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6th day of March, 2023, at 7:55 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 46, 2025
Proposal No. 74, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Adrianne Slash to the Indianapolis Public Transportation
Corporation Board of Directors.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis Public Transportation Corporation Board of Directors, the
Council appoints:

Adrianne Slash

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending March 3, 2029. The person appointed by this resolution shail serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7th day of April, 2025, at 7:40 p.m.

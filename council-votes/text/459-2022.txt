LOADING ZONE ORDINANCE NO. 1, 2022 
 
A PROPOSAL ratifying a loading zone established by the board of public works. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. Pursuant to Revised Code Sec. 621- 423, the following loading zone has  been established  by the board of public works: 
 
Vehicle Loading Zone 
7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. 
Monday through Friday 
 
Illinois Street, on the east side, 
From a point 140 feet south of Washington Street, 
To a point 254 feet south of Washington Street. 
 
SECTION 2. The council hereby ratifies the action of the board of public works. 
 
SECTION 3. This loading zone is deemed established upon adjournment of the general full meeting of the 
City-County Council on Monday, December 5, 2022. 
 
The foregoing loading zone was ratified and thereby established by the City -County Council this 5th day  of December, 2022, at 7:47 p.m.

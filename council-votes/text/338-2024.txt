CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.    , 2024 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2024 (City-County 
Fiscal Ordinance No. 14, 2023) by an additional appropriation of one hundred fifty thousand dollars  ($150,000) and a transfer of appropriation of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) for the purposes  of the Marion County Information Services Agency.  
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers  hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Information Service Agency. 
 
SECTION 2. The Marion County Information Services Agency  requests a transfer of two hundred fifty  thousand dollars ($250,000) from character one to character three and an additional appropriation in the 
Enhanced Access Fund of one hundred fifty thousand dollars ($150,000) in character three to address  additional costs related to IT services and equipment: 
 
FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL 
Information Services -250,000  250,000   - 
Enhanced Access    150,000   150,000

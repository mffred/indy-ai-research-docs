CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO.     , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION  to approve and authorize the execution of an amendment  to an existing franchise agreement with a franchise holder for the operation of a district cooling system  in  the City of Indianapolis, Indiana. 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) enacted Special Ordinance No. 10, 1990,  which granted a district cooling franchise to Mid -America Energy Resources, Inc. (“MAER”) , within a  specified geographic area within Marion County; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City thereafter entered into a franchise agreement (the “Franchise”) with MAER for  operation of a district cooling system in downtown Indianapolis; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City subsequently enacted Special Ordinance No. 5, 2000, which consented to the  assignment, sale, and transfer of the district cooling system (the “System”) and corresponding Franchise  to the Board of Directors for Utilities of the Department of Public Utilities of the City of Indianapolis , d/b/a 
Citizens Thermal (“Citizens”), among other provisions; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City and Citizens executed the first amendment to the aforementioned Franchise,  pursuant to Special Ordinance No. 5, 2000, on or around November 16, 2000, and the term of such  amended Franchise is set to run through December 31, 2025; and 
 
WHEREAS, on December 12, 2024, Citizens provided its notice to the City of its application to renew  its Franchise with the City pursuant to Section 7 of the Franchise, as amended; and  
 
WHEREAS, the City and Citizens thereafter engaged in negotiations regarding the terms of the  requested renewal, resulting in agreement to modify the term of the Franchise, as well as pro visions of  the Franchise related to the franchise fee and geographic scope of the Franchise; and  
 
WHEREAS, the City believes that the Franchise with Citizens should be renewed, extended, and  amended in the form attached hereto as “Exhibit A”; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City-County Council has the sole authority to authorize the renewal of such a 
Franchise under Sec. 866-4 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County; and

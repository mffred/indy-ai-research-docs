CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 32, 2023
Proposal No. 265, 2023

A GENERAL RESOLUTION designating a portion of Indiana Avenue, from Senate Avenue to Blackford
Street, as Madame CJ Walker Memorial Way.

WHEREAS, Madam C.J. Walker was born Sarah Breedlove on December 23, 1867, the first free child of former slaves on a plantation in the Louisiana Delta; and

WHEREAS, with little more than three months education, she launched a line of haircare products that was so successful that she ultimately became the first female self-made naire in the United States;
and

WHEREAS, in 1910 she relocated her business to Indianapolis where she opened a factory, a salon, and a beauty school employing thousands of agents; and

WHEREAS, the property purchased by Walker later became the site of the iconic Walker Theater building; a structure that is registered as an historic site with the National Historic Landmarks; and

WHEREAS, Madam Walker was also known as a generous philanthropist and a social activist, supporting anti-lynching campaigns, education and women’s rights; and

WHERAS, the Madam C.J. Walker Legacy Center is planning a bicentennial Mural of Madam Walker downtown on Indiana Avenue and Senate Street that will educate the community about the Indianapolis legend with a “down the street” connection to the Walker Building; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council does hereby honor the memory of Madame CJ Walker by designating that portion of Indiana Avenue from Senate Avenue to Blackford Street, as the” Madame CJ
Walker Memorial Way.”

SECTION 2. As per Revised Code Section 431-405(a) this authorization shall expire fifteen (15) years after it is passed by the City-County Council.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2"4 day of October, 2023, at 7:42 p.m.

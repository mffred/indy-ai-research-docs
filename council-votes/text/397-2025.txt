CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.     , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION  recognizing and honoring Steven Quick for his  extraordinary career, his decades of service to the City of Indianapolis, and his lasting impact on the  community. 
 
WHEREAS, Steven Quick was born on February 19, 1959, in Indianapolis, Indiana, and is a proud  graduate of Emmerich Manuel High School, Class of 1978; and 
 
WHEREAS, in 1988, Steven married his beloved wife, Leslie, and together they have built a strong  and loving family that includes three daughters, two sons, twenty grandchildren, and three great - grandchildren; and 
 
WHEREAS, Steven began his career with the Department of Transportation in January 1984 as a  laborer, quickly advancing through the ranks due to his strong work ethic and dedication to public service,  ultimately becoming a Heavy Equipment Operator in 1987; and 
 
WHEREAS, Steven’s leadership and commitment to his fellow workers led to his election as Vice 
President of AFSCME Local 725 in 1993, and just one year later, to his election as President —a position  he has held with distinction for over 30 years, most recently reaffirmed by his re -election in July 2025; and 
 
WHEREAS, Steven has consistently demonstrated a deep commitment to community service,  contributing his time and talents to numerous boards, school programs, and nonprofit organizations  throughout Indianapolis; and 
 
WHEREAS, throughout his 41 -year career with the Department of Public Works, Steven has been a  vital partner in advancing the City’s mission, playing a key role in the collaborative model established in  the early 1990s under Mayor Stephen Goldsmith and the leadership of Charles Snyder; and continuing  through the Peterson, Ballard and Hogsett administrations; and 
 
WHEREAS, Steven has been a very trusted partner of the City -County Council and has worked  collaboratively with its many members through these years to support priorities of the city’s constituents;  and  
 
WHEREAS, Steven Quick’s legacy is one of leadership, service, and unwavering dedication to the  betterment of his community and the lives of those around him; now, therefore:

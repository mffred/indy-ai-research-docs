CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 30, 2023
Proposal No. 224, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring BLACK: A Festival of Joy.

WHEREAS, Black people have traditionally been described by their circumstances, by their deficit and by a funding need. It's time that we describe Black people by who they are, and speci , their joy. Black
Joy is triumphant. It is bright and It is a critical component of the sustainability and overall culture of the city of Indianapolis; and

WHEREAS, BLACK: A Festival of Joy was first launched in the summer of 2022. In an effort to be a movement more than a moment, BLACK features not only a one-day event highlighting the essence of
Black Joy in all its magnificence through art, food, music and fellowship, but also a fund dedicated to
‘supporting Black creatives, therefore extending that joy and magnificence throughout the year, and

WHEREAS, BLACK was i ly conceived through the efforts of Pamela Ross - affectionately known as Boss Ross by many in the community. Ross, with the support of GANGGANG, convened a roster of
Black activists, event curators and artists to launch this one-of-a-kind offering; and

WHEREAS, BLACK: A Festival of Joy is a free celebration organized by a collective of Black organizations and individuals in Indianapolis seeking to support, center, and uplift Black joy in indianapolis.
This event will spark a movement to describe Black people by our contribution-and sustaining factor: our joy; and

WHEREAS, Saturday, July 8, 2023 will be known as Black Joy Day in Indianapolis; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes the vision and efforts of Pamela Ross, with the support of GANGGANG to highlight the essence of Black Joy in all its majesty through art, food, music and fellowship.

SECTION 2. The Coun

extends its appreciation and wishes BLACK: A Festival of Joy continued success.
SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10" day of July, 2023, at 7:44 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 17, 2024
Proposal No. 37, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Colin Flora to the Marion County Public Defender Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Marion County Public Defender Board, the Council appoints:

Colin Flora
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2026. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council, Upon expiration of the term,

the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5" day of February, 2024, at 8:04 p.m.

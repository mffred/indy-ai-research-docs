CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 31, 2023
Proposal No, 225, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Chris Tolliver and the Annual YMCA Holiday Toy Drive Program.

WHEREAS, with his YMCA experience in the Indianapolis area over the past 24 years, Chris Tolliver has aided hundreds of families. He began working at the Fall Creek YMCA of Greater Indianapolis as the
Site Director for the before- and after-school program, was later promoted to Program Coordinator, and is now the Community Engagement Specialist; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Tolliver established the Annual YMCA Holiday Toy Drive Program to assist underprivileged families in providing Christmas for children and to better the communities served; and

WHEREAS, The YMCA started its annual toy drive in 2000, and the first year it helped 900 people. has benefited more than 58,000 kids in Greater Indianapolis over the past 22 years; and

WHEREAS, the YMCA of Greater Indianapolis collaborates with more than 25 organization to collect toys and resources to serve families during the Holiday season. And more than 3,00 youth from Indianapolis area will participate in the program in 2023; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. City-County Councilior La Keisha Jackson recognizes Chris Tolliver’s dedication and commitment to support youth and families of the city of Indianapolis.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily recognizes Chris Tolliver and the YMCA for their shared vision and commitment to build a healthy spirit, mind, and body for all.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10% day of July, 2023, at 7:51 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 13, 2026
Proposal No. 135, 2026

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No.12, 2025) by an additional appropriation of four n eight hundred thirty thousand ($4,830,000) for the purpose of Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of
Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department.

SECTION 2. Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department requests an additional appropriation totaling two million three hundred seventy-seven thousand dollars ($2,377,000) in character one, two, three, and four in the State of Indiana Grants Fund, an additional appropriation totaling seventy-five thousand dollars ($75,000) in character four in the Federal Grants Fund, an additional appropriation totaling two million three hundred thousand seventy-eight thousand dollars ($2,378,000) in character one, two, three, and four in the
General Fund for the purposes of funding costs associated with the grant cleanup, Internet Crimes Agains'
Children program, and Indiana Department of Transportation work zone program.

SECTION 4. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does not intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the appropriation for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or project, or both, and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city-county council immediately upon receipt of any information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

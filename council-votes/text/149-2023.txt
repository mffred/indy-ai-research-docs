A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 18, 2022) by appropriating a total of two hundred twenty-five thousand dollars ($225,000) for the purpose of hiring of attorneys to be detailed as Special Assistant United States Attorneys to focus on investigating and prosecuting of gun crimes in Marion County.

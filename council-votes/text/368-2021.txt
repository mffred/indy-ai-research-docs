CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 35, 2021
Proposal No. 368, 2021

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Assistant Council Clerk Angela Gonzalez upon receipt of the
Lifetime Achievement Award from the Midwest Clown Association and 25 years of service to the
Indianapolis City-County Council.

WHEREAS, Angela Gonzalez joined the staff of the Indianapolis City-County Council office in August of 1996, and has now served this body for just over 25 years; and

WHEREAS, in that timeframe, Angela has served as the Council’s Assistant Clerk and webmaster while also acting as primary caregiver for an ill family member, but has nevertheless made time to serve the
Indianapolis community as an Auguste clown; and

WHEREAS, Angela “Q.T. Pie’ Gonzalez has been clowning in the Indianapolis area since 1989, devoting most of her energy to “ministry clowning”; and

WHEREAS, Angela “Q.T Pie” Gonzalez joined the Midwest Clown Association in 2005, and has since served in every position on the Board of Directors except Treasurer, has acted as the organization’s
Webmaster, is the long-time Editor of MCA’s 3-Ring News and Funny Papers newsletter, Chaired the
Annual Conference committee, and competed, lectured, or judged at every annual Midwest Clown
Association Conference since 2005 in addition to leading Sunday morning worship at many of their annual conferences; and

WHEREAS, her colleagues in the Midwest Clown Association describe her as “wonderfully respected by her fellow clowns and very passionate about the craft of clowning” and as "one of the most hard-working members’ the organization has; and report that “she is always willing to take on whatever challenge or duty she is presented with”; and

WHEREAS, according to one MCA member, “Angie had many obstacles putting on the convention for 2021, but she pulled it off without anyone knowing the problems because she smiled non-stop and remained calm’; and

WHEREAS, on October 15, 2021, Angie “Q.T. Pie” Gonzalez received the 2020 Lifetime Achievement
Award from the Midwest Clown Association in “recognition of her exemplary lifetime efforts dedicated to improving and encouraging the Clowning Profession”; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianapolis City-County Council recognizes Angela Gonzalez for 25 years of faithful service to this body and 32 years of service to the Indianapolis community through her clowning.

SECTION 2. The Council extends its warm congratulations to her upon her much-deserved Lifetime
Achievement Award from the Midwest Clown Association.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8 day of November, 2021, at 7:46 p.m.

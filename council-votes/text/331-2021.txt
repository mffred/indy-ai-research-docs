CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.       , 2020 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing past and present women Councillors of the Indianapolis -Marion 
County City-County Council. 
 
WHEREAS, on January 16, 1920, the Indiana General Assembly ratified the 19th Amendment of the 
U.S. Constitution, granting women the right to v ote, which was later ratified by Congress on August 18,  1920. However, it was not until a special state election in September 1921 that Indiana women were  actually granted the right to vote at the state level; and 
 
WHEREAS, after the establishment of Uni Gov, which combined city government with county  government in 1970, the first women elected to the Indianapolis -Marion County City-County Council were 
Beverly Miller (R) and Artricia Noel Chandler (D) in 1972; and 
 
WHEREAS, to-date, 43 women, which include 22 Democrats and 21 Republicans, have been elected  to serve on the Council since its establishment; and 
 
WHEREAS, these honorable women include: Beverly Miller, Artricia Noel Chandler, Lula M. Journey, 
Beulah A. Coughenour, Joyce Brinkman, Betty Stewart Ruh mkorff, Patricia Nickell, Amy S. Bradley, 
Susan Williams, Beverly Mukes-Gaither, Mary Moriarty Adams, Diana V. Strader, Z. Mae Jimison, Maggie 
Brents, Linda Beadling, Tommie Jones, Marilyn Moores, Joanne Sanders, Jackie Nytes, Lynn McWhirter, 
Karen Celestino Horseman, Angela Mansfield, Becky Langsford, Sherron Franklin, N. Susie Day, Virginia 
‘Ginny’ Cain, Cherrish  Pryor, Doris Minton -McNeill, Barbara Malone, Pamela Hickman, Marilyn Pfisterer, 
Christine Scales, Janice McHenry, Maggie A. Lewis, La Keisha Jac kson, Colleen Fanning, Susie Cordi, 
Danielle Coulter, Alison 'Ali' Brown, Crista Carlino, Kristin Jones, and Jessica McCormick; now, therefore : 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1 .  The Indianapolis City -County Council proudly recognizes past and present women 
Councillors of the Indianapolis-Marion County City-County Council.  
 
SECTION 2. The Council wholeheartedly thanks all former and current women Councillors for their  dedicated service to the City and County.

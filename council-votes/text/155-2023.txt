CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 20, 2023
Proposal No. 155, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve the issuance of general obligation bonds by the
Health and Hospital Corporation of Marion County, Indiana (the "Health and Hospital Corporation’) to finance ali or any portion of the 2023-2024 Capital Improvement Projects (as defined below) and the appropriation of the proceeds thereof.

WHEREAS, for purposes of this Resolution, the “2023-2024 Capital Improvement Projects” is defined as all or any portion of the design, engineering, construction, renovation, repair, improvement and equipping of various capital projects at facilities owned or operated by the Health and Hospital Corporation, each of which is reasonably considered to be an independently desirable end in itself without reference to another capital project, including, but not limited to, all or any portion of the following: (1) complete renovation of the new security building located at 3950 Meadow Dr., Indianapolis, Indiana, including mechanical, electrical and plumbing system upgrades and improvements, various interior and exterior improvements, landscaping and overall site improvements, and the addition of a new ambulance bay for use by Indianapolis Emergency
Medical Services, (2) repurposing of the Eskenazi Health Center North Arlington facility, located at 2505
N. Arlington Avenue, Indianapolis, Indiana, and/or the Eskenazi Health Center Forest Manor facility, located at 3840 N. Sherman Dr., Indianapolis, Indiana, after such facilities are vacated by Eskenazi Health, (3) construction and equipping of renovations at the Mosquito Control/Fleet Operation building, including fencing improvements, access control and security camera upgrades, resurfacing and improvements to the parking lot and/or addition of a vehicle storage building, (4) purchase and installation of security camera systems, badge access controls, and/or Metasys system upgrades at various buildings, (5) purchase and equipping of new or existing vehicles and miscellaneous equipment, including service vans and trucks, scissor lifts, powered pallet jacks, forklift, skid steer, lawn mowers, and/or utility terrain vehicles, (6) Pogue’s
Run clean-up at the Eskenazi Hospital clinic located at 6002 E. 38th Street, Indianapolis, Indiana, (7) all projects related to any of the projects or facilities described in clauses (1) through and including (6); and

WHEREAS, before the Health and Hospital Corporation may issue its general obligation bonds, Indiana
Code 36-3-6-9 requires that the City-County Council approve the issuance of such bonds; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council being duly advised, finds that it is in the best interests of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”), the County and their citizens for the Health and Hospital Corporation to issue its general obligation bonds to finance all or any portion of the 2023-2024 Capital Improvement
Projects, in order to better serve the residents of the City and the County; now therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. In accordance with Indiana Code 36-3-6-9, the City-County Council hereby approves the issuance by the Health and Hospital Corporation of its general obligation bonds in one or more series (the “Bonds’), for the purpose of providing funds to finance all or any portion of the costs of the 2023-2024
Capital improvement Projects, all upon the following conditions: (a) the original aggregate principal amount of the Bonds shall not exceed $6,100,000; (b) the maximum interest rate or rates on the Bonds shall produce a yield not exceeding six and one-half percent (6.50%) per annum; and (c) each series of the
Bonds will have a maximum term of five (5) years.

SECTION 2. In accordance with Indiana Code 6-1.1-18-5, the City-County Council hereby approves the additional appropriation made by the Health and Hospital Corporation not to exceed $6,100,000 which appropriation is to be provided out of the proceeds of the Bonds received by the Health and Hospital
Corporation in connection with the sale of the Bonds, together with all investment earnings thereon, for the use by the Health and Hospital Corporation in paying for a portion of the cost of the 2023-2024 Capital
Improvement Projects, paying interest on the Bonds, if determined to be necessary or appropriate in accordance with the terms of this Resolution, and paying for the costs of issuance of the Bonds. Such appropriation shall be in addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy and shall continue in effect until the completion of the 2023-2024 Capital improvement Projects. Any surplus of such proceeds may be used to pay debt service on the Bonds or otherwise used as permitted by law.

SECTION 3. This Resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code §§ 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15 and 36-3-4-16.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.  , 2024 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City ‑County Annual Budget for 2024 (City -County Fiscal 
Ordinance No. 14, 2023) by an additional appropriation of seven hundred twelve thousand dollars  ($712,000) for the purposes of Marion County Coroner. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY‑COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF 
MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers  hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Coroner. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Marion County Coroner  requests an additional appropriation totaling one hundred  twenty thousand dollars ($120,000) in the County General Fund in character three, and an additional  appropriation totaling five hundred ninety -two thousand dollars ($592,000) in the State of Indiana Grants -
County Fund in character three for the purpose of funding pathology and toxicology services, trauma  informed care programs and other office related expenses.

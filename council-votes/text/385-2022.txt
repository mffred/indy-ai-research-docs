CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2022
Proposal No. 385, 2021

ACOUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Louis Profeta to the Indianapolis-Marion County Forensic Services
Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis-Marion County Forensic Services Board, the Council reappoints:

Louis Profeta
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2023. The person

appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council and until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, unless the duration of the holdover period for this office is limited by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10 day of January, 2022, at 8:07 p.m.

COUNCIL RESOLUTON RECORD 2026 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 12, 2026 
Proposal No. 67, 2026 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s appointment of Tanya B. Terry as the Chief of Police  for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 279-221 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated 
City and County,” the appointment of the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police 
Department is subject to the confirmation by the City-County Council; and  
 
 WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Tanya B. 
Terry to serve as the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department at his pleasure  for a term ending December 31, 2026; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. Tanya B. Terry is confirmed by the City- County Council to serve as the Chief of Police  for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department at the pleasure of the mayor, for a term ending 
December 31, 2026, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.  
 
SECTON 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC §  36-3-4-14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2nd day of March, 2026, at 7:23 p.m.

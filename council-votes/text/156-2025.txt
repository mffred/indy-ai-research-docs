CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2025
Proposal No, 156, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Sacred Heart’s 150th Jubilee year celebration.

WHEREAS, in 1850, Germans were the largest immigrant group in Indianapolis. Initially, German
Catholics attended Mass at St. John’s, an English-speaking church. Aware that other German denominations were forming their own churches, plans were soon underway for a church where sermons, confessions and devotions could be heard in their familiar German; and

WHEREAS, the Bishop approved a new parish site in the Nathan B. Palmer's Addition, west of
Madison and indianapolis railroad tracks and north of Palmer Street. This was a rural location for farmland and was purchased for $10,000. On September 19, 1875, various societies and musical units organized at St. Mary’s and marched down Meridian Street to the new Sacred Heart, where Benedictine Father Bede
O’Connor laid the cornerstone for the new building; and

WHEREAS, since 1875, Sacred Heart of Jesus Catholic Church has been central to the life and culture of the Old Southside Neighborhood. Sacred Heart is noteworthy for being the only church in
Indianapolis designed by Franciscan Brother Adrian Wewer, OFM, a prolific architect who designed more than 100 Catholic churches between 1864 and 1914. Brother Wewer continued to design every building that was added to the Sacred Heart campus. This historic Church includes decorative painting, hand- carved woodwork by local artisan GH Hermann, beautifully crafted statues and altars with stained glass windows from Franz Mayer of Munich. Sacred Heart’s twin bell towers ring out the hours as an enduring symbol of strength and hope for families with deep roots and all the new residents who are bringing a revitalization to the Old Southside; and

WHEREAS, the community of Sacred Heart of Jesus, a Franciscan parish with the power of the Holy spirit, joyfully proclaims the Gospel of Jesus Christ through words, action and prayer for the salvation of souls. Sacred Heart is now celebrating 150 years of service to the southside of Indianapolis. “Celebrating
Our Past, Embracing the Present, and Building for our Future” will be the guide for the next 150 years; now, therefore.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Sacred Heart's 150th Jubilee year celebration, which will be held on September 20, 2025.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily thanks the Sacred Heart Church for their contributions to the City of
Indianapolis and wishes them the best in their future endeavors.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9! day of June, 2025, at 7:29 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION 2026 
Proposal No. 137, 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to direct the City -County Council to accept, for the record,  the 2026 First Quarter Report submitted by the Marion County Sheriff (Sheriff), as required by HEA 1208  (2025), Public Law 54 (2025), and IC 36-8-10-21. 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21 establishes a jail commissary fund (“the fund”); and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(c) requires that the Sheriff deposits all money from the fund in a depository  as designated under IC 5-13-8; and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(d) allows the Sheriff to disburse, without appropriation of the City -County 
Council, money from the fund for specifically-listed items; and 
 
 WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(e) further requires that the Sheriff maintain a record of the fund’s receipts  and disbursements and provide a copy of those records, on a semiannual basis, to the City-County Council;  and 
 
 WHEREAS, this quarterly report also includes, for informational purposes only, correspondence from  the National Commission on Correctional Health Care, providing a Certificate of Accreditation; as well as  the Marion County Adult Detention Center Report dated January 27, 2026, as tendered to the Indiana 
Department of Correction, in accordance with IC 36-2-13-12; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1. The Marion County Sheriff hereby submits to the City-County Council, for the record, its April  15
th, for the quarter ending March 31, 2026, commissary report, attached hereto as Exhibit A . 
 
SECTION 2. It is acknowledged and recognized that IC 36-8-10-21 requires no further action by the full  council. 
 
The report was received by the City-County Council this 1st day of June, 2026.

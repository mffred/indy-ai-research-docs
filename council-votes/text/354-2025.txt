CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 18, 2025
Proposal No. 364, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the 50th Annual Southside Reunion Picnic.

WHEREAS, the Annual Southside Reunion Picnic, held on August 2, 2025, has been celebrated each year in Babe Denny Park for the past 50 years, this resolution honors and commemorates that history.
Affectionately known as the “First Saturday In August,” this cherished tradition unites generations of the
Babe Denny neighborhood, preserving bonds of friendship and family that endured even after Interstate 70 displaced this close-knit, historically African American, and formerly also Jewish community in the 1970s;
and

WHEREAS, the reunion began as William Lester Craig Day in honor of the owner of Craig Funeral
Home, whose leadership with the men’s Southside Fellowship Club helped sustain family ties. The event was first held at Meikel Street Park, later renamed Babe Denny Park in 1977 to honor Edward Bay “Babe”
Denny, a Southsider, a dedicated city employee, and the first Black motorcycle police officer in indianapolis;
and

WHEREAS, through the deep bonds shared by close-knit families and friends, this community has remained strong thanks to this annual reunion. Echoes of the past still resonate today as documented in the 2012 publication The Neighborhood of Saturdays, which chronicles the oral history of African American and Sephardic Jewish residents who lived side by side from the 1910s to the 1960s in this historic and diverse Near Southside neighborhood; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council of Indianapolis, Indiana, recognizes this 50th Annual Southside
Reunion Picnic—which included music, laughter, tears of joy, food, activities, guest speakers, and awards— as more than an occasion for reminiscence and reconnection. This annual event, which continue to be celebrated jong into the future, stands as a powerful testament to the community’s enduring resilience and unity in the face of change; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianapolis City-County Council recognizes the 50" Annual Southside Reunion Picnic held on August 2, 2025, representing a community’s steadfast unity and resilience.

SECTION 2. The Council celebrates the extraordinary contributions of the residents, past and present, of this historic neighborhood surrounding the Babe Denny Park on the near southside of Indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3rd day of November, 2025, at 7:19 p.m.

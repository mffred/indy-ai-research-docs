CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF DENIED ZONING PETITION           ) 
 
 I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Interim Current Planning Administrator and Principal 
Planner II, Division of Current Planning, Department of Metropolitan Development,  certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on March 19, 2025 following a public hearing as required  by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis 
Star” and “Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion 
County, Indiana, said Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed  zoning ordinance amendment prayed for in petition: 
 
2024-ZON-073 2155 Kessler Boulevard, West Drive (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 132, 2025 Washington Township, Council District #2 
 DENIAL UPHELD Broadmoor Investments, LLC, by Russell L. Brown 
  04-07-25   25-0 Rezoning of 6.01 acres from the SU-34 (FF) district to the D-4 (FF)  district to provide for residential uses. 
 
2024-ZON-073B  2155 Kessler Boulevard, West Drive (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 133, 2025 Washington Township, Council District #2 
 DENIAL UPHELD Broadmoor Investments, LLC, by Russell L. Brown 
  04-07-25   25-0 Rezoning of 7.68 acres from the SU-34 district to the D-5II district  to provide for residential uses. 
 
2024-ZON-110 6670 East 38th Street (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 134, 2025 Lawrence Township, Council District #9 
 DENIAL UPHELD Tikal, Inc., by Mitch Sever 
  04-07-25   25-0 Rezoning of 2.37 acres from the C-3 (TOD) district to the C-7  (TOD) classification to provide for a building contractor, with  outdoor storage of equipment and materials.  
 
 I FURTHER CERTIFY that the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members recommended denial of 2024-
ZON-073, 2024-ZON-073B, and 2024-ZON-110 to the City-County Council.

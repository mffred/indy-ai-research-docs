CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 30, 2023
Proposal No. 229, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve the First Amendment to the 2007 Restated
Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Benefit Plan.

WHEREAS, the Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Benefit Plan (hereinafter referred to as "Benefit Plan”) was established by the Marion County Sheriff's Office, Indianapolis, Indiana, formerly known as the Marion County Sheriffs Department, effective November 1, 1972, and was restated in 1987 and again in 2007; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Sheriff's Office reserved the right to amend the Benefit Plan as allowed by state law; and

WHEREAS, the First Amendment to the Benefit Plan shall amend the following provisions of the
Benefit Pian:

(1) A change to Article li, Section 3(a) of the Benefit Plan will resolve an ambiguity between two sentences in this section, the first of which is: "Otherwise, a Participant may be determined to be eligible for disability benefits under this Section by the Pension Board based upon medical evidence which is satisfactory to the Pension Board”; and the second of which is “The Pension
Board will determine the Participant to be disabled only if at least two (2) of the three (3) doctors shall find that the Participant is unable, by reason of disability, to perform the duties assigned to him by IMPD, or in the case of the Sheriff, by the Sheriffs Department.” The amendment removes the second sentence to clarify that the responsibility to determine whether a participant is disabled rests with the Marion County Sheriff's Department Pension Board as the first sentence states.

(2) A change to Article Il, Section 3(b) of the Benefit Plan to provide that the benefit paid to a participant who is determined to have incurred a disability not in the line of duty shall be based on the participant’s actual years of credited service, rather than the greater of twenty (20) years of credited service or actual years of credited service as the Benefit Plan currently provides. This change will equalize benefits paid to disabled and non-disabled officers who retire from service under the Benefit Plan and the Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Retirement Pian (hereinafter referred to as “Retirement Plan’).

(3) Achange to Article 11, Section 3(b) of the Benefit Plan to provide that the monthly disability benefit under the Benefit Plan will not commence until the participant has received from the Retirement
Plan the aggregate amount of monthly payments equal to the participant's net amount of contributions to the Retirement Plan with interest to the date of severance from employment, plus the amount transferred by the participant to the Retirement Plan for the purchase of credited service. This change will equalize benefits paid to disabled and non-disabled officers who retire from service under the Benefit and Retirement Plans; and

WHEREAS, Indiana Code 36-8-10-15 mandates that a department may not establish or modify a disability benefit program without approval of the county fiscal body; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Pursuant to the specific authority grant by Indiana Code 36-8-10-15, the “First Amendment to 2007 Restated Marion County Sheriffs Department Personnel Benefit Plan” as attached hereto and incorporated herein, shall be and hereby is approved.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance with Indiana Code 36-3-4-14.

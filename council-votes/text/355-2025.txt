A PROPOSAL FOR A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving a schedule of regular council meetings for the year 2026.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby approves the following schedule of regular meetings for the year 2026:

(1) Monday, January 5, 2026 (8) Monday, August 10, 2026

(2) Monday, February 2, 2026 (9) Monday, September 14, 2026 (3) Monday, March 2, 2026 (10) Monday, October 5, 2026

(4) Monday, April 6, 2026 (11) Monday, October 19, 2026
(5) Monday, May 4, 2026 (12) Monday, November 2, 2026
(6) Monday, June 1, 2026 (13) Monday, December 7, 2026

(7) Monday, July 6, 2026

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4~ 44.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3° day of November, 2025, at 7:33 p.m.

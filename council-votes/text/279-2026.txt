CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve the issuance of general obligation bonds by 
The Health and Hospital Corporation of Marion County, Indiana (the “Health and Hospital Corporation”) to  finance all or any portion of the 2026/2027 Capital Improvement Projects (as defined below) and the  appropriation of the proceeds thereof. 
 
WHEREAS, for purposes of this Resolution, the “2026/2027 Capital Improvement Projects” is defined  as all or any portion of the following projects, each of which is reasonably considered to be an  independently desirable end in itself without reference to another capital project, including, but not limited  to, all or any portion of the following: (1) acquisition of separate parcels of real estate and the design,  engineering, construction, re- construction, renovation, remodel, repair, improvement and/or equipping  thereon of three (3) separate new ambulance stations, including mechanical, electrical and plumbing  system upgrades and improvements, various interior and exterior improvements, landscaping and overall  site improvements, and/or the addition of one or more new ambulance bays, each to be used by 
Indianapolis Emergency Medical Services; (2) the acquisition of other real estate to further the purposes  of the Health and Hospital Corporation; (3) the purchase of equipment for use at facilities owned or  operated, or to be owned or operated, by the Health and Hospital Corporation; (4) security system  upgrades at facilities owned or operated, or to be owned or operated, by the Health and Hospital 
Corporation; and (5) all projects related to any of the projects or facilities described in clauses (1) through  and including (4); and 
 
WHEREAS, before the Health and Hospital Corporation may issue its general obligation bonds, 
Indiana Code 36-3-6-9 requires that the City-County Council approve the issuance of such bonds; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City-County Council being duly advised, finds that it is in the best interests of the City  of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”), the County and their citizens for the Health and Hospital Corporation  to issue its general obligation bonds to finance all or any portion of the 2026/2027 Capital Improvement 
Projects, in order to better serve the residents of the City and the County; now therefore:  
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. In accordance with Indiana Code 36- 3-6-9, the City -County Council hereby approves the  issuance by the Health and Hospital Corporation of its general obligation bonds in one or more series (the  “Bonds”), for the purpose of providing funds to finance all or any portion of the costs of the 2026/2027 
Capital Improvement Projects, all upon the following conditions: (a) the original aggregate principal  amount of the Bonds shall not exceed $25,000,000; (b) the maximum interest rate or rates on the Bonds  shall produce a yield not exceeding six percent (6.00%) per annum; and (c) each series of the Bonds will  have a maximum term of twenty (20) years. 
 
SECTION 2. In accordance with Indiana Code 6- 1.1-18-5, the City-County Council hereby approves the  additional appropriation made by the Health and Hospital Corporation not to exceed $25,000,000, which  appropriation is to be provided out of the proceeds of the Bonds received by the Health and Hospital 
Corporation in connection with the sale of the Bonds, together with all investment earnings thereon, for  the use by the Health and Hospital Corporation in paying for a portion of the cost of the 2026/2027 Capital 
Improvement Projects, paying interest on the Bonds, if determined to be necessary or appropriate in  accordance with the terms of this Resolution, and paying for the costs of issuance of the Bonds.  Such  appropriation shall be in addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy and  shall continue in effect until the completion of the 2026/2027 Capital Improvement Projects.  Any surplus  of such proceeds may be used to pay debt service on the Bonds or otherwise used as permitted by law.  
 
SECTION 3. This Resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana 
Code §§ 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15 and 36-3-4-16.

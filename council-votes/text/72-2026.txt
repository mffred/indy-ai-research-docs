CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 8, 2026
Propasal No. 72, 2026

A PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION determining the need for the Marion County Clerk’s Office to lease approximately 36,377 square feet of office space at 1330 S. Madison Ave, indianapolis, IN 46225 (as depicted in Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein).

WHEREAS, the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana ("City-
County Council’) is the fiscal body of the City of Indianapolis pursuant to Ind. Code 36-1-2-6.

WHEREAS, the Marion County Clerk’s Office (“Clerk”) is responsible for keeping and managing the records and dockets of the Marion County Circuit and Superior Courts (“Courts”), as well as performing other duties in accordance with Indiana law.

WHEREAS, in accordance with those duties set forth by Indiana law, the Clerk requires a facility to securely store and maintain the paper and microfilm copies of records.

WHEREAS, the Clerk currently leases a structure located at 1330 S. Madison Ave, Indianapolis, IN 46225 (the “real estate”) from Linda Kopetsky, LLC. This lease expires on April 30, 2026, without an option therein for renewal.

WHEREAS, for the above-stated purposes, the Clerk wishes to amend the current lease of the real estate to:

(1) renew the term for:
(a) a five (5) year renewal term; and
(b) one renewal option for an additional five (5) years term;

(2) amend the rental terms to indicate the new annual rental amounts during:
(a) the initial five (5) year renewal term (which exceeds $250,000/annually on average); and (b) the renewal option (which shall be for fair market rental value — as defined in the lease); and

(3) modify other terms and conditions for repairs and maintenance.

WHEREAS, pursuant fo Ind. Code 36-1-10-7, if the annual cost of a proposed lease is $250,000 or more, the leasing agent (the Clerk) cannot lease a structure unless it receives: (1) a petition signed by fifty (50) or more taxpayers of Marion County; and (2) the fiscal body (the City-County Council) determines, after investigation, that the structure is needed.

WHEREAS, the Clerk has obtained the requisite taxpayer petition and signatures required.

WHEREAS, the City-County Council, having considered the lease of the structure, conducting an appropriate investigation into the need of the structure, and being duly advised, finds that the structure is needed by the Clerk; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby determines that a structure for the Marion County Clerk’s
Office’s records is needed, that the structure located at 1330 S. Madison Ave, Indianapolis, IN 46225 is adequate, and approves the renewal of the lease between the Clerk and Linda Kopetsky, LLC for said structure.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION urging data center developers to heed community voices.  
 
WHEREAS, hundreds of taxpayers and residents of Indianapolis and Marion County have  participated in public meetings, rallies, and protests against proposed data center developments in the  second half of 2025; and 
 
WHEREAS, several high -profile data center developments have utilized non -disclosure agreements  (NDAs) which have prevented all taxpayers and residents from learning details about the proposed  developments early in the development process; and 
 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis-Marion County’s current zoning and planning processes do not always allow  the City-County Council to vote on approving data center projects; and 
 
WHEREAS, the State of Indiana offers a number of very competitive tax abatements, including a  potential 50-year sales tax exemption for data centers, but 
 
WHEREAS, three counties in Indiana have adopted complete moratoriums on all data center projects  in their counties; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. The City -County Council of Indianapolis and Marion County calls for developers currently  working on proposals involving data centers to transparently engage with the community without the use  of Non-Disclosure Agreements and seek mutually beneficial Community Benefit Agreements.  
 
SECTION 2. Developers are discouraged from pursuing local tax abatements for their projects.  
 
SECTION 3. The City -County Council would prefer to avoid the need for a total moratorium on all data  centers, and is thus offering guidance as to our wishes for developers. 
 
SECTION 4. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.  
 
SECTION 5. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-4- 14.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2025
Proposal No. 387, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Joseph Wynns to the Board of Parks and Recreation.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Parks and Recreation, the Council reappoints:

Joseph Wynns
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025. The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term,

the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8" day of January, 2025, at 7:19 pm

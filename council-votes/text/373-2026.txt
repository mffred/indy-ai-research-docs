CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing October 2026 as Youth Justice Action Month. 
 
WHEREAS, every young person in Indianapolis deserves the opportunity to grow, learn, heal, and  thrive in safe, supportive communities that recognize their dignity, strengths, and potential; and  
 
WHEREAS, Youth Justice Action Month, observed nationally each October and led by the Coalition  for Juvenile Justice, raises awareness of the experiences of young people impacted by youth justice  systems and promotes prevention, healing, youth leadership, family engagement, and community -based  solutions; and 
 
WHEREAS, young people with lived experience in youth justice and child welfare systems possess  valuable knowledge and perspectives that are essential to developing more responsive policies,  practices, and systems; and 
 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis is home to organizations, advocates, families, and young leaders working to  advance youth justice, including the Marion County Commission on Youth, Indiana Youth Institute, 
Children’s Policy and Law Initiative of Indiana, Tru Colors, Center for Lived Futures, and many others;  and 
 
WHEREAS, meaningful youth engagement requires more than listening to young people’s stories; it  requires recognizing their expertise, supporting their leadership, and creating opportunities for them to  participate in decisions affecting their lives; and 
 
WHEREAS, Youth Justice Action Month provides an opportunity for residents, community  organizations, public officials, and system leaders to strengthen collaboration and advance approaches  rooted in opportunity, accountability, healing, and community well-being; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY , INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Indianapolis City -County Council recognizes October 2026 as Youth Justice Action 
Month and encourages residents and community partners to celebrate youth leadership, elevate lived  expertise, and work together to build a future where all young people can thrive.  
 
SECTION 2.  The Indianapolis City -County Council commends the young people, families, advocates,  service providers, and community organizations whose dedication, leadership, and lived expertise

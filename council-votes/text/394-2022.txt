CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 18, 2022
Proposal No. 394, 2022

ASPECIAL ORDINANCE of the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana amending a special ordinance authorizing the issuance of one or more series of its City of Indianapolis, Indiana Economic
Development Tax Increment Revenue Bonds, Series 202_ (Monon 32 Project) and its City of Indianapolis,
Indiana Economic Development Tax Increment Revenue Bonds, Series 202_ (Domino Project).

WHEREAS, on September 12, 2022, the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “Council”) adopted its Special Ordinance No. 10, 2022 (the "Bond Ordinance”) approving, among other matters, the issuance by the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) of if taxable or tax-exempt economic development tax increment revenue bonds in one or more series (collectively, the “Bonds”) to finance a portion of the costs of (i) a multi-family residential development along 1120 East 32" Street, situated between the Monon Trail and Winthrop Avenue, known as “Monon 32” and consisting of approximately 81 new construction, high-qual professionally managed for rent apartment homes (townhome/duplex project) (the “Monon 32 Project”) and (ii) a multi-family residential development along 3122 Dr. Andrew J.
Brown Avenue, situated between the Monon Trail and Dr. Andrew J. Brown Avenue, known as “The
Domino” and consisting of approximately 220 new construction, high-quality apartment units with parking and amenities (the “Domino Project’, together with the Monon 32 Project, the “Projects”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 3 of the Bond Ordinance, the Council authorized the Bonds for the
Project to bear interest at an interest rate not exceeding six and one-half percent (6.5%) per annum; and

WHEREAS, the Council has been advised by its staff and municipal advisor that as a result of recent changes in the financial market conditions it should authorize an increase in the maximum interest rate on the Bonds to eight percent (8.0%) per annum; and

WHEREAS, the Council having considered the matter finds that the Bond Ordinance should be so amended; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The reference to the not fo exceed interest rate in Section 3 of the Bond Ordinance is hereby amended to reflect a maximum interest rate not to exceed eight percent (8.0%) per annum.

SECTION 2. The Council hereby approves a corresponding change in the not to exceed interest rate reflected in the Financing Documents (as defined and referenced in the Bond Ordinance).

SECTION 3. This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon compliance with Indiana Code 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5" day of December, 2022, at 8:05 p.m.

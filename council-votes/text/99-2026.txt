CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 3, 2026
Proposal No. 99, 2026

PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL ORDINANCE authorizing the City of Indianapolis-Marion County to advance the curfew hours to “public safety” curfew hours pursuant to Indiana Code §31-37-3-4 and Chapter 381,
Sections 103 through 105 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City of Indianapolis - Marion County.

WHEREAS, Indiana Code (IC) § 31-37-3-2 and Chapter 381 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated
City of Indianapolis — Marion County ("Rev Code”) set out curfew hours for children 17 years of age and younger; and

WHEREAS, IC 31-37-3-4 states, in part, that whenever the curfew times are later than is reasonable for public safety, the city, by ordinance, may advance the curfew hours by not more than 2 hours; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 31-37-3-4 the City of Indianapolis-Marion County has established pu safety curfew hours in Rev Code Sections 381-103 through 105; and

WHEREAS, it has been determined by the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department that an increase in public safety incidents involving juveniles warrant advancing the curfew hours to “public safety” hours; now, therefore

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council finds that conditions do exist within the county thereby establishing that the current curfew hours are later than is reasonable for public safety.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council hereby adopts public safety curfew hours, as established in Rev
Code Sections 381-103 through 105.

SECTION 3. In compliance with Rev. Code Chapter 381, Section 105, the invocation of the public safety curfew hours shall expire one hundred twenty (120) days after this ordinance goes into effect.

SECTION 4. This ordinance shail be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance with Ind. Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 4th day of May, 2026, at 8:12 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 15, 2026
Proposal No. 71, 2026

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Shea Joyce as the Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 201-4 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County, the position of Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations is created; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to 1C 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-4 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County, a mayoral appointment of Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations is subject to approval by the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Shea Joyce to serve as the Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations at his pleasure for a term ending
December 31, 2026 now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The position of Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations is created.
SECTION 2. Shea Joyce is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Deputy Mayor for government affairs and operations for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is

appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6th day of April, 2026, at 7:15 p.m.

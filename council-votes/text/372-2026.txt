CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION  of the City -County Council of Indianapolis and Marion County honoring and  expressing gratitude to the first responders  and volunteers  who courageously served the residents of 
Indianapolis during the historic flooding of August 2026. 
 
WHEREAS, numerous residents and volunteers devoted their time and labor to filling and placing  sandbags, checking on neighbors, sharing emergency information, delivering supplies, and assisting  with evacuation, cleanup, and recovery; and 
 
WHEREAS, elected town officials, neighborhood leaders, community organizations, businesses, and  faith communities helped organize volunteers and connect affected residents with shelter, food, and other  much needed assistance; and 
 
WHEREAS, first responders, dispatchers, public works employees, emergency management  personnel, and partner agencies worked alongside these community members to protect residents and  respond to changing conditions; and 
 
WHEREAS, residents who experienced flooding, displacement, property damage, and uncertainty  have firsthand knowledge that is essential to understanding the response and improving preparations for  future emergencies; and 
 
WHEREAS, their efforts protected lives across Marion County and surrounding regions as the White 
River reached historic crest levels, surpassing records  that were previously held  for more than a century;  and 
  
WHEREAS, honoring this service carries a responsibility to support recovery, examine the decisions   made during the e mergency, and pursue lasting improvements in future flood protection and  preparedness; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Council expresses its heartfelt gratitude to the residents, volunteers, and local leaders   who served their neighbors during the August 2026 floods and the recovery that followed, including those   who offered help while facing losses of their own.

SECTION 2.  The Council also honors the first responders, public employees, community organizations,  businesses, and other partners whose efforts supported affected neighborhoods and helped protect lives.  
 
SECTION 3.  The Council urges the City administration, in partnership with affected communities and   relevant agencies, to conduct a thorough and transparent after -action review of flood preparedness,   emergency response, and the transition to recovery, and to make its findings and recommendations   publicly available. 
 
SECTION 4.  Meaningful public participation should be a central part of the review.  Impacted residents  should have opportunities to share their stories, ask questions, and recommend improvements before  findings and recommendations are finalized. 
 
SECTION 5.  The Council further urges the administration to publish online, in an easily accessible  location on the City's website, all plans, policies, procedures, and agreements governing flood protection,  floodgate operations, and emergency response procedures. 
 
SECTION 6.  The Council urges the administration to continue working with affected communities on  recovery and long-term flood protection. 
 
SECTION 7.  The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.  
 
SECTION 8.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36- 3- 4-14.

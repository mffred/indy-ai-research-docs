CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 28, 2026 
Proposal No. 130, 2026 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Lieutenant Larry P. Adkins  to the Domestic Violence Fatality 
Review Team. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Domestic Violence Fatality Review Team (fulfilling the requirement of a  member of law enforcement), the Council reappoints:  
 
Lieutenant Larry P. Adkins 
 
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending June 1, 2028.  The person  reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term, the  reappointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the  holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of June, 2026, at 7:14 p.m.

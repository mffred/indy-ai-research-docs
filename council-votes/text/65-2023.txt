CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 12, 2023
Proposal No. 65, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Pike Township Trustee, Annette Johnson, as the recipient of the 2022
Harold “Gene” Stofer Trustee of the Year.

WHEREAS, Annette M. Johnson works as an educator, a trustee for Pike Township, and an elected official. She graduated from Ball State University and Martin University, where she earned three degrees in adult community education, executive development for public service, and career technical education.
She also possesses a master's and bachelor's degree in elementary education from Martin University; and

WHEREAS, Trustee Johnson serves in various capacities with Voter Outreach Education; Voter
Registration as a Precinct Committee Chair, and Voter Inspector; and

WHEREAS, Trustee Johnson was a writer for the Indiana Herald Newspaper and a blogger with the
Indianapolis Recorder. She is a member of the NAACP, Indiana Democratic African-American Caucus (IDAAC), Top Ladies of Distinction, Inc., Gamma Phi Delta Sorority, Inc., Kiwanis, Toastmasters, and the
National Council for Negro Women (NCNW); and

WHEREAS, Trustee Johnson is a member of the boards of the Ortho Indy YMCA, Community Action of Greater Indianapolis, the Fay Biccard Glick Neighborhood Center, and the Martin Center Sickle Cell
Initiative. She also sits on the boards of Hoosier Interfaith Power & Light and lobbies for the African-
American community and environmental concerns. In addition, she participates on the Indiana Black Expo
Education Committee; and

WHEREAS, Trustee Johnson has worked as a program educator specialist for IPS, the Marion County
Sheriffs Office, and the Indiana Department of Education's Outreach Division. She currently serves as the
Trustee of Pike Township and is an Elected O |, where she runs a small claims court, an emergency help office, and offers fire and EMS services through the Pike Township Fire Department; and

WHEREAS, Trustee Johnson was recognized as a 2016 Trailblazer of the Year Volunteer Impact
Award Recipient. She was awarded the 2019 Charles S. Kinchelow Civil and Human Rights Award and the 2019 Biack Opal Honoree Educator Award; and

WHEREAS, at Ball State University, Trustee Johnson is presently pursuing a doctoral degree while working in the field of adult, higher, and community education; and

WHEREAS, Annette, a mother, grandmother, ardent Democrat, activist, and mild rabble-rouser, continues to be an advocate for not only her district. She is renowned in the neighborhood for being everywhere and for sticking up for what is right, which is demonstrated by her continuous presence in the neighborhood; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes Pike Township Trustee, Annette Johnson, as the recipient of the 2022 Harold “Gene” Stofer Trustee of the Year by the Indiana Township Association.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis City-County Council acknowledges and appreciates Trustee Annette
Johnson’s dedication and commitment to bettering the lives of citizens who not only live in Pike Township, but Indianapolis as a whole.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4, This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 43, 2021
Proposal No. 59, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving Richard Amberger as the Director of the Indianapolis-Marion County
Forensic Services Agency.

WHEREAS, pursuant fo Section 283-331 of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” the Mayor shall appoint a director of the Indianapolis-Marion County Forensic Services Agency which was recommended by the board and is subject to the approval of the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor has appointed Richard Amberger at the recommendation of the Indianapolis-
Marion County Forensic Services Board; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Richard Amberger is approved by the City-County Council to serve as the Director of the
Indianapolis-Marion County Forensic Services Agency.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its adoption by the Council and compliance with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

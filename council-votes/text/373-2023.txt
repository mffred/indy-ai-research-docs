CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2023 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Arael Stigler for winning the Boys & Gir ls Clubs of Indianapolis 2023 
Youth of the Year Award.  
 
WHEREAS, for 130 years, Boys & Girls Clubs of Indianapolis (BGCI) has provided a safe and nurturing  place for every young person in Indianapolis to grow and thrive. It has worked tirelessly to provide them  with the tools and mentorship they need to reach t heir full potential and become positive, productive  citizens. Each year, BGCI recognizes its members for the exemplary work as members, student, and  citizens; and  
 
WHEREAS, a native of Indianapolis, Arael Stigler has come a long way as a BGCI youth. Since joining  in 2016, Arael has grown leaps and bounds personally and academically. Starting as a reserved, shy  member of our Club, Arael has transformed into a bold and fearless leader both within the Club and her  community; and  
 
WHEREAS, Arael has been actively involved in the Finish Line Club for the past few summers, serving  as a small group leader. She will take the lead in anything related to the arts. As an active participant in  dance, step, and cheer teams, Arael is always willing to volunteer with dance/step programming; and  
 
WHEREAS, as a student at Lawrence Central High School, Arael is also a standout leader. She is a  student section leader in the choir, a member of the National Honor Society, and participates in Key Club. 
Additionally, Arael chooses to sacrifice her time at the Club as a staff member so that she can focus solely  on academic achievement; and  
 
WHEREAS, Arael attributes her leadership and growth at the Club to consistent support and  encouragement from her Club director, Tialana Kastner. She says that the value in her time at the Club has  made her who she is today; and  
 
WHEREAS, Arael has grown into an amazing leader amongst peers, she carries herself with incredible  poise and she is loved and respected by all. After years of Club and personal growth and dedication, she  has made her academic goals very clear: Arael plans to attend Tennessee State University to major in  nursing; now, therefore:

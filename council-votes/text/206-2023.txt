CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 29, 2023
Proposal No. 206, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Wells family for their incredible accomplishments in the Soap
Box Derby.

WHEREAS, the Indianapolis Soap Box Derby is a youth gravity racing program for boys and girls ages eight through seventeen years. The goals of the Soap Box Derby program have not changed since it began in. 1934: to promote families working together and to teach children basic skills in science and technology, mechanics, family, teamwork, leadership, diversity, and friendship; and

WHEREAS, a long time ago Cleo Wells stated that his sons; CJ, Marcus, Marlon, Matthew and Geordan were “in it to win it.” Little did he know just how hard that would be; and

WHEREAS, the boys worked hard and had to go through failures to get to the top, but with their father with them every step of the way, they achieved incredible things; and

WHEREAS, CJ, Cleo’s eldest, placed 4" in the worid in the Masters Division. Marlon won the
Indianapolis Soap Box Derby in the Masters Division and also placed 9, 7", and 1% in the World
Championship. He also placed 3" in the All-American Rally Challenge race, and

WHEREAS, Geordan also won the Indianapolis Soap Box Derby in the Masters Division and also in the Super Stock Division. He also was the first World Champion in the Legacy Division and was also awarded the All-American Leadership Award, presented to him at the World Championships; and

WHEREAS, Marcus won the Indianapolis Soap Box Derby in the Masters Division, as well as placing 24 in Nationals, in the Legacy Division; and

WHEREAS, Matthew placed 4" in the World in the Masters Division. Throughout the history of the
Soap Box Derby, many families have accomplished tremendous things, with some having success with one child in multiple divisions, some having placed more than one child in the World Championships; and

WHEREAS, to best knowledge, no family has ever had four different children place in the World
Championship; with two World Championship firsts, and two placing second in the World; and

WHEREAS, because of hard work and determination, the Wells family is the most successful racing family today in Soap Box Derby; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 4. The Indianapolis City-County Council congratulates the Wells family for their many accomplishments in the Soap Box Derby races.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the efforts and goals of the
Indianapolis Soap Box Derby.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14,

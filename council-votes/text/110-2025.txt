CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 8, 2025
Proposal No. 110, 2025

ASPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Early Intervention Planning Council during April’s National Child
Abuse Prevention Month, and supporting the council's focus on building hope for the future of children and families.

WHEREAS, the Early Intervention Planning Council was established in 2005 by the City-County
Council of Indianapolis-Marion County to study the complex issues that contribute to child and family involvement with Marion County's child welfare and juvenile legal systems; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Commission on Youth (MCCOY) has served as the designated convener of the EIPC since 2009, supporting the council's mission to eliminate child abuse, neglect, and juvenile involvement with the legal system by coordinating efforts that increase availability and access to services and advocate for high-quality early intervention and prevention services in Marion County; and

WHEREAS, the EIPC has put forth the Where the Children Are report — a report on the state of child permanency in Marion County, Indiana, identifying permanency as a critical component in positive youth development and a priority of early intervention and prevention; and

WHEREAS, understanding the Where the Children Are report data at the county and state level, the
EIPC hopes to develop a common understanding of the state of child permanency in our county, as well as begin to understand the circumstances of children who have been removed from one environment to be placed in another, thereby potentially disrupting their overall situation of permanency; and

WHEREAS, over 7,000 children lacked permanency in Marion County last year due to housing insecurity, juvenile detention, or out-of-home placement as a Child in Need of Services (CHINs) ; and

WHEREAS, the EIPC recognizes April as National Child Abuse Prevention Month, a time to connect with families, communities, prevention advocates, and supporters to focus on the creation of an ecosystem of primary prevention in Marion County and emphasize the importance of building a narrative of hope for ren and families; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes the Early Intervention Planning Council during April's
National Child Abuse Prevention Month, and expresses its support for the council’s focus on building hope for the future of children and families.

SECTION 2. The EIPC calls upon leaders across the county to be informed and take action to improve outcomes for our children whose circumstances have led or will lead to displacement and lack of permanency.

SECTION 3. The Council asks that the community and its organizations work together to assure all children have access to safe, stable, nurturing relationships and homes.

SECTION 4. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 5. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with iC 36-3-4- 14.

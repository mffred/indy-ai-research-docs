CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 2, 2024
Proposal No. 9, 2024

A GENERAL RESOLUTION approving the intergovernmental transfer of certain real property from the
Consolidated City to the Department of Metropolitan Development, on behalf of the Metropolitan
Development Commission.

WHEREAS, IC 36-1-11-8 authorizes the transfer or exchange of real property between governmental entities upon terms and conditions agreed upon by the entities as evidenced by adoption of a ly identical resolution by each entity, and the Consolidated City of Indianapolis and Marion
County (“City”) is a governmental entity entitled to initiate such an intergovernmental transfer; and

WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, acting as the
Redevelopment Commission of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “Commission”), being the governing body of the Redevelopment District of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana, exists and operates under the provisions of Indiana Code 36-7-15.1, as amended from time to time; and

WHEREAS, IC 36-7-15.1-7 authorizes the Commission to acquire real property via gift or exchange when such property is needed for the redevelopment of areas needing redevelopment that are located within the Redevelopment District; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds that it is in the public interest for the City to transfer, and the
Commission desires to accept, ownership of the real property generally known as 37 Place, located at 2605 E. 25" Street, Indianapolis, IN 46218, as more particularly described on Exhibit A to this Resolution (the “Property”), for purposes of redevelopment; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Controller, or her designee within the Office of Finance and Management, is directed and authorized to effectuate the transfer of the Property to the Department of Metropolitan Development on behalf of the Commission, pursuant to IC 36-1-11-8 and IC 36-7-15.1-7.

SECTION 2. The intergovernmental transfer so authorized shall be for a purchase price of zero dollars ($0.00) and shall constitute the receipt of a gift by the Commission pursuant fo IC 36-7- 15.1-12.

SECTION 3. The appropriate officers of the City are hereby authorized to execute a deed and enter into any other agreements as may be necessary or desirable to further the purposes of this Resolution.

SECTION 4. This Resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3- 4-14 and the adoption of a substantially identical resolution by the Commission.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5!" day of February, 2024, at 8:17 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 51, 2022
Proposal No. 129, 2022

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Barato Britt to the Board of Business and Neighborhood Services.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Business and Neighborhood Services, the Council appoints:
Barato Britt

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2022. The person

appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2"4 day of May, 2022, at 8:19 p.m.

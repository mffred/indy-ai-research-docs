PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2021 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 20, 2020) transferring One Hundred Eight Thousand Dollars ($108,000) in the
Consolidated County General Fund for purposes of the City-County Council.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended by the transfer hereinafter stated for purposes of the
City-County Council, to cover the cost of a bilingual constituent services liaison and reflect current health insurance enrollment costs.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council requests a transfer from Character 03 to Character 01 totalling One
Hundred Eight Thousand Dollars ($108,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund.

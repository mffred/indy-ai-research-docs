CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2024
Proposal No. 112, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION celebrating the Recorder Women’s Auxiliary.

WHEREAS, the Recorder Women's Auxiliary was established December 1950. It was created as the
Christmas Cheer Fund, fo assist needy families during the holiday season; and

WHEREAS, the Recorder Women's Auxiliary was founded by Dr. Louise Batties, along with 19 other concerned female citizens; and

WHEREAS, the Recorder Women's Auxiliary proudly supports college-bound students with financial assistance, such as scholarships and book awards; and

WHEREAS, the Recorder Women's Auxiliary volunteers for many worthwhile causes throughout the year by lending their experience and expertise when called upon; and

WHEREAS, the Recorder Women's Auxiliary supports the Indianapolis Recorder Newspaper. With nearly 75 years of service to the community, The Recorder Women's Auxiliary is still a viable and integral part of the Indianapolis community; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Recorder Women's Auxiliary for their assistance to many needy families.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily recognizes the Recorder Women’s Auxiliary for their dedicated service to the communities in the City of indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4- 14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this ist day of April, 2024, at 7:38 p.m.

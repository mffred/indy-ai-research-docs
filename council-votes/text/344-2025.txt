CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.     , 2025 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 6, 202 4) by an additional  appropriation of twelve million dollars  ($12,000,000) and a transfer of  appropriation of eight hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($ 875,000) for the purposes of Indianapolis 
Metropolitan Police Department. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:  
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 20 25 is hereby amended by the  character increases, decreases and  transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department.  
 
SECTION 2.  Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department  requests an additional appropriation totaling  twelve million dollars ($12,000,000) in character one and three, and a transfer of appropriation totaling  two hundred sixty -five thousand dollars (265,000) from character four to character s one and three in the 
IMPD General Fund, a transfer of appropriation totaling two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000)  in  the City Cumulative Capital Fund from character two to character four, a transfer of appropriation totaling   one hundred and twenty five thousand dollars ($125,000) in the State Law Enforcement  Fund from  character two to character four, a transfer of appropriation totaling  hundred and twenty five thousand   dollars ($125,000) in the Federal Law Enforcement  Fund from character two to character four, and  a  transfer of appropriation totaling  one hundred and ten thousand  dollars ($110,000) in the Federal Grants 
Fund from character three to character four for the purposes of funding  collective bargaining agreement  with the Fraternal Order of Police Lodge 86 , mold remediation, law enforcement  equipment, and grant  cleanup.

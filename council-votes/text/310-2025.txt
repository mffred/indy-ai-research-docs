CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 84, 2025
Proposal No. 310, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Brian Barton to the Marion County Property Tax Assessment
Board of Appeals.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As amember of the Marion County Property Tax Assessment Board of Appeals, the Council reappoints:

Brian Barton
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2026. The person reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the

term, the reappointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

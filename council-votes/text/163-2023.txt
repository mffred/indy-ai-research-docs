Proposal No. 163, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL ORDINANCE approving an interlocal cooperation agreement between the
Consolidated City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana (“Indianapolis”) and the Town of Avon, Indiana (‘Avon’) for the construction and operation of improvements to Raceway Road.

WHEREAS, 1|.C. § 8-17-1-45 provides that each county is responsible for the construction, reconstruction, maintenance, and operation of the roads, including the ditches and signs for those roads, making up its southern and eastern boundaries; and

WHEREAS, Raceway Road is the boundary road between Marion County and Hendricks County, and it is the eastern boundary of Hendricks County; therefore, Avon is responsible for the construction, reconstruction, maintenance, and operation of the segment of Raceway Road within its boundaries; and

WHEREAS, Avon is preparing plans and specifications for a capital improvement project to improve the intersection of Raceway Road and County Road 100 North, which. is West 10" Street in Indianapolis (the “Project’), and the project will require Avon to acquire right-of-way and to conduct construction activities within the boundaries of Indianapolis; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to I.C. § 36-1-4-5, municipalities have the corporate power fo acquire and own interests in real property and, pursuant to IC. § 36-1-4-6, to use and improve interests in real property; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to |.C. §§ 36-1-4-18 and 36-1-3-9(c), a municipality may exercise its corporate powers outside its boundaries and inside the boundaries of another municipality if the two entities enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement approved by the fiscal body of each municipality under I.C. § 36-1-7-2; and

WHEREAS, accordingly, Avon and Indianapolis desire to enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement to allow Avon to carry out the Project and to operate and maintain the Project improvements within the boundaries of indianapolis; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council, having considered the matter and being duly advised, finds that the intersection or Raceway Road and West 10 Street (CR 100 N) serves and affects the citizens of and the economic well-being of both Indianapolis and Avon, that the Project will improve safety and mobility, and will accommodate future economic growth in the region, and that it will be of public utility and benefit to enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement with Avon to carry out the Project; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby approves an authorizes the Department of Public Works to enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement with the Town of Avon for purposes of authorizing Avon to acquire right-of-way, to operate and maintain such right-ofway, and to conduct construction activities within the boundaries of indianapolis as necessary for completion of the Project to improve the intersection of Raceway
Road and West 10" Street (County Road 100 North in Avon) and for the continued operation and maintenance of the improvements to said intersection.

SECTION 2. The Director of the Department of Public Works is hereby authorized to finalize, execute, and deliver an appropriate interlocal cooperation agreement on behalf of the Consolidated City of Indianapolis and
Marion County upon approval of the form and substance of such agreement by the Corporation Counsel or their designee.

SECTION 3. This Special Ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon Its passage by the City-County Council and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

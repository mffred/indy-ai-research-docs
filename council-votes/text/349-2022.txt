COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2022 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 77, 2022 
Proposal No. 349, 2022 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Sherry Seiwert to the Indianapolis Housing Agency Board of 
Directors. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis Housing Agency Board of Directors, the Council reappoints:  
 
Sherry Seiwert 
 
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending February 28, 2026.  The  person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term,  the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed  the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 17th day of October, 2022, at 7:21 p.m.

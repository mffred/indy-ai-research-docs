CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 13, 2023
Proposal No. 289, 2023

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 18, 2022) by an additional appropriation of One Million Three Hundred Thousand dollars ($1,300,000) for the purposes of the Indianapolis Fire Department.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2023 is hereby amended by the fund and character increase hereinafter stated for purposes of the Indianapolis Fire Department.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis Fire Department requests an additional appropriation of four hundred twenty- five thousand dollars ($425,000) in character one and eight hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($875,000 in character three in the Federal Grants fund to support expenses incurred during the deployment of Indiana
Task Force One in response to the Maui Fire deployments.

SECTION 4. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does not intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the appropriation for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or project, or both, and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city-county council immediately upon receipt of any information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.     , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the 130th Anniversary of the Indianapolis 
Recorder Newspaper.   
 
WHEREAS, the Indianapolis Recorder Newspaper, founded in 1895 by visionary publisher George P. 
Stewart, has served as a steadfast voice for Indiana’s Black community for 130 years, making it one of  the longest -running African American newspapers in the United States and a cornerstone of Indiana’s  journalistic and cultural landscape; and 
 
WHEREAS, born during an era when mainstream media often excluded or misrepresented Black  voices, the Recorder was established to inform, uplift, and advocate for African Americans in Indianapolis  and across the state, providing essential coverage of civil rights, education, business, faith, and everyday  life from the Black perspective; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Recorder’s historic stewardship has passed through the dedicated leadership of  notable owners including Marcus C. Stewart, Eunice Trotter, and William G. Mays, each of whom made  lasting contributions to preserving the publication's integrity and extending its impact; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Recorder has also benefited from the leadership of distinguished past presidents 
Charles Blair, Carolene Mays, and Shannon Williams, who played key roles in guiding the publication  through critical times of transition, growth, and innovation; and 
 
WHEREAS, throughout its long and distinguished history, the Indianapolis Recorder  has chronicled  both local and national movements —from the Great Migration and Jim Crow era to the Civil Rights 
Movement, modern -day calls for police reform, and today’s ongoing struggles for social, racial, and  economic justice; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Recorder has remained resilient through wars, economic downturns, and shifting  media landscapes, evolving with the times by embracing digital platforms, expanding its multimedia  reach, and serving as a training ground for Black journalists and storytellers committed to truth and  equity; and 
 
WHEREAS, in addition to its award -winning journalism, the Recorder has created a profound civic  impact through programs and initiatives that engage the community, including voter registration drives,  public health education, youth empowerment projects, and economic development partnerships; and

WHEREAS, the Recorder’s commitment to journalistic excellence has been recognized nationally and  locally, having earned over 100 awards in the past six years alone from esteemed institutions such as the 
Hoosier State Press Association, the Society of Professional Journalists, and the National Newspaper 
Publishers Association; and 
 
WHEREAS, the newspaper’s signature Champions of Diversity Awards , now in its 18th year, has  grown to become one of Indiana’s largest annual celebrations of equity and inclusion, honoring  individuals and institutions that are creating lasting change in business, education, healthcare, and civic  life; and 
 
WHEREAS, under the current leadership of Robert Shegog, President and CEO of Recorder Media 
Group—which includes the Indianapolis Recorder Newspaper and Indiana Minority Business Magazine — the Recorder continues to thrive as a multimedia force through its affiliated platforms, while investing in  investigative journalism, community engagement, and content that preserves and protects Black history;  and 
 
WHEREAS, the paper’s enduring motto —“Our News, Your Voice” —embodies its commitment to  inclusive storytelling, cultural pride, and speaking truth to power, especially on behalf of underrepresented  and marginalized communities; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City -County Council of Indianapolis and Marion County recognizes the Indianapolis 
Recorder not just as a publication but as a movement—one that continues to shape the conscience of our  city and state through fearless reporting, civic service, and an unwavering belief in the power of  information to transform lives; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City -County Council recognizes and honor s the Indianapolis Recorder’s   powerful legacy and commends its continued impact on the community.  
 
SECTION 2. The Council pledges their support as the Recorder enters its next chapter, equipped to  inform, inspire, and lead the community for generations to come. 
 
SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.  
 
SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-4- 14.

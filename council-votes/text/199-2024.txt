A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Abbe Hohman to the indianapolis Public Transportation Corporation (IndyGo) Board of Directors.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis Public Transportation Corporation (IndyGo) Board of
Directors, the Council appoints:

The person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Coun Upon expiration of the term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Barato Britt to the Board of Business and Neighborhood Services. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Business and Neighborhood Services, the Council reappoints:

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2023.  The person  appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term, the  appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a perio d not to exceed the  holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5th day of December, 2022, at 7:31 p.m.

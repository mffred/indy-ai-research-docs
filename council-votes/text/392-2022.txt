CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 25, 2022
Proposal No. 392, 2022

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2022 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 22, 2021) by an additional appropriation of eighty-five thousand two hundred ten dollars ($85,210) and the transfer of appropriations totaling eighty-nine thousand dollars ($89,000) for the purposes of the Marion
County Recorder.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2022 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Recorder.

SECTION 2. The Marion County Recorder requests an additional appropriation totaling eighty-five thousand two hundred ten dollars ($85,210) in character three and a transfer of appropriations totaling eighty-nine thousand dollars ($89,000) in the County Records Perpetuation Fund from character one and character four to character three for the purposes of financing temporary staffing contractual costs:

Where Beech Grove's money goes, 2020 to 2027

Beech Grove's Common Council passed the 2027 budget on October 19, 2026, 5 to 1, at roughly $19.6 million. The final adopted order isn't posted yet, so 2027 figures here are the advertised budget filed with the state in September: $19.69 million, about 32% more than 2020. The history comes from the city's own budget ordinances, budget forms and September 2024 fiscal plan. It covers the civil city (police, fire contract, EMS, streets, parks, City Hall). The sewer, stormwater and TIF funds are budgeted separately.

    Reading the numbers

      Notes on this data

      • Advertised, not final. The 2027 Form 1 "Adopted" column is a copy of "Advertised" because the forms were generated on September 22, before the vote. Mirror Indy reports the final budget as $19.6 million; the advertised total is $19,693,139. We'll update when the city posts the adopted order (Form 4).
      • Checked against the city's own totals. Every year's function totals add up to the total printed on that year's ordinance, department budgets add up to each year's General Fund, and the 208 line items for 2027 add up to each fund's total on Form 1. Form 3 shows the lease rental fund at $1,074,000 where Form 1 shows $1,077,000.
      • Scanned ordinances. The 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026 ordinances are scans, read with OCR. Where OCR dropped a number (2023 police pension, road, highway and training funds), it was filled in from the ordinance's own printed total and cross-checked against the fiscal plan. The 2022 ordinance lists no fire pension appropriation.
      • Mixed sources by year. Department budgets for 2021 to 2024 come from the fiscal plan, which adds up to each year's adopted General Fund exactly. 2025 and 2026 come from the Council's October 6, 2025 budget summary table (2025 Senior Center set to the adopted total), and 2027 from Form 1. For 2026, the Council cut $606,801 from the $20.05 million presented on September 2, 2025, to reach $19,440,824.
      • The city's "2026 Budget Ordinance" link is actually 2025 (adopted October 21, 2024). The real 2026 ordinance is in the city's archive folder as "Gen Ord 6 2025".
      • Not included: the sewer utility, stormwater and TIF funds, which the reading notes above summarize from the fiscal plan's actuals.
      • AI-parsed. Figures were extracted from PDFs by script and cross-checked as described. Descriptions of what a line "buys" are the city's own labels except where a source is named. The per-resident figure uses the 2020 Census count of 14,717.

Indianapolis City-County Council District 19 · Election turnout

Where District 19 voted in 2023

How many registered voters cast a ballot in each of District 19's 24 precincts in the May 2023 primary and the November 2023 general election, from the Marion County Election Board's precinct results. District 19 covers part of Indianapolis's southeast side in Center and Perry townships, including the city of Beech Grove.

    Notes on this data

    • Official results, as published. All counts come from the Marion County Election Board's precinct-level result files posted on the City of Indianapolis open data portal. Nothing here is a projection or estimate.
    • Registered voters differ slightly between May and November. Each election's turnout uses that election's own registration count.
    • Primary turnout is all ballots. It includes Democratic, Republican and nonpartisan ballots. Indiana primaries are open: voters choose a party's ballot on Election Day without registering by party.
    • Precinct boundaries are the city's current voting precinct map. The 24 precincts together cover exactly the area of City-County Council District 19. The opening map draws the city's major-road centerlines for orientation. Zoomed in, the street map is OpenStreetMap and the aerial photo is the city's spring 2024 flight.
    • Address search uses the City of Indianapolis address locator. It places the pin at the address point the city has on file, which can sit a little off the building.

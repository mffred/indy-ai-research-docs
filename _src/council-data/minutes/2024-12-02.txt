AND

OF 
INDIANAPOLIS, MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
MONDAY, DECEMBER 2, 2024 
 
The City -County Council of Indianapolis, Marion County, Indiana and the Indianapolis Police 
Special Service District Council, Indianapolis Fire Special Service District Council and 
Indianapolis Solid Waste Collection Special Service District Council convened in regular 
concurrent sessions, in the Council Chamber of the City-County Building at 7:06 p.m. on Monday, 
December 2, 2024, with Councilor Osili presiding. 
 
Councilor Jones introduced Pastor Jed Fuller, director of Impact Old Southside, a ministry of Mt. 
Pleasant Christian Church, who led the opening prayer.  Councilor Jones then invited all to join her 
in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag. 
 
ROLL CALL 
 
The President instructed the Clerk to take the roll call and requested members to register their 
presence on the voting machine.  The roll call was as follows: 
 
22 YEAS: Allen, Bain, Barth, Boots, Brown-J, Cahill, Carlino, Delaney, Dilk, Gibson, Graves, 
Hart, Jones, Lewis, Mascari, McCormick, Mowery, Nielsen, Osili, Perkins, Roberts, Robinson 
3 ABSENT:  Annee, Brown-A, Evans

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 86, 2024 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A COUNCIL RESOLUTION establishing a study commission to evaluate intellectual and 
developmental disability service providers and funding allocations in Marion County. 
WHEREAS, the Consolidated City of Indianapolis believes in empowering individuals with disabilities and their 
families; and 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis and Marion County have provided public support to service providers for decades through 
various funding streams, including through the authority of Indiana Code 12-29 et seq.; and 
WHEREAS, it is the Council’s opinion that future funding allocations for public support should be made based on 
shared, measurable outcomes; now, therefore:  
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY , INDIANA: 
 
Section 1. Purpose:  A study commission is hereby established under the authority of the council to review and analyze 
the historical use of funds collected under Indiana Code 12-29 et seq., to consult with experts to consider the current 
method of fund distribution, any future fund distribution methodology, and to develop recommendations on how to create 
a fund distribution model that evaluates nonprofit intellectual and developmental disability (I/DD) service providers and 
funding allocations in Marion County.  The commission shall hold public meetings and report its findings and 
recommendations to the full council.  Public comment shall be allowed under any rules devised by the Commission. 
 
Section 2. Name:  The commission shall be named the “Commission on Nonprofit Intellectual and Development 
Disability Provider Support” (Commission).” 
 
Section 3. Scope: The Commission shall consider the following in carrying out its purpose:  
(1) best practices and recommendations from industry experts (e.g. the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality 
(AHRQ) and the National Quality Forum); and

(4) methods to equitably meet the needs of individuals with an I/DD in Marion County; and

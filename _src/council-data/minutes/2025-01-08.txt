 MINUTES OF THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL 
AND 
SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT COUNCILS 
OF 
INDIANAPOLIS, MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 8, 2025 
 
The City -County Council of Indianapolis, Marion County, Indiana and the Indianapolis Police 
Special Service District Council, Indianapolis Fire Special Service District Council and 
Indianapolis Solid Waste Collection Special Service District Council convened in regular 
concurrent sessions , in the Council Chamber of the City -County Building at  7:12 p.m. on 
Wednesday, January 8, 2025, with Councilor Osili presiding. 
 
Councilor Lewis introduced Pastor James Jackson Fervent Prayer Church, who led the opening 
prayer.  Councilor Lewis then invited all to join her in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag. 
 
ORGANIZATION OF CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL 
 
Selection of Temporary Presiding Officer 
 
Councilor Osili asked for consent to appoint General Counsel LeAnnette Pierce as temporary 
presiding officer for purposes of elections.  Unanimous consent was given. 
 
Election of Officers 
 
Ms. Pierce opened the floor for nominations for President of the Council.  Councilor McCormick 
nominated Councilor Osili for President.  Councilor Graves seconded the nomination.  Councilor 
Lewis moved, seconded by Councilor Carlino, to close nominations.  Nominations were closed by 
unanimous consent, thereby electing Councilor Osili as President.  President Osili stated that he 
looks forward to continuing to serve and work with the members of this body in the coming year.   
 
Ms. Pierce opened the floor for nominations for Vice- President of the Council.  Councilor Nick 
Roberts nominated Councilor A. Brown for Vice -President.  Councilor Graves seconded the 
nomination.  Councilor McCormick moved, seconded by Councilor Graves, to close nominations.  
The motion carried by a vote of 18- 6, and Councilor A. Brown was thereby elected as Vice 
President.  Councilor A. Brown thanked her colleagues for their faith in her and promised to do her 
best for the City of Indianapolis. 
 
Ms. Pierce opened the floor for nominations for Clerk of the Council.  Councilor McCormick 
nominated Yulonda Winfield as Clerk of the Council.  Councilor Graves seconded the nomination.  
Councilor Carlino moved, seconded by Councilor Graves, to close nominations.  Nominations were 
closed by unanimous consent, thereby electing Yulonda Winfield as Clerk of the Council.   
 
Ms. Pierce passed the gavel to newly elected President Osili.

addressing and remedying those underlying conditions; and

unconstitutional conditions; and

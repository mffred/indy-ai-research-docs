CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 35, 2023 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Indiana Union Construction Industry and their recruitment arm, the Indiana 
Careers in Construction Association. 
 
WHEREAS, the week of November 13th – 19th, 2023 the country celebrates National Apprenticeship Week 
recognizing the value of skilled training to workforce and economic development, we recognize the impact made by the 
Indiana Union Construction Industry and their recruitment arm, the Indiana Careers in Construction Association, 
throughout Indiana; and 
  
WHEREAS, by providing skilled apprenticeship training programs to Hoosiers statewide, the Indiana Union 
Construction Industry and the Indiana Careers in Construction Association are providing opportunities for residents of 
Indianapolis, Marion County and the surrounding areas to improve their skills, careers, and their lives.  These registered 
apprentice training programs are an asset to our community and our state; and 
  
WHEREAS, there are currently more than 75,000 skilled trades men and women in the Indiana Union Construction 
Industry; and 
 
WHEREAS, there are over 8,200 apprentices enrolled in union construction apprenticeship programs in the Indiana 
Union Construction Industry, consisting of 20% minorities, 9% veterans, and 6% females; and 
 
WHEREAS, 71% of active apprentices in Indiana’s construction industry receive their training from a Union 
training program; and 
 
WHEREAS, these skilled tradespersons are essential for successful completion of public works and private 
construction projects that elevate our communities; and 
 
WHEREAS, each year, the Indiana Union Construction Industry makes a private industry investment of more 
than $54 million dollars in apprenticeship training through their jointly managed apprenticeship programs which 
represents 85% of annual apprentice program investments throughout the State; and 
  
WHEREAS, since 1993, more than 21,500 apprentices from the Indiana Union Construction Industry have earned 
an Associate’s Degree in Applied Science from Ivy Tech Community College as a direct result of their enrollment in a 
union construction apprenticeship programs.  This two-year degree is earned at no additional cost to the student; now, 
therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Indiana Union Construction Industry and 
their recruitment arm, the Indiana Careers in Construction Association. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Council heartily congratulates the Indiana Union Construction Industry and the Indiana Careers in 
Construction Association and wishes them continued success.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 36, 2023 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the career and contributions of Marion County Surveyor, Debra S. Jenkins. 
 
WHEREAS, Debra S. Jenkins has served in local government for more than 40 years including time spent working 
in the Marion County Superior Courts as a Court Reporter and time spent working in the Marion County Recorder’s 
office as the Chief Deputy Recorder and; and 
 
WHEREAS, since her election in 2008, Ms Jenkins has served the Marion County residents as the County Surveyor, 
an Indiana Constitutional Office first filled in 1819; and 
 
WHEREAS, since being elected, she has implemented various strategies and policies to increase the effectiveness 
of the office, such as digitizing the 2400 section corners in the county, improving and expanding the digitization of other 
documents including, but not limited to, subdivision maps, subdivision plats, railway valuation maps, and legal surveys; 
and 
 
WHEREAS, this digitization of office records has resulted in a substantial ease and efficiencies in storing and 
sharing these records via email or disc; and   
 
WHEREAS, her office works  closely with the Marion County Sheriff’s office on determining the actual distance 
between schools and the work or home locations of sex offenders, works with the State Alcohol and Tobacco Commission 
to verify liquor license locations, and provides an annual average of 50 no-cost surveying projects to other government 
departments and agencies; and 
 
WHEREAS, Ms Jenkins guidance has enabled the office to be a more cost-effective size, saving county tax dollars; 
and  
 
WHEREAS, when encountering the problem of attracting and retaining employees only to have employees leave 
within months of being trained to go to the private sector or other higher paying jobs, Ms Jenkins, working with City HR, 
the Office of Corporation Counsel, and the  Controller’s Office, implemented the Higher Education and Longevity or 
HELP Program where employees can earn surveyor certifications by taking hybrid classes from Vincennes University 
over the course of 6 -8 years at no cost to the employees if they stay an additional 2 years; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Association of Indiana Counties awarded Marion County the 2023 County Achievement Award 
for the Surveyor’s HELP program; and 
 
WHEREAS, in addition to the HELP program, Ms Jenkins has made it a point to introduce the  career of land 
surveying to high school students, by hiring them in the summer, and provide early career  employment opportunities to 
college students studying land surveying, now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes of Marion County Surveyor, Debra Jenkins for 
her years of dedicated public service.

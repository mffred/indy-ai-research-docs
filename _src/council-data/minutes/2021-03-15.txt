paying interest, they can loan the money to themselves, without interest, and pay it back ahead of

Councillor Mowery moved the  adjournment of this meeting of the Indianapolis City -County 
Council in recognition of and respect for the life and contributions of Matthew Lingenfelter. Becky 
Davis, Thomas Edward Risch, Rev. Dr. Richard E. Hamilton, Mary Schaffer, Juliann Bischoff, 
Robert A. Lindemann, Wells P. Martin, Janet Campbell Baker, and N.E. Oakes.  He respectfully 
asked the support of fellow Councillors.  He further requested that the motion be made a part of the 
permanent records of this body and that a letter bearing the Counc il seal and the signature of the 
President be sent to the families advising of this action. 
There being no further business, and upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting adjourned 
at 9:37 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 12, 2025 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring State Senator La Keisha Jackson. 
 
WHEREAS, The Honorable La Keisha Jackson faithfully served the citizens of Indianapolis for over a decade as a 
member of the Indianapolis -Marion County City -County Council, representing the Far Eastside (Districts 14 and 15), 
and worked tirelessly to uplift her community and improve the quality of life for residents; and 
  
WHEREAS, during her Council tenure, Councilor Jackson provided impactful leadership as Chair of the Municipal 
Corporations Committee and as a member of key committees, helping shape policies on development, public safety, and 
finance; and 
  
WHEREAS, Councilor Jackson led transformative efforts on the Far Eastside, including the redevelopment of 
Oaktree Apartments, cleanup of Towne & Terrace, major infrastructure investments, and the expansion and improvement 
of Grassy Creek Park, enhancing access to green space and recreation for local families; and 
  
WHEREAS, a steadfast advocate for equity, she promoted economic inclusion, supported minority -owned 
businesses, expanded affordable housing, and spearheaded anti-violence and food access initiatives; and 
  
WHEREAS, beyond the Council, Jackson has served over 20 years as CEO of Pathway Resource Center, mentoring 
youth, connecting families to resources, and making a lasting impact on over 100,000 residents; and 
  
WHEREAS, in 2024, Jackson was elected to the Indiana State Senate representing District 34, where she continues 
to champion transportation, housing, food security, and economic justice for her constituents; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  That the Indianapolis City -County Council hereby honors and thanks La Keisha Jackson for her 
distinguished service, celebrates her lasting contributions to the city, and extends best wishes for her continued leadership 
in the Indiana State Senate. 
 
SECTION 2.  That a copy of this resolution be presented to Senator Jackson as a token of the Council’s high regard and 
appreciation. 
 
SECTION 3.  The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

Proposal No. 156, 2025 was retitled SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2025, and reads as follows: 
 
CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 13, 2025 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Sacred Heart’s 150th Jubilee year celebration. 
 
WHEREAS, in 1850, Germans were the largest immigrant group in Indianapolis. Initially, German Catholics 
attended Mass at St. John’s, an English -speaking church.  Aware that other German denominations were forming their 
own churches, plans were soon underway for a church where sermons, confessions and devotions could be heard in their 
familiar German; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Bishop approved a new parish site in the Nathan B. Palmer’s Addition, west of Madison and 
Indianapolis railroad tracks and north of Palmer Street. This was a rural location for farmland and was purchased for 
$10,000.  On September 19, 1875, va rious societies and musical units organized at St. Mary’s and marched down 
Meridian Street to the new Sacred Heart, where Benedictine Father Bede O’Connor laid the cornerstone for the new 
building; and 
 
WHEREAS, since 1875, Sacred Heart of Jesus Catholic Church has been central to the life and culture of the 
Old Southside Neighborhood.   Sacred Heart is noteworthy for being the only church in Indianapolis designed by 
Franciscan Brother Adrian Wewer, OFM, a  prolific architect who designed more than 100 Catholic churches between 
1864  and 1914. Brother Wewer continued to design every building that was added to the Sacred Heart campus.   This 
historic Church includes decorative painting, hand -carved woodwork by local artisan GH Hermann, beautifully crafted 
statues and altars with stained glass windows from Franz Mayer of Munich.  Sacred Heart’s twin bell towers ring out the 
hours as an enduring symbol of strength and hope for families with deep roots and all the new residents who are bringing 
a revitalization to the Old Southside; and  
 
WHEREAS, the community of Sacred Heart of Jesus, a Franciscan parish with the power of the Holy spirit, joyfully 
proclaims the Gospel of Jesus Christ through words, action and prayer for the salvation of souls.   Sacred Heart is now 
celebrating 150 years of service to the southside of Indianapolis.   “Celebrating Our Past, Embracing the Present, and 
Building for our Future” will be the guide for the next 150 years; now, therefore. 
  
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Sacred Heart’s 150th Jubilee year celebration, 
which will be held on September 20, 2025. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Council heartily thanks the Sacred Heart Church for their contributions to the City of Indianapolis and 
wishes them the best in their future endeavors.  
 
SECTION 3.  The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto. 
 
SECTION 4.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

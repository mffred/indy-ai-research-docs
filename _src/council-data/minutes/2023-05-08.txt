CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 21, 2023 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION condemning the attacks on young LGBTQIA+ Hoosiers and reaffirming the City of 
Indianapolis’ commitment to their safety and freedom, and proclaiming May 8, 2023, as “Transgender Youth and Family 
Safety Day.” 
 
WHEREAS, the safety and personal freedom of our transgender youth and their families are under attack; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis values and recognizes the LGBTQIA+ community and will not support any 
efforts to compromise the welfare and safety of our LGBTQIA+ youth and their families; and 
 
WHEREAS, affirmation of their gender identity and freedom of expression are vital  to how children and teens see 
themselves and their ability to thrive; and 
 
WHEREAS, family support and professional care reduce suicide attempts by LGBTQIA+ youth by 40%; and 
 
WHEREAS, it is critical for families and transgender youth to have safe access to resources, medical care, and 
professional guidance to ensure a positive quality of life; and 
 
WHEREAS, the top major medical healthcare organizations support peer -reviewed gender -affirming care and 
oppose legislation that will hinder medical care for transgender youth; and 
 
WHEREAS, actions to the contrary are likely to increase rejection and target harassment and hate crimes against 
transgender, nonbinary, and intersex youth; and 
 
WHEREAS, dismissing the needs of transgender youth and families is fundam entally negligent and poses a 
significant risk that may have harmful and dangerous physical and mental health consequences; and 
 
WHEREAS, anti-trans bills are a gross misrepresentation of medical knowledge, mental healthcare, and science, 
and tend to erode the rights of families and LGBTQIA+ youth statewide; and

solidarity with our LGBTQIA+ community, including actions taken by the City-County Council to lobby against any 
legislation that would diminish the City’s ability to protect civil rights, threaten its welcoming business environment, or 
tarnish its status as an inclusive community; and

CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 15, 2023 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to authorize the city controller to make a public purpose grant to the 
Arts Council of Indianapolis for the public art grants program pursuant to Revised Code Sec. 271-106.  
 
WHEREAS, the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022), Section 4.01(c), 
provides that any expenditures in 2023 for public purpose grants other than those enumerated therein must receive Council 
approval by a resolution that identifies the amount and recipient of the grant; and  
 
WHEREAS, Revised Code Section 271-106 provides that the Council may appropriate money from the public art 
for neighborhoods fund for the purpose of awarding sub-grants for public art and public space beautification through the 
public art grants program, and that such program shall be administered by the Arts Council of Indianapolis;  
 
WHEREAS, the Council desires to make a public purpose local grant for the public art grants program in compliance 
with Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022 and Revised Code Sec. 271-106; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1.  The City-County Council hereby finds that a public purpose grant for the support of the public arts grant 
program, as established by Rev. Code Sec. 271 -106, is necessary and desirable in the public  interest and is in 
furtherance of a purpose  for which the controller is otherwise authorized to expend public moneys.   
 
SECTION 2.  The City-County Council hereby authorizes the city controller to make a public purpose local grant to the 
Arts Council of Indianapolis, for purposes of the public arts program, in the amount of One Hundred Seven Thousand 
Six Hundred Fifty dollars ($107,650). Such grant shall be in addition to, and authorized on the same basis as, the public 
purpose local grants enumerated in Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022, Section 4.01(c). 
 
SECTION 3.  This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the City -County Council and compliance 
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

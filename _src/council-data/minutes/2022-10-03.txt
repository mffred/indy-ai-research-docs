President Osili called on General Counsel Toae Kim to review the ground rules for public testimony 
as adopted by this body.  Ms. Kim reminded Council members and the public of the ground rules 
for the public comment portion of the agenda.  She said that in order for everyone to have a fair 
chance to speak and be heard, it is important to observe the following rules.  First, each speaker 
will be limited to two minutes.  Second, any public comments must reasonably relate to the agenda 
item under consideration.  Third, speakers who stray from the item under consideration or become 
unduly repetitious may be asked to move on to their next point or conclude their comments.  Finally, 
attendees who cause disruptions that prevent the Council from proceeding through today’s agenda 
in a reasonably efficient manner will be removed.  Ms. Kim added that some types of threatening 
speech or incitement to violence are not protected by the First Amendment and will be dealt with 
if they come up.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 13, 2022 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL ORDINANCE approving an interlocal cooperation agreement between the Consolidated 
City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana (“Indianapolis”) and the City of Greenwood, Indiana (“Greenwood”) for 
the design, construction, and operation of improvements to County Line Road. 
 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis and Greenwood are both municipalities, each of which has, pursuant to I.C. 36-1-3-9(a) , 
exclusive jurisdiction over the bridges, streets, alleys, sidewalks, watercourses, sewers, drains, and public grounds inside 
its corporate boundaries, unless a statute provides otherwise; and 
 
WHEREAS, I.C. § 8-17 -1-45 provides that each county is responsible for the construction, reconstruction,  
maintenance and operation of the roads, including the ditches and signs for those roads, making up its southern and eastern 
boundaries; and 
 
WHEREAS, County Line Road is the boundary road between Marion County and Johnson County, and it is Marion 
County’s southern boundary road; therefore, the Indianapolis Department of Public Works (“DPW”) is the agency with 
responsibility for the construction, reconstruction, maintenance and operation of County Line Road; and 
 
WHEREAS, DPW has prepared plans and specifications for two capital improvement projects which will require 
DPW to acquire right-of-way and to conduct construction activities within the jurisdiction of Greenwood: (1) improvements 
to West County Line Road to accommodate additional  traffic resulting from the construction of an interchange for 
Interstate 69 at West County Line  Road by the Indiana Department of Transportation; and (2) improvements to South 
Emerson Avenue between Stop 11 Road and East County Line Road; and 
 
WH
EREAS, both Indianapolis and Greenwood have corporate power as municipalities to acquire and own interests 
in real property pursuant to I.C. § 36-1-4-5, and to use and improve interests in real property pursuant to I.C. § 36-1-4-6; 
and 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to I.C. § 36-1-7-2, a power that may be exercised by a political subdivision and by one or more 
other governmental entities may be exercised: (1) by one or more entities on behalf of others, or (2) jointly by the entities, 
if the entities enter into a written interlocal cooperation agreement approved by the entities’ fiscal bodies by ordinance or 
resolution; and 
 
WHEREAS, accordingly, Indianapolis and Greenwood desire to enter into an interlocal cooperation  agreement to 
allow DPW to carry out the Project; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City-County Council, having considered the matter and being duly advised, finds that West County Line 
Road and South Emerson Avenue each serve and affect the citizens of and the economic well-being of both Indianapolis and 
Greenwood, that the two projects will improve safety and mobility, and will accommodate future economic growth in the 
region, and that it will be of public utility and benefit to enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement with Greenwood to 
carry out the Project; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annuai Budget for 2025 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 6, 2024) by additional appropriations totaling three million dollars ($3,000,000) for the
purposes of the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increase hereinafter stated for
purposes of the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services.

SECTION 2. The Department_of Business and Neighborhood Services requests additional appropriations
totaling three million dollars ($3,000,000) in the Capital Asset Lifecycle and Development Fund in character
three for the purposes of funding the costs associated with improving local alleyways.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 9, 2023
Proposal No. 153, 2023

A SPECIAL ORDINANCE of the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana amending a special
ordinance authorizing the issuance of one or more series of its City of Indianapolis, Indiana Economic
Development Tax Increment Revenue Bonds, Series 202_ (Motto Hotel Project).

WHEREAS, on May 2, 2022, the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “Council’)
adopted its Special Ordinance No. 5, 2022 (the “Bond Ordinance”) approving, among other matters, the
issuance by the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) of its taxable or tax-exempt economic development
tax increment revenue bonds in one or more series (collectively, the “Bonds”) to finance and/or reimburse
a portion of the costs of acquiring, renovating and rehabilitating the vacant King Cole Building located at 1
N. Meridian Street in the City for reuse as a hotel development, consisting of but not limited fo: (i) an
approximately 116-unit Motto Hotel, (ii) multiple full-service restaurants and bars, including a rooftop and
lobby café, and (iii) other amenities including public art, totaling an approximately 47,000 square foot
development, together with all necessary appurtenances, related improvements and equipment, and all
incidental expenses in connection therewith (collectively the “Project”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 3 of the Bond Ordinance, the Council authorized the Bonds for the
Project to bear interest at an interest rate not exceeding six and one-half percent (6.5%) per annum; and

WHEREAS, the Council has been advised by its staff and municipal advisor that as a result of recent
changes in the financial market conditions It should authorize an increase in the maximum interest rate on
the Bonds to eight percent (8.0%) per annum, and

WHEREAS, the Council having considered the matter finds that the Bond Ordinance should be so
amended; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The reference to the not to exceed interest rate in Section 3 of the Bond Ordinance is hereby
amended to reflect a maximum interest rate not to exceed eight percent (8.0%) per annum.

SECTION 2. The Council hereby approves a corresponding change in the not to exceed interest rate
reflected in the Financing Documents (as defined and referenced in the Bond Ordinance).

SECTION 3. This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon compliance with Indiana Code 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10 day of July, 2023, at 8:17 p.m.

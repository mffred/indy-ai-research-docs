CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2025
Proposal No. 392, 2024

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION authorizing the payment of legal fees and litigation costs by
the Marion County Sheriff's Office ("MCSO”) from the Jail Commissary Fund.

WHEREAS, in 1972 the inmates of the Marion County Jail sued the Sheriff of Marion County in federal
court for the unconstitutional conditions of the Marion County Jail, Cause IP-72-04246-C-B/S, U.S. District
Court, Southern District of Indiana, Indianapolis Di Sand

WHEREAS, when Frank J. Anderson was elected Sheriff in 2002 he made a commitment to the citizens
of Marion County to resolve all pending litigation over unconstitutional conditions within the Marion County
Jail by fully addressing and remedying those underlying conditions; and

WHEREAS, upon taking office on January 1, 2003, Sheriff Anderson discovered numerous other
lawsuits by individual inmates, filed under 42 U.S. Code §1983, alleging deprivation of federal civil rights
because of the Jail’s unconstitutional conditions; and

WHEREAS, Sheriff Anderson promptly appointed a new Legal Deputy and a new law firm to address
the many lawsuits facing the MCSO, and also requested that the City-County Council adopt a Resolution
authorizing payment of the Sheriff's Office legal fees and litigation costs using the Jail Commissary Fund;
and

WHEREAS, IC 36-8-10-21(d)(9) authorizes expenditures from the commissary fund for any “purpose that
benefits the sheriffs department that is mutually ‘agreed upon by the county fiscal body and the county sheriff’;
and :

WHEREAS, the legal expenses incurred in are a purpose for which expenditures are authorized from the
Jail commissary fund; and

WHEREAS, as is required by IC 36-8-10-21(d), the expenditure of such funds are supplemental or in
addition to, rather than a replacement for, regular appropriations previously made; and

WHEREAS, Special Resolution No. 77, 2003, (Proposal 567, 2003), was adopted on October 27, 2003,
and since that time MCSO legal fees and litigation costs have been borne by the Commissary Fund, rather
than by the Sheriffs Office regular appropriation; and

WHEREAS, since 2003, the MCSO has semi-annually provided a copy of the Commissary Fund
reports to the City-County Council detailing the legal fees and litigation costs incurred by the MCSO; and

WHEREAS, the FCC issued a ruling in July 2024 that will severely limit funds to be deposited within
the Sheriffs Office Commissary Fund, and thereby reduce the MCSO’s ability to fund legal fees and
litigation expenses outside its regular appropriation; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council approves continued funding of the MCSO legal fees and litigation
costs from the Commissary Fund, and recognizes that in the future such legal fees and litigation expenses
may need to be a part of the Sheriff's Office regular appropriation; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council, pursuant to I.C. 36-8-10-21, hereby agrees to the expenditure of
funds from the Jail Commissary Fund to pay the legal fees and litigation expenses incurred by the Marion
County Sheriff.

SECTION 2. The Marion County Sheriff shall maintain a record of the Commissary Fund’s receipts and
disbursements and shall semi-annually continue to provide a copy of the record of receipts and
disbursements to the City County Council, as required by I.C. 36-8-10-10-21(e).

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC §36-3-4-14.
GENERAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025

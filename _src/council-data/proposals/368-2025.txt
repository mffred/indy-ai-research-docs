DIGEST:  approves a transfer of $1,000,000 from the Department of Public Works (Stormwater 
Management Fund) to the Department of Parks and Recreation (Parks General Fund) to isolate funds for

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.    , 2025 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City‑County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 6, 2024) by a transfer of appropriation of one million dollars ($1,000,000) for the purposes of the 
Department of Public Works and the Department of Parks and Recreation. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY‑COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City -
County- Annual Budget for 2025 is hereby amended by the character increases and decreases hereinafter 
stated for purposes of the Department of Public Works and the Department of Parks and Recreation. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Department of Public Works requests a reduction in appropriation of one million dollars 
($1,000,000) in the Storm Water Management Fund in character four for the purpose of urban forestry 
preservation: 
 
FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL 
Storm Water 
Management      -1,000,000   -1,000,000 
 
SECTION 3.  The Department of Parks and Recreation  requests an additional appropriation totaling one 
million dollars ($1,000,000) in the Parks General Fund in character four for the purpose outlined in Section 
2: 
 
FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL 
Parks General      1,000,000   1,000,000 
 
SECTION 4.  In support of the appropriations requested in Section 3, funds on deposit totaling one million 
dollars ($1,000,000) in the Storm Water Management Fund, as originally funded via supplemental income 
taxes in City-County Fiscal Ordinance No. 8, 2025, will be transferred to the Parks General Fund.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 66, 2023
Proposal No. 232, 2023

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the appointment of the Health and Hospital Corporation's Board of
Trustee’s nomination of Virginia A. Caine, M.D. as the Director of the Division of Public Health.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Indiana Code §16-22-8-30(b), the Health and Hospital Corporation Board of
Trustee’s appointment of the Director of the Division of Public Health is subject to the approval of the City-
County Council; and

WHEREAS, at its July 25, 2023, meeting, the Health and Hospital Corporation’s Board of Trustees
reappointed Virginia A. Caine, M.D. as the Director of the Public Health Division; and

WHEREAS, the Health and Hospital Corporation’s Board of Trustees has submitted to this Council
the name of Virginia A. Caine, M.D. to continue serving as Director of the Division of Public Health; now,
therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CHTY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The reappointment of Virginia A. Caine, M.D, is hereby approved by the City-County Council
to serve as Director of the Division of Public Health.

SECTION 2. This resolution shail be in effect from and after its adoption by the Council and compliance
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 11 day of September, 2023, at 7:27 p.m.

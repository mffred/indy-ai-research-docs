CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 10, 2023
Proposal No. 226, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022) by appropriating a total of three million six hundred thousand dollars
($3,600,000) for the purposes of the Department of Public Works.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2023 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for
purposes of the Department of Public Works.

SECTION 2. The Department of Public Works requests an additional appropriation totaling three million six
hundred thousand dollars ($3,600,000) in character four in the Federal Grants Fund to afford design costs
related to the projects awarded under the Federal Rebuilding American Infrastructure with Sustainability and
Equity (RAISE) Grant Program.

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 | CHARS5 TOTAL
Federal Grants 3,600,000 3,600,000

SECTION 3. Upon approval of this and other pending proposals, the following unappropriated fund balances
are projected to remain at the end of 2023:

Fund Projected 2022 Year-End Balance Projected 2023 Year-End Balance
Federal Grants 0 0

SECTION 4. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does not
intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the appropriation
for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or project, or both,
and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city-county council immediately upon receipt of any
information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shail be in full force and effect upon-adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14 day of August, 2023, at 8:16 p.m.

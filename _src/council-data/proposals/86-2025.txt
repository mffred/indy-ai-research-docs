 I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Interim Current Planning Administrator and

newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan 
Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance 
amendment prayed for in petition: 
 
2024-ZON-138  7947 Camby Road (Approximate Address)

 R.O. No. 86, 2025 Arbor Homes, by Domonic Dreyer

2024-ZON-138 to the City-County Council.

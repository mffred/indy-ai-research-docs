CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2023
Proposal No. 2, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve amendments to the lease between the City of
Indianapolis and the City Market Corporation, revising the footprint of the leased Premises, allowing
development of the East Plaza, and generating income to support City Market operations.

WHEREAS, Indianapolis Revised Code section 285-203 requires the City of Indianapolis to from time
to time negotiate lease terms with the City Market Corporation subject to approval by the City-County
Council; and

WHEREAS, the City has negotiated an amendment to the City Market Corporation’s lease that revises
the footprint of the leased Premises and generates income to support City Market operations; and

WHEREAS, this amendment is also necessary to facilitate the development of the plaza to the east of
the historic market house building; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The lease amendment identified as Exhibit A to this proposal is in all respects approved and
authorized by the City-County Council consistent with Indianapolis Revised Code section 285-203.

SECTION 2. The Department of Metropolitan Development and Office of Finance and Management are
authorized to include the technical description of the east plaza in Exhibit A to the lease amendment upon
receipt of corresponding information from a parcel survey.

SECTION 3. The east plaza is being removed from the City Market Corporation’s lease through the
amendment approved in Sections 1 and 2 of this Proposal. The Department of Metropolitan Development
and Office of Finance and Management are authorized to convey that east plaza by inter-governmental
lease or deed, for no consideration and on such terms as they shall mutually agree, to the Metropolitan
Development Commission for redevelopment purposes consistent with Indiana Code chapter 36-7-15.1.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code sections 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15, and 36-3-4-16.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6" day of February, 2023, at 8:16 p.m.

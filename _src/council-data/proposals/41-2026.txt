CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION 2026 
Proposal No. 41, 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to direct the Rules and Public Policy Committee to review 
and prepare a report pertaining to the fiscal impact statement associated with the City of 
Indianapolis/Marion County union agreement with the American Federation of State, County, and Municipal 
Employees (AFSCME 962).  
 
 WHEREAS, Indianapolis Revised Code section 291- 610 requires that once the City of Indianapolis 
reaches a tentative agreement with a bargaining agent it reduce that agreement to writing; and  
 
 WHEREAS, the same section of the Revised Code states that the agreement shall not be effective until 
a fiscal impact statement, prepared by the controller, is submitted to the city -county council and reviewed 
at a public hearing held by the rules and public policy committee; and 
 
 WHEREAS, this committee is charged by that ordinance with producing a report after holding the public 
hearing; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the ordinance requires no further action be taken by the full council after the production of 
the committee report on the fiscal impact statement; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1.  The rules and public policy committee is directed to review the fiscal impact statement, 
attached hereto as Exhibit A, associated with the tentative union agreement between the City of 
Indianapolis/Marion County and the American Federation of State, County, and Municipal Employees 
(AFSCME 962). 
 
SECTION 2. The rules and public policy committee is further directed to hold a public hearing on the matter 
and prepare a report as required by Revised Code Section 291-610. 
 
SECTION 3. The rules and public policy committee is directed to provide a copy of that report to the clerk 
to have available for public review and to the mayor or the elected official with ultimate supervisory 
responsibility for the employees in the bargaining unit. 
 
SECTION 4. It is acknowledged and recognized that Revised Code Section 291 -610 requires no further 
action by the full council. 
 
Said report was received by the City-County Council this 2nd day of March, 2026, at 7:35 p.m.

CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
             
I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of Metropolitan 
Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on October 20, 2021, a public hearing as required by law pursuant to 
required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and Commercial 
Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan Development 
Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment prayed for in petition:  
 
2021-ZON-042 3610 NORTH SHADELAND AVENUE (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
(AMENDED)   WARREN TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #13 
Prop No. 363, 2021 PEARL GROUP LLC, by Pat Rooney 
R.O. No. 114, 2021 Rezoning of 1.47 acres from the C-4 district to the C-5 district.  
 
2021-ZON-102 1409, 1413, 1417, 1421 AND 1429 EAST MICHIGAN STREET 
Prop No. 364, 2021 (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
R.O. No. 115, 2021 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #17 
BEHC LLC, CHRISTOPHER BLANTON, ECONOMIC INSIGHT LLC, AND 
TWO CHICKS AND A HAMMER INC., by David Kingen and Emily Duncan 
Rezoning of 0.72 acre from the MU-1 district to the D-8 district. 
 
2021-ZON-103 145 WEST 22ND STREET (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 365, 2021 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #11 
R.O. No. 116, 2021 SG MANAGEMENT LLC, by David Kingen and Emily Duncan 
Rezoning of 0.40 acre from the HD-2 (W-5) district to the D-8 (W-5) district. 
 
2021-CZN-840 400, 402, 405, 425 BIXLER ROAD AND 4700 AND 4804 SOUTH 
Prop No. 366, 2021 EAST STREET (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
R.O. No. 117, 2021 PERRY TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #16 
 PEDCOR INVESTMENTS, by Brain J. Tuohy 
 Rezoning of 19.59 acres from the D-A and SU-1 districts to the D-6II 
district to provide for multi-family development. 
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance prayed for in said 
above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, by 
a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended 2021-ZON-042 (Amended), 2021-
ZON-102, 2021-ZON-103, and 2021-CZN-840 to the City-County Council for adoption to the end that 
adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety from fire, flood, and other danger may be 
secured, that property values may be preserved and that the public health, comfort, morals and 
convenience and general welfare may be preserved.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 8, 2023
Proposal No. 53, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Dennis Goins for his dedication to education and serving his
community.

WHEREAS, Dennis Joseph Goins grew up in a family of nine children in Rushville, Indiana, where his
father and mother stressed faith in God, family, education, and developing a tremendous work ethic; and

WHEREAS, Dennis graduated from Rushville High School in 1979. As a freshman, he started for the
Lions’ state runner up team. During his senior year, the Lions went 17-9, and Goins averaged 13 points, 9
assists, and 7 rebounds a game, earning All Sectional, Regional, All-Conference honors and was named
an indiana All-Star. Goins’ 1,080 points ranks an all-time fourth in Rushville History, and his 539 assists is
first on the list. He was also a four-year starter on Rushville’s football team: where he was All-State his
senior season; and

WHEREAS, Dennis received All-State honors in football and basketball, where he earned athletic
scholarships to attend Vincennes University and SiU-Carbondale to play college basketball. He later
graduated from Ball State University and served 20 years as an award-winning photojournalist, covering
events from Hurricane Andrew to the Sydney, Australia Summer Olympics, even making a guest
appearance on “The Late Show with David Letterman.” Mr. Goins stepped out of the television industry by
faith and created a television curriculum from scratch at the Ben Davis High School/Area 31 Career Center;
and

WHEREAS, for the past 18 years, Dennis has served as the Director of BDTV and the Performing Arts
Department Chair, developing relationships, servant leadership, and student-scholars demonstrating
amazing successes locally and globally; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Goins received his Master of Education and Principal License from Indiana Wesleyan
University, and has earned the Wayne Township Extra Mile Award, 11-time Senior Choice Academic
Award, M.G. Raby Distinguished Service Award, and the Claes Nobel Educator of Distinction Award from
the National Society of High School Scholars. In 2019, Mr. Goins received Congressional Recognition for
the African American Excellence in Education Award and The College Mentor for Kids Inspire Award. This
award was for the Education, Government, and Non-Profit Mentor of the Year recognition. He also was
elected to the Indiana Basketball Hall of Fame-Silver Anniversary team, winner of the Vincennes University
Pathfinder Alumni Award and a member of the Ball State Telecommunications Hall of Fame; and

WHEREAS, in 2016, Mr. Goins was named a Top 3 Finalist for the Indiana Teacher of the Year Award
and from 2017 to 2019, he served on the Indiana Department of Education’s Teacher Advisory Council.
Mr. Goins is not only dedicated to educating students and serving his community, but he is also dedicated
to his family: Desiree (wife), Jordyn (daughter), Joseph Dennis (deceased son), Benjamin (son), Emersyn

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Dennis Goins for his dedication to
education and innovation in creating specialized curriculum.

SECTION 2. The Council congratulates Dennis Goins for his many accomplishments and heartily thanks
him for his dedication to educating students and serving his community and the City of Indianap

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
44.

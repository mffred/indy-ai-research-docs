CITY-COUNTY GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 4, 2023
Proposal No. 5, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE to amend the Revised Code to clarify the definition of City
Market consistent with plans to redevelop the plaza to the east of the historic market house and to make
related changes.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Sections 285-201, 285-203 and 285-204 of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and
County,” regarding the City Market, hereby are amended by the deletion of the language that is stricken
and the addition of the language that is underscored, to read as follows:

Sec. 285-201. City market.

(a) For the purpose of this article, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:

(1)

City Market shall mean the historic
market house building excluding any subsequently added wings or additions. commonly
known as the Indianapolis City Market, a place wherein spaces are leased for a valuable
consideration for the purpose of selling and displaying for sale to the general public any items
or products approved by the City Market Corporation.

(2) | Marketer shall mean any person who brings anything to sell and sells it from a space or who
operates a space in the marketCity Market.

(3) Space shall mean any open floor or ground area marked off or designated in any of the main
open areas of the marketCity Market for the use of a marketer in the sale of a marketer's
products, in common with similar areas and not separated by partitions or walls on more than
three (3) sides of the space. Space shall also include any fioor space set off by partitions or
walls on ail four (4) sides thereof, or which may be so designated when so bounded upon at
least three (3) sides, and where the whole area is used for sales.

(b) The legal-deseription of the city markeiCity Market sits_on, but makes up only a portion of, the
following parcelis-as-follows: South half of Square 43, Smith's Heirs Subdivision, Plat Book 8, page 193.

Sec. 285-203. Lease authorized.

The city shall enter into a lease with the City Market Corporation for the lease of the foregeing city
FnarketCity Market property on such terms and conditions as may be negotiated between the city and the
City Market Corporation from time to time and approved by the city-county council.

Sec. 285-204. Authority delegated.

The City Market Corporation shall set the policies, approve the budget and hire the general manager

of the city marketCity Market.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code sections 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15, and 36-3-4-16.

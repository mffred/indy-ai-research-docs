COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2026 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 24, 2026 
Proposal No. 119, 2026 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s  appointment of Jordan Gathers as the Deputy Mayor 
for neighborhood engagement for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is appointed 
and confirmed.  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 201- 4 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County, the 
position of Deputy Mayor for neighborhood engagement is created; and  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36- 3-5-2 and Section 201-4 of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City 
and County, a mayoral appointment of Deputy Mayor for neighborhood engagement is subject to approval 
by the City-County Council; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Jordan 
Gathers to serve as the Deputy Mayor for neighborhood engagement at his pleasure for a term ending 
December 31, 2026 now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1. The position of Deputy Mayor for neighborhood engagement is created.  
 
SECTION 2. Jordan Gathers is approved and confirmed by the City -County Council to serve as Deputy 
Mayor for neighborhood engagement for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is 
appointed and confirmed.  
 
SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.  
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of June, 2026, at 7:11 p.m.

Proposal No. 10, 2026

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No.12, 2025) by an additional appropriation of sixty-five thousand dollars ($65,000) for the purposes of Marion
County Coroner's Office.

BE !T ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of
Marion County Coroner's Office.

SECTION 2. Marion County Coroner's Office requests an additional appropriation totaling sixty thousand
dollars ($60,000) in character one, two, three, and four in the County Grants Fund. and an additional
appropriation totaling five thousand dollars ($5,000) in character two and four in the State of Indiana Grants
Fund for the purposes of funding costs associated with participation in a documentary series, and the Health
First Indiana initiative grant project.

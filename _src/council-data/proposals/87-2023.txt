CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 14, 2023
Proposal No. 87, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Brittany Tinkler, 2023 recipient of the Milken Educator Award.

WHEREAS, Brittany Tinkler, a long-time southside Perry Township resident of indianapolis, is a
graduate of Perry Meridian High Schoo! and a Second Grade Teacher at Rosa Parks Elementary School,
and

WHEREAS, Brittany and her firefighter husband Sidney have two children: Will, age 12, and Kate, age
7; and Brittany joined the Rosa Parks Elementary family in October of 2022; and

WHEREAS, even as a child, Brittany would play school and create worksheets for her stuffed animals
and sister, showing that she had an affinity for teaching as early as kindergarten. She has always aspired
to become a teacher and has a true heart for education; and

WHEREAS, on February 8, 2023, in front of a school audience, Brittany was presented with the
esteemed Milken Educator Award, which honors creative teaching and project-based learning and is
awarded to 40 educators nationwide, by Dr. Katie Jenner, Indiana Secretary of Education, and Dr. Jane
Foley, senior vice president of the Milken Educator Awards; and

WHEREAS, the Milken Educator Award was created in 1987 by businessman and philanthropist Lowell
Milken, a product of public schools who became the founder of the National Institute for Excellence in
Teaching. This award has become the preeminent teacher recognition program in the country, and many
have called it the “Oscars of Teaching.” The award targets early-to-mid-career education professionals for
their already impressive achievements and, more significantly, for the promise of what they will accomplish
in the future. They have rewarded and inspired excellence in the worid of education by honoring top
educators around the county; and

WHEREAS, in addition to receiving all-expenses-paid travel to the Milken Educator Awards Forum in
Los Angeles, California in April; Ms. Tinkler also received a $25,000 unrestricted grant to promote even
more creative and project-based learning opportunities; and

WHEREAS, this award is just the beginning. Brittany is now a part of an elite national group of
educators, who will have access to strong networking and professional development tools throughout the
rest of their career in education; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council recognizes Brittany Tinkler for her excellent work as an
educator, and congratulates her on receiving the 2023 Milken Educator Award.

SECTION 2. The Mayor i ited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6 day of March, 2023, at 7:35 p.m.

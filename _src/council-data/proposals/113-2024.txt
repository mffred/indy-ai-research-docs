CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 14, 2024
Proposal No. 113, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the 25" Anniversary of Brooke’s Place.

WHEREAS, thirteen-year-old Brooke Wright lent her name to the organization after her family
experienced the death of her father, Thomas H. Wright, in the American Eagle Flight #4184 plane crash
near Roselawn, IN, on October 31, 1994; and

WHEREAS, Brooke's Place hosted its first grief support group on April 15, 1999, with 41 people in
attendance. Since then, the organization has provided individual grief therapy services, ongoing support
groups, Camp Healing Tree, BP8 school-based programming, and community education to over 30,000
children, teens, young adults and caregivers; and

WHEREAS, Brooke's Place mission is to empower children, teens, young adults and their families to
thrive in the midst of grief. Because 1 in 10 children in Indiana will experience the death of a parent or
sibling by age 18, Brooke's Place works tirelessly to create a community where every young person living
with grief feels safe, supported, and understood during their personal grief journey; and

WHEREAS, Brooke's Place aims to continue to grow with expanded services to serve children, teens
and young adults ages 3-29, and their caregivers, by partnering with organizations, corporations and
volunteers in Central Indiana; and

WHEREAS, April 15, 2024, will be observed as Brooke's Place Day in recognition of their steadfast
dedication, ceaseless work, and significant influence on the lives of bereaved children and families in
Indianapolis; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes the 25% Anniversary of Brooke’s Place.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis City-County Council acknowledges and appreciates Brooke's Place’s
dedication and commitment to bettering the lives of children in the midst of their grief.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of April, 2024, at 7:45 p.m.

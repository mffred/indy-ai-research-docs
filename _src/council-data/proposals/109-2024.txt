CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 10, 2024
Proposal No. 109, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Fair Housing Month.

WHEREAS, April 2024 marks the 56" anniversary of the passage of the federal Fair Housing Act,
which prohibits discrimination in the rental, sale, financing, Insuring, or zoning of housing based on race,
color, religion, national origin, gender, disability, and famillal status. The federal law seeks to provide
equal housing opportunities, to affirmatively further housing choices, to eliminate legal barriers to equal
housing, and to emphasize equal housing as a fundamental human right for all; and

WHEREAS, the Fair Housing Center of Central Indiana (FHCCI) works to ensure equal housing
opportunities by eliminating housing discrimination through advocacy, enforcement, education, and
outreach. Each April, during Fair Housing Month, the FHCCI hosts its annual conference along with other
activities and events to spread awareness of fair housing laws; and

WHEREAS, historic patterns of segregation and discrimination in housing exist not only nationwide,
but also here in Marion County. Many persons facing discrimination do not feel safe to report their
experiences and struggles. Therefore, the magnitude of segregation and discrimination in housing is
greater than what data show; and

WHEREAS, members of protected classes are increasingly and disproportionately more at risk to
displacement, rental housing discrimination, poor quality housing, and barriers to opportunity due to rising
home ownership, rental housing costs, and imbalanced consumer protections laws; and

WHEREAS, homeownership is the primary means for most Americans to build generational wealth,
yet the homeownership gap between white Americans and Black and Brown Americans is as large as it
was when the federal Fair Housing Act was passed; and

WHEREAS, renters in Marion County are chailenged by the lack of affordable and accessible housing
options, substandard housing conditions, and lack of equality in their rights; and

WHEREAS, equal access to housing is a vital component to our city, as housing is critical to family
and community health and stability as too many residents face barriers in accessing a quality education
and health care, finding nutritious and affordable food, and having stable employment with high quality
transportation options simply due to where they live; now therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1: The Indianapolis City-County Council proclaims April as Fair Housing Month in Indianapolis.
SECTION 2: The Council is committed to building an even more inclusive community by ensuring all

neighbors have access to safe, clean, affordable and fair housing and preventing the negative impact that
discrimination, in ail its forms, has on all citizens of Indianapolis and Marion County.

SECTION 3: The Council expresses our appreciation and gratitude to the Fair Housing Center of Central
Indiana and other housing advocates for their commitment to promoting fair housing for all citizens in our
city and state.

SECTION 4: The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 5: This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

SPECIAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2024

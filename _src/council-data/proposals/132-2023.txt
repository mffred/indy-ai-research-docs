CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 17, 2023
Proposal No. 132, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Ben Davis ROTC Drill teams.

WHEREAS, the Ben Davis MCJROTC Golden Eagle Battalion Rifle Team won the Indiana State ROTC
Rifle Invitational on December 3,2022 and the Ben Davis MCJROTC Golden Eagle Battalion won the
Marine Corps JROTC Mid-West Regional drill meet held at Ben Davis High School on January 28, 2023.;
and

WHEREAS, the Goiden Eagles completed the season with an impressive record of 11-0, and competed
in the extended season against Regional (Indiana, Illinois, Kentucky and Ohio) teams at Concordia
Lutheran High School and handedly took 15 Place; and

WHEREAS, the Golden Eagle Battalion’s Unarmed Division took 1% Place in their Division and the
Armed Division took 3" place resulting in the Golden Eagles Overall Placement of 1°" Place in the Mid-West
Regional drill meet; and

WHEREAS, The Golden Eagle Battalion also competed in the extended season against All-Services
Regional (indiana, Illinois, Kentucky and Ohio) Teams at Concordia Lutheran High School on March 4,2023
and took 15 Place in the Unarmed Division. Many of the team that competed at the Concordia Lutheran
High School drill meet are perennial National Champions, now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Ben Davis ROTC Drill teams on
their championship placements at the State Invitational and Mid-West Regional competitions.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily congratulates the Ben Davis ROTC Drill teams and wishes them
continued future success.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with !C 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8 day of May, 2023, at 7:33 p.m.

CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
 
 
I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development 
Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on June 5, 2024, a public hearing as required by law 
pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and 
Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment 
prayed for in petition: 
 
2024-ZON-015  2053 Yandes Street (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 227, 2024 Center Township, Council District #13 
   Hollister Properties, LLC, by Kristin Hollister 
Rezoning of 0.13 acres from the I-3 district to the D-8 district to provide for 
residential uses. 
 
2024-ZON-023 3801 North Raceway Road (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 228, 2024 Pike Township, Council District #11  
   D.R. Horton – Indiana, LLC, by Brian J. Tuohy 
Rezoning of 8.16 acres from the D-A district to the D-6 district to provide for a 
townhome development.  
 
2024-ZON-028 5332 South Franklin Road (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 229, 2024 Franklin Township, Council District #25  
   Lennar Homes of Indiana, LLC., by Brian J. Tuohy 
Rezoning of 75.3 acres from the D-A district to the D-4 district to provide for

2024-ZON-035 1201 South Holt Road (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 230, 2024 Wayne Township, Council District #17 
   Badesha Bros, by Jamilah Mintze

I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance prayed for 
in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, 
Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended 2024-ZON-015, 2024-
ZON-023, 2024-ZON-028, 2024-ZON-035, 2024-ZON-041, 2024-ZON-043, 2024-ZON-045, and 
2024-ZON-046 to the City-County Council for adoption to the end that adequate light, air, 
convenience of access and safety from fire, flood, and other danger may be secured, that property 
values may be preserved and that the public health, comfort, morals and convenience and general 
welfare may be preserved. 
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the records of the Metropolitan Development Commission of

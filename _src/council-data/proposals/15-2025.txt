COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
 
 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 20, 2025 
Proposal No. 15, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Percy Bland, Jr. to the Metropolitan Zoning Appeals Board. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Metropolitan Zoning Appeals Board, the Council reappoints:  
 
Percy Bland, Jr. 
 
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025.  The 
person reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the 
term, the appointee may serve until his or her succe ssor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to 
exceed the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3
rd day of February, 2025, at 8:23 p.m.

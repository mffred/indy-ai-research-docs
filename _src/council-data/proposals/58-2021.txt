CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 54, 2021
Proposal No. 58, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Joseph Wynns to the Board of Parks and Recreation.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Parks and Recreation, the Council reappoints:

Joseph Wynns
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2021. The person
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the
holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2274 day of February, 2021, at 8:15 p.m.

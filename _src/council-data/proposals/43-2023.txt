CITY-COUNTY GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 6, 2023
Proposal No. 44, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE to amend the Revised Code to clarify procedures for
improvement location permits related to signs.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Chapter 226, Article Il! of the "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County," regarding the
department of business and neighborhood services, division of administration, logistics, licenses and permits,
is hereby amended by the insertion of new text in Section 226-304 (previously reserved), to read as follows:

Sec. 226-304. Procedure for signs.

Notwithstanding any other provision of Chapter 740, Article VIII of the Revised Code, the following
provisions apply where an improvement location permit is sought for the location, erection, alteration, or
repair of a sign (as that term is defined by Section 740-202 of the Revised Code):

A. The Administrator or the Administrator's designee shall provide an initial response to an application
for permit no later than twelve (12) business days after the application is received by the
Administrator. An application will be considered received upon being properly submitted in the

manner prescribed by the Administrator. An | response is defined as one of the following:

1. the issuance of the permit;

2. the denial of the permit application; or

3. awritten response to the applicant stating (a) that the application is incomplete because it
lacks information required by Section 740-802 and (b) that review will be completed only
after such missing required information is supplied.

B. If the Administrator or the Administrator's designee timely provides an initial response under
subparagraph (A)(3) above, the Administrator or the Administrator's designee shall subsequently
issue a final response, constituting either the issuance of the permit or the denial of the application,
no later than five (5) business days after the Administrator receives from the applicant the additional
information necessary to make the permit application complete.

C. Except as provided in paragraph (D) below, the permit is deemed issued if all applicable permit
application fees have been paid in full and the Administrator or the Administrator's designee does
not provide an initial response within the time period (twelve (12) business days) required by
paragraph (A) above or a final response within the time period (five (5) business days) required by
paragraph (B) above.

D. The permit shall not be deemed as issued, tegardless of the amount of time elapsed since the
application is received by the Administrator, if the Administrator or the Administrator's designee
timely provides an initial response under subparagraph (A)(3) above and the applicant does not
subsequently supply the missing required information.

E. Apermit that is deemed issued under paragraph (C) above is subject to automatic revocation under
Section 740-809 and revocation in event of violation under Section 740-810, on the same basis as
if a permit were actually issued. A sign that is located, erected, altered, or repaired pursuant to a
permit deemed issued under paragraph (C) remains subject to all requirements of the zoning
ordinance that pertain to signs, and the enforcement provisions of Article X of Chapter 740 apply
to the location, erection, alteration, or repair of a sign that is deemed permitted on the same basis
as if a permit were actually issued. For the avoidance of doubt, a deemed-issued permit obviates
the permit requirement under Section 744-903 but does not legally establish a non-conforming use
or otherwise exempt a sign from the enforcement provisions of Article X of Chapter 740.”

SECTION 2. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its adoption and compliance with
Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

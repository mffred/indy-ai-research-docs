CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 10, 2026
Proposal No. 132, 2026

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No.12, 2025) by an additional appropriation of two hundred twenty thousand five hundred dollars ($220,500)
for the purposes of Marion County Coroner's Office.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-County
Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for purposes of
Marion County Coroner's Office.

SECTION 2. Marion County Coroner’s Office requests an additional appropriation totaling sixty thousand
dollars ($60,000) in character two, and three in the County Grants, and an additional appropriation totaling
one hundred sixty thousand five hundred dollars ($160,500) in character two and three in the State o
Indiana Grants Fund for the purposes of funding costs associated with the American Medical Association
project, the Health First Indiana Initiative project, and Trauma Informed Care project.

SECTION 4. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does no
intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the appropriation
for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or project, or both,
and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city-county council immediately upon receipt of any
information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-14.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 3, 2024
Proposal No. 25, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Arael Stigler for winning the Boys & Girls Clubs of In
Youth of the Year Award.

WHEREAS, for 130 years, Boys & Girls Clubs of indianapolis (BGCI) has provided a safe and nurturing
place for every young person in Indianapolis to grow and thrive. It has worked tirelessly to provide them
with the tools and mentorship they need to reach their full potential and become positive, productive
citizens. Each year, BGCI recognizes its members for the exemplary work as members, student, and
citizens; and

WHEREAS, a native of Indianapolis, Arael Stigier has come a long way as a BGCI youth. Since joining
in 2016, Arael has grown leaps and bounds personally and academically. Starting as a reserved, shy
member of our Club, Arael has transformed into a bold and fearless leader both within the Club and her
community; and

WHEREAS, Arael has been actively involved in the Finish Line Club for the past few summers, serving
as a small group leader. She will take the lead in anything related to the aris. As an active participant in
dance, step, and cheer teams, Arael is always willing to volunteer with dance/step programming; and

WHEREAS, as a student at Lawrence Central High School, Arael is also a standout leader. She is a
student section leader in the choir, a member of the National Honor Society, and participates in Key Club.
Additionally, Arael chooses to sacrifice her time at the Club as a staff member so that she can focus solely
on academic achievement; and

WHEREAS, Arael attributes her leadership and growth at the Club to consistent support and
encouragement from her Club director, Tialana Kastner. She says that the value in her time at the Club has
made her who she is today; and

WHEREAS, Arael has grown into an amazing leader amongst peers, she carries herself with incredible
poise and she is loved and respected by all. After years of Club and personal growth and dedication, she
has made her academic goals very clear: Arael plans to attend Tennessee State University to major in
nursing; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly honors Arael Stigler for winning the Boys & Girls
Clubs of Indianapolis 2023 Youth of the Year Award.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily thanks Arael Stigler for her dedication to being a remarkable leader at
the Boys & Girls Clubs of Indianapolis and wishes her an abundance of success in her future endeavors.

SECTION 3. The Mayor has been invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5 day of February, 2024, at 7:28 p.m.

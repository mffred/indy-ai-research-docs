I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of

and Commercial Record" newspapers of generai circulation in Marion County, indiana, said
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment
prayed for in petition:

| FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance
prayed for in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended
2022-ZON-126 (Amended) and 2022-ZON-132 to the City-County Council for adoption to the
end that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety from fire, flood, and other
danger may be secured, that property values may be preserved and that the public health,
comfort, morals and convenience and general welfare may be preserved.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 23, 2023
Proposal No. 328, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022) by an additional appropriation of four jon one hundred fifteen thousand
dollars ($4,115,000) for the purposes of the Department of Metropolitan Development and the City Non-
Departmental.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2023 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for
purposes of the Department of Metropolitan Development and the City Non-Departmental.

SECTION 2. The Department of Metropolitan Development requests an additional appropriation totaling
one million seven hundred ninety thousand dollars ($1,790,000) in the Consolidated County General Fund
in characters three and four and an additional appropriation totaling seven hundred seventy-five thousand
dollars ($775,000) in the State of indiana Grants Fund in character three for the purpose of providing local
contributions to the Housing to Recovery program and land acquisition:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 GHAR 4 CHAR § TOTAL
Consolidated County
General 1,040,000 | 750,000 1,790,000
State of Indiana
Grants 775,000 775,000

SECTION 3. The City Non-Departmental requests an additional appropriation totaling one million five
hundred fifty thousand dollars ($1,550,000) in the Revenue Bonds Fund in character three for the purpose
of affording additional debt service capacity for the HUD Section 108 Loan program:

FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL
Revenue Bonds 4,550,000 1,550,000

SECTION 4. In support of the additional appropriation provided in Section 3, HUD Federal Grants revenues
are anticipated to be received totaling one million five hundred fifty thousand dollars ($1,550,000) and
placed on deposit in the Federal Grants Fund, and this amount will be transferred from the Federal Grants
Fund to the Revenue Bonds Fund.

SECTION 5. Upon approval of this and other pending approvals, the 2022 year-end and projected 2023
year-end fund balances are as follows:

Fund 2022 Year-End Balance Projected 2023 Year-End Balance

Consolidated County General 166,288,731 170,764,649

State of Indiana Grants - -

Revenue Bonds 1,603,942 1,345,810

SECTION 6. Except to the extent of matching funds, if any, approved in this ordinance, the council does
not intend to use the revenues from any local tax regardless of source to supplement or extend the
appropriation for the agencies or projects authorized by this ordinance. The supervisor of the agency or
project, or both, and the controller are directed to notify in writing the city-county council immediately upon
receipt of any information that the agency or project is, or may be, reduced or eliminated.

SECTION 7. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC
36-3-4-14.

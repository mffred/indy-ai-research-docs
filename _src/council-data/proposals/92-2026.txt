CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 16, 2026
Proposal No. 92, 2026

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Doran Moreland as Chief Administrative Officer/Policy Director for
the City-County Council of Indianapolis, Marion County.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As required by Section 151-98 of the Revised Code of the City of Indianapolis — Marion
County, the City-County Council, has considered for approval the recommendation of the committee on
tules and public policy the appointment of Doran Moreland for the position of Chief Administrative
Officer/Policy Director for the City-County Council.

SECTION 2. The Council hereby approves the appointment of Doran Moreland as the Chief Administrative
Officer/Policy Director for the Council office.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6th day of April, 2026, at 7:16 p.m.

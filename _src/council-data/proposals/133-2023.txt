CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 18, 2023
Proposal No. 133, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Ben Davis High Schoo! Boys Basketball team.

WHEREAS, the Ben Davis High School Boys Basketball team finished off their season with the Indiana
Class 4A IHSAA Basketball championship title at Gainbridge Fieldhouse on March 23, 2023; and

WHEREAS, the Ben Davis team will go down in history as being one of the best high school teams in
Indiana history, after the Giants finished a perfect 33-0 run with a 53-41 Class 4A state championship
triumph over the Kokomo wildcats; and

WHEREAS; Coach Don Carlisle is the first coach in history to win as both a player and a coach at the
same high school, 5" coach to win as a player and coach; and

WHEREAS, Mark White Jr., Mark Zakery IV.,Jesse Faires, Sheridan Sharp, Justin Harrington, Clay
Butler Ill, Lincoin Murff, Ramone Enis, Jamir Carnell, KJ Windham, Shaun Arnold, Zane Doughty, Tavion
Williams, Justin Parks, Jahmir Avery, Kevin Elliott, Shaun Paul Turner, MJ Turner, LaJuan Ashely, TJ
Harris, Josh Banks, Darren Hardy, Chloe Belton, Terran Coles, Kyndell Minor, Mathias Pfister, Carter
Pinner, Ahmani Smith, Xavier Stevenson, Don Carlisle, Courtney James, Corey Taylor, Kevin Butler, Virgil
Akers, Michael Ray, Phil Brown, Fred Long, and Dominique Ingram are the members and coaches of the
2023 Indiana Class 4A state championship team; and

WHEREAS, we are grateful and honored to have the Ben Davis Giants Basketball team represent the
City and Marion County at the state championship and even more excited that they brought home the 4A
state championship title; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council recognizes the Ben Davis High School Basketball team
for winning the Class 4A State Championship.

SECTION 2. The Council extends its congratulations to the team on their achievement and wishes them
success in future endeavors, both athletically and academically.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8" day of May, 2023, at 7:42 p.m.

COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2024 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 30, 2024 
Proposal No. 46, 2024 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Christopher Roberson to the Animal Services Advisory Board. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Animal Services Advisory Board, fulfilling the requirement as a community 
member, the Council appoints:  
 
Christopher Roberson 
 
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2024.  The person 
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the 
appointee may serve until his or her succes sor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the 
holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 4
th day of March, 2024, at 7:43 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 10, 2023
Proposal No. 55, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Cub Scout Pack 51 of St. Jude Parish.

WHEREAS, Cub Scout Pack 51 is located at St. Jude Parish in Perry Township. The Cub Scout pack
is a member of the Pathfinder District within the Crossroads of America Council of BSA (formerly Boys
Scouts of America); and

WHEREAS, the Pathfinder District encompasses a portion of Center Township, all of Perry and Franklin
Townships, and all of Johnson County. Crossroads Of America Council ranges from the Ohio to Illinois
borders on the east and west, as far north as Blackford County, and southwest to Sullivan County, with
Franklin County on the east; and

WHEREAS, Cub Scout Pack 51 earned more than any other participating Boy Scouts of America pack
or troop in all of Marion County during the 2022 popcorn sale, a total of $29,258; and

WHEREAS, to be the top earner of all Boy Scouts of America took massive dedication of the scouts,
parents, volunteers, organizers and the greater community. This is also the top amount ever raised by the
Pack (more than doubling last year’s record); and

WHEREAS; Pack 51 (and ail participating scouting units) receive approximately one-third of the money
raised, which pays for all the activities and participating in summer camps around town. More specifically,
it pays for camping trips, internal competitions (such as the pinewood derby and the rain gutter regata,)
admissions to Wolf Park and the Link Observatory (1U-Bloomington), as well as helping to get scouts to and
from unique places like the Marian University Pulliam EcoLab; and

WHEREAS, Boy Scouts learn goal setting, problem solving, math and money skills, independence,
team work, self-confidence, public speaking skills, understanding towards hard work, and get to practice
gratitude; and

WHEREAS, this is a phenomenal achievement for the boys and girls of the organization. These kids
are amazing in their efforts and have learned a lot; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Cub Scout Pack 51 of St. Jude
Parish.

SECTION 2. The Council thanks Cub Scout Pack 51 for their dedication and congratulates them on
becoming the top earner of all Boy Scouts of America.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6 day of February, 2023, at 7:38 p.m.

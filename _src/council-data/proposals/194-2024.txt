I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of Metropolitan

required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and 
Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment 
prayed for in petition:

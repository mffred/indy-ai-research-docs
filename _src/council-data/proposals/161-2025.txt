CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
 
I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Interim Current Planning Administrator, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at the meeting of the Metropolitan Development 
Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on April 16, 2025, a public hearing as required 
by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” 
and “Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, 
Indiana, said Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning 
ordinance amendment prayed for in petition:  
 
 
2024-ZON-115 6600 and 6908 Hickory Road (Approximate Addresses) 
Prop No. 159, 2025 Franklin Township, Council District #25 
R.O. No. 43, 2025 Manheet Singh, by Joseph D. Calderon 
Rezoning of 145 acres from the D-A (FF) district to the D-P (FF) district 
to provide for a multi-family and single-family attached senior 
community development, a single-family detached residential 
development consisting of 240 units, and educational uses, including a 
daycare or nursery school, with amenities, including a community 
garden, a swimming pool, game courts, playgrounds, and pathways. 
 
2025-ZON-014 6650 Telecom Drive (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 160, 2025 Pike Township, Council District #6 
R.O. No. 44, 2025 Intech Partners, LLC, by Michael Rabinowitch 
Rezoning of 9.4 acres from the C-S district to the C-S district to provide 
for C-1 uses, a hotel, an eating establishment, and a conference 
center, within an existing structure. 
 
2025-ZON-020 3520 Washington Boulevard (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 161, 2025 Center Township, Council District #8 
R.O. No. 45, 2025 M. Jacqueline Nytes, by Jonathan Blake 
Rezoning of 0.82-acre from the D-3 district to the C-1 district to provide 
for religious and office commercial uses. 
 
2025-ZON-021 2102 Brookside Avenue (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 162, 2025 Center Township, Council District #8 
R.O. No. 46, 2025 Powderhouse Indy, LLC, by Nicholas Rosiello 
Rezoning of 0.079-acre from the MU-1 district to the D-8 district to 
provide for residential development.

I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance 
prayed for in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended 
2024-ZON-115, 2025-ZON-014, 2025-ZON-020, and 2025-ZON-021 to the City-County 
Council for adoption to the end that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety 
from fire, flood, and other danger may be secured, that property values may be preserved 
and that the public health, comfort, morals, and convenience and general welfare may be 
preserved.

CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 7, 2026
Proposal No. 68, 2026

A GENERAL RESOLUTION designating a portion of New York Street, from East Street to College Avenue,
as James T. Kienle Memorial Way.

WHEREAS, James T. Kienle practiced architecture for over 50 years and was elevated to the American
Institute of Architects College of Fellows and was awarded the Sagamore of the Wabash for his pioneering
work in preservation; and

WHEREAS, he was committed to service and civic leadership; and

WHEREAS, for twenty-five years, with twelve as President, he served on the Indianapolis Historic
Preservation Commission; and

WHEREAS, he was involved in numerous civic preservation projects throughout his career including
the restoration of the Hilbert Circle Theatre and the Indiana State Capitol; and

WHEREAS he was on the boards and committees of several non-profit organizations including Indiana
Landmarks, and City of Indianapolis’s Urban Design Oversight Committee, board member of the The
American Red Cross — Greater Indianapolis, a Rotarian in the Downtown Indianapolis Rotary Club, and a
graduate of Stanley K. Lacy Executive Leadership Series. now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council does hereby honor the memory of James T. Kienle by
designating the portion of New York Street from East Street to College Avenue, as the “James T. Kienle
Memorial Way.”

SECTION 2. As per Revised Code Section 431-405(a) this authorization shall expire fifteen (15) years after
itis passed by the City-County Council.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6th day of April, 2026, at 7:23 p.m.

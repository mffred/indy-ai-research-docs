COUNCIL RESOLUTON RECORD 2026 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 10, 2026 
Proposal No. 34, 2026 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s  appointment of Christopher L. Bailey as the Chief 
Deputy Mayor for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.  
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-4 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City 
and County,” a mayoral appointment of Chief Deputy Mayor is subject to approval of the City -County 
Council; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Christopher 
L. Bailey to serve as the Chief Deputy Mayor at his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2026; now, 
therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. Christopher L. Bailey is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as 
Chief Deputy Mayor for a term ending December 31, 2026, and until a successor is appointed and 
confirmed. 
 
SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-
3-4-14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2nd day of March, 2026, at 7:22 p.m.

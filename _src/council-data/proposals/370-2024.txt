CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO.  , 2024 
 
A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE to amend the Revised Code Section of the Consolidated City 
of Indianapolis Marion County to add guidelines for the submittal of Special Resolutions 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County Section 151-61 is hereby amended to 
add the portions underlined and to delete the portions stricken through and highlighted to read as follow reading 
as follows: 
Sec. 151-61. Designation of proposals. 
(a) Any proposal for action by a council by ordinance or general resolution shall be in writing and 
entitled in the following form: "a proposal for a ____________ ," inserting therein the class of 
ordinance or general resolution and the subject matter of the proposal.  
 
(b) Any proposal which contemplates a resolution or other action by a council in the nature of a 
memorial, commendation or other admonishment or proclamation shall be designated "a proposal 
for a special resolution." The proposal shall be properly before the c ouncil for final action unless 
the president or the council refers or tables the proposal. If eight (8) councilors so request, the 
proposal shall be referred to a committee. The following guidelines shall apply to all Special 
Resolutions 
 
(1) Memorials:  
a. Must be a deceased person recognized county or state-wide.  
i. The named individual may only be recognized once. 
b. Must be a milestone event or anniversary recognized county or state-wide. 
c. Nationally recognized events must have specific significance locally 
i. Nationally recognized events may not be slated for the agenda if time is of the essence 
for any particular month. 
 
(2) Commendations/Admonishments/Proclamations: 
a. Subject matter

i. must be of general interest and within the power and jurisdiction of the Council.  
ii. must be something generally known to be supported by a substantial portion of the 
councilors and the county-wide citizenry. 
iii. should not be politically partisan or potentially or intentionally divisive to the community 
at large. 
b. Must be a living person recognized county or state-wide 
i. Individual or group must not have received a similar recognition from the council within 
the last year (unless it is a state-wide sporting championship.) 
c. Must be a milestone event or anniversary. 
 
(3) Other restrictions or guidelines 
a. No Special Resolutions will be introduced during City -County annual budget deliberations 
(currently August through October) 
b. A maximum of two speakers, speaking no more than two minutes each, will be allowed per 
resolution. 
c. Each Councilor can lead -sponsor no more than three Special Resolutions per calendar 
year. 
d. Special Resolutions should be no more than one page (not including signatures.)  
e. Exceptions to these guidelines may be made for city-sponsored events or people. 
f. The President shall have the power to make the final determination as to whether a matter 
meets the guidelines spelled out here -in or determine which matters will be added to the 
agenda. 
g. Proposals not meeting the requirements in this section shall be eligible for an individual 
Proclamation from the individual Councilor. 
 
(c) Any proposal which contemplates action relating only to the internal procedures of a council, its 
finances or its staff, or relates to appointments, removal of appointments or confirmation of 
appointments made exclusively by the council, shall be designate d "a proposal for a council 
resolution." The proposal shall be properly before the council for final action at the same meeting 
at which it is introduced, unless the president or the council refers or postpones it.  
 
(d) All other actions of a council shall be taken by ordinance or general resolution. Ordinances or 
general resolutions shall be designated in one (1) of the following classes:  
(1) General ordinances, which shall include all ordinances of general application to the public;  
 
(2) Fiscal ordinances, which shall include all ordinances establishing annual budget or 
appropriations, and any revisions to such budgets or appropriations;  
 
(3) Special ordinances, which shall include ordinances of special application or private 
concessions;  
 
(4) Rezoning ordinances, which are zoning map changes certified by the metropolitan 
development commission;  
 
(5) General resolutions, which are resolutions effecting official actions of governmental or general 
application.  
 
(e ) Proposals for action by a special service district council shall be designated similarly including the 
respective council.  
 
SECTION 2.  Should any provision (section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or any other portion) of this 
ordinance be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid for any reason, the remaining provision 
or provisions shall not be affected, if and only if such remaining provisions can, without the invalid provision or 
provisions, be given the effect intended by the Council in adopting this ordinance.  To this end the provisions 
of this ordinance are severable. 
 
SECTION 3.  This ordinance shall be in effect from and after January 1, 2025, and compliance with Ind. Code 
§ 36-3-4-14.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 14, 2026
Proposal No. 223, 2026

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION celebrating the life, legacy, and Indianapolis roots of Janet Flanner,
Internationally acclaimed journalist, author, and cultural correspondent.

WHEREAS, Janet Flanner was born in Indianapolis, Indiana, in 1892 and began her writing career in
her hometown before embarking on a path that would make her one of the most influential journalists of the
twentieth century; and

WHEREAS, writing under the pen name “Genét,” Flanner became one of the founding voices of The
New Yorker magazine and served as its Paris correspondent for more than fifty years, helping generations
of Americans better understand the culture, politics, literature, and artistic life of Europe; and

WHEREAS, through her celebrated “Letter from Paris” columns, Flanner chronicled major historical
events, artistic movements, and cultural figures of the twentieth century, establishing herself as one of
America’s most respected literary journalists; and

WHEREAS, in recognition of her extraordinary contributions to journalism and French cultural life,
Fianner was awarded the titie of Chevalier of the Légion d'Honneur, one of France's highest ci n honors;
and

WHEREAS, Flanner also demonstrated personal courage by living openly within Paris's expatriate
artistic and LGBTQ+ communities during a period when such freedom was often unavailable to many
Americans, leaving a lasting legacy of authenticity, inclusion, and cultural progress; and

WHEREAS, Janet Flanner's Indianapolis roots remain an important part of her story, and her
achievements stand as a reminder of the profound impact that individuals from this city have had on national
and international culture and public life; and

WHEREAS, the Flanner family's contributions to Indianapolis extend beyond journalism through the
establishment of Flanner House by her father, Dr. Frank W. Flanner, creating a lasting legacy of community
service, education, and opportunity for generations of Indianapolis residents; and

WHEREAS, The District Theatre's production of They Call Me Genét offers Indianapolis an opportunity
to rediscover and celebrate one of its most accomplished native daughters and her enduring contributions
to journalism, literature, and international understanding; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes and celebrates the life,
achievements, and enduring legacy of Janet Flanner, whose work elevated the standards of journalism and
enriched cultural understanding throughout the world.

SECTION 2. The Council commends The District Theatre and all those involved in the production of They
Cali Me Genét for preserving and sharing the story of this remarkable Indianapolis native.

SECTION 3. The Council encourages residents of Indianapolis to learn more about Janet Flanner's
contributions to journalism, literature, culture, and international understanding and to take pride in her
enduring connection to the City of Indianapolis.

SECTION 4. A copy of this resolution shall be presented to representatives of The District Theatre in
recognition of their efforts to celebrate and preserve the legacy of Janet Flanner.

SECTION 5. The Mayor is invited fo join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 6. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

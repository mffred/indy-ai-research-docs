CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 5, 2024
Proposal No. 53, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring, Pastor Tim and Dr. Tanya Harris for their partnership in making
significant and transformative impacts on the community.

WHEREAS, Dr. Tanya Harris, born as the second of four children and the sole daughter, has been
shaped by a myriad of experiences, fostering in her a deep empathy for youth and families confronting
adversities; and

WHEREAS, in June 1978, she entered into marriage with Tim Harris from Chillicothe, Ohio, and from
this union, two children, Taquoya and Tim Il, were born, fulfilling her desire for a large family. Knowing her
chiidren would choose their partners. Elden Porter (Taquoya) and Andrea Hoke (Tim Il) became integral
parts of the Harris family, complementing it with their unions, followed by the blessings of six cherished
grandchildren; and

WHEREAS, alongside her husband Tim, Dr. Tanya Harris founded TM Youth Camp in 1980 on the
Hallowed Hills Campgrounds in Zanesville, Ohio. This enduring initiative continues to impact youths across
the United States and Canada, fulfilling its mission to reach the current generation and strengthen the
brethren. Furthermore, the inaugural TM Pastor and Leaders Camp in January 2018, conceived and
executed by Tim and Tanya together, remains committed to this divine purpose; and

WHEREAS, extending their commitment to serving God's people of all ages, Dr. Tanya's daughter,
Taquoya, initiated TM Children’s Camp in 2014, compieting the full spectrum of outreach activities under
the TM umbrella; and

WHEREAS on December 31, 1996, Dr. Tanya Harris, in partnership with her husband Tim, established
the Turning Point Family Worship Center, guided by the motto, "/f you do it the Bible way, you'll get the
Bible results." Their vision remains steadfast in teaching, training, transforming, and testifying, aligning
every aspect of their ministry with God's will; and

WHEREAS, the vision of Turning Point Family Worship Center and TM Camp has blossomed into a
thriving reality, encompassing 23 acres in the heart of Indianapolis’ far eastside community. Within this
haven, 26 ministries, including The Community of Scholars, TP Kiddie Academy, and TP Schools catering
from 1st to 8th grades, are making significant and transformative impacts on the community; and

WHEREAS, Dr. Tanya Harris, alongside her husband Tim, have tirelessly devoted their lives to serving
others, leaving an indelible mark of love, faith, and an unwavering commitment to uplifting and empowering
countless hearts and minds; and

WHEREAS, February 5, 2024, shall forever be celebrated as Mr. Tim and Dr. Tanya Harris Day, a day
dedicated to honoring their unwavering commitment, tireless efforts, and profound impact on the lives of
countless individuals and communities. This day serves as a tribute to their selfless dedication, love, and
the transformative influence they have imparted, leaving an indelible mark on the world; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes Pastor Tim and Dr. Tanya Harris.

SECTION 2. The Indianapolis City-County Council acknowledges and appreciates Pastor Tim and Dr.
Tanya Harris’s dedication and commitment to bettering the lives of not just Turning Point's Community, but
Indianapolis as a whole.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

SPECIAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2024

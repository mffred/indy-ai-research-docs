CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 16, 2023
Proposal No. 234, 2023

A GENERAL ORDINANCE approving the appropriation of proceeds and investment earnings thereon of
(a) “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Central Library
Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023A” in an original aggregate principal amount not to
exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of financing ail or
any portion of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project; (b) “Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update
Project), Series 2023B” in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred
Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of financing all or any portion of the 2023 Nora
Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project; and (c) “Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library
General Obligation Bonds (2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series 2023C" in
an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars
($6,105,000), for the purpose of financing all or any portion of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and
Facility Update Project.

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board (the “Library Board”) of the Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library, Indiana (the “Public Library”), adopted Resolution 43-2023 (the “2023 Central
Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more
series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion
County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update
Project), Series 2023A" (collectively, the “2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project
Bonds’), in an original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand
Dollars ($6,105,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Central Library
Renovation and Facility Update Project (as defined in the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility
Update Project Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023
Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 44-2023 (the "2023 Nora Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of
general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion County Public
Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series
20238” (collectively, the “2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds’), in an
original aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars
($6,105,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and
Facility Update Project (as defined in the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project
Resolution) and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023 Nora Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 45-2023 (the “2023 Pike Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution”) authorizing the issuance of one or more series of
general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-Marion County Public
Library General Obligation Bonds (2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project), Series
2023C” (collectively, the “2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds’), in an original
aggregate principal amount not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000),
for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay for the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update
Project (as defined in the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Resolution) and costs
related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility
Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 46-2023, appropriating a sum
not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), out of the proceeds of the
2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Bonds, together with all investment earnings
thereon, for the purpose of paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and
Facility Update Project; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 47-2023, appropriating a sum
not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), out of the proceeds of the
2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Bonds, together with all investment earnings thereon,
for the purpose of paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility
Update Project; and

WHEREAS, on July 24, 2023, the Library Board adopted Resolution 48-2023, appropriating a sum
not to exceed Six Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000), out of the proceeds of the
2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Bonds, together with all investment earnings thereon,
for the purpose of paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Fa
Update Project; and

WHEREAS, the Library Board has requested the approval of the City-County Council, pursuant to
Indiana Code § 6-1.1-18-5(j), as amended, of the approval of the additional appropriation of: (a) the
proceeds of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds and investment
earnings thereon in an amount not to exceed $6,105,000 for the 2023 Central Library Renovation and
Facility Update Project, (b) the proceeds of the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project
Bonds and investment earnings thereon in an amount not to exceed $6,105,000 for the 2023 Nora
Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project, and (c) the proceeds of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation
and Facility Update Project Bonds and investment earnings thereon in an amount not to exceed
$6,105,000 for the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project, and the City-County
Council now finds that the appropriations set forth above should be approved; and

WHEREAS, notice of a hearing on said appropriations has been published as required by law; and

WHEREAS, such public hearing on said appropriations has been held on the date hereof at this
location and prior to consideration of this Ordinance at which all taxpayers and interested persons had an
opportunity to appear and express their views as to such additional appropriations; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The City-County Council does hereby approve the appropriation of a sum not to exceed Six
Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000) out of the proceeds of the 2023 Central Library
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds together with all investment earnings (the "2023 Central
Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation”), all of which sum shall be for the use of the
Public Library in paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility
Update Project.

SECTION 2. The 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation shall be in
addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy of the Public Library, and shall
continue in effect until the completion of the 2023 Central Library Renovation and Facility Update Project.
Any surplus of such proceeds shall be credited to the proper fund as required by law.

SECTION 3. The City-County Council does hereby approve the appropriation of a sum not to exceed Six
n One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000) out of the proceeds of the 2023 Nora Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds together with all investment earnings (the "2023 Nora
Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation”), all of which sum shall be for the use of
the Nora Branch in paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility
Update Project.

SECTION 4. The 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation shail be in
addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy of the Public Library, and shall
continue in effect until the completion of the 2023 Nora Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project.
Any surplus of such proceeds shall be credited to the proper fund as required by law.

SECTION 5. The City-County Council does hereby approve the appropriation of a sum not to exceed Six
Million One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($6,105,000) out of the proceeds of the 2023 Pike Branch
Renovation and Facility Update Project Bonds together with all investment earnings (the “2023 Pike
Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation’), all of which sum shall be for the use of
the Public Library in paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility
Update Project.

SECTION 6. The 2023 Pike Branch Renovation and Facility Update Project Appropriation shalt be in
addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy of the Public Library, and shall

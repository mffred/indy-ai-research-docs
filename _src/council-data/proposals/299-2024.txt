COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2024 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 70, 2024 
Proposal No. 299, 2024 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Lindsay Stawick to the Domestic Violence Fatality Review Team. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Domestic Violence Fatality Review Team (fulfilling the requirement of a 
medical practitioner), the Council appoints:  
 
Lindsay Stawick 
 
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending September 23, 2026.  The 
person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term, 
the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed 
the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7th day of October, 2024, at 7:08 p.m.

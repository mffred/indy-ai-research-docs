SPONSOR: Councilor Robinson 
 
DIGEST:  approves an additional appropriation totaling $12,600,000 in the 2025 Budget of the 
Indianapolis Fire Department (Fire General and Fire Cumulative Funds) for the purposes of funding the 
collective bargaining agreement with the Indianapolis Professional Firefighters Local 416 and the payoff 
of the 2021 apparatus lease

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.     , 2025 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 6, 2024) by an additional appropriation of twelve million six hundred thousand dollars ($12,600,000) for 
the purposes of the Indianapolis Fire Department. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:  
 
SECTION 1.  To pro vide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 20 25 is hereby amended by the character increases hereinafter stated for 
purposes of the Indianapolis Fire Department. 
 
SECTION 2. The Indianapolis Fire Department requests an additional appropriation totaling eleven million 
three hundred thousand  dollars ($11,300,000)  in the Fire General  Fund in character one,  and an 
additional appropriation totaling one million three hundred thousand dollars ($1,300,000) in the Fire 
Cumulative Fund in character four for the purposes of funding the collective bargaining agreement with the 
Indianapolis Professional Firefighters Local 416 and the payoff of the 2021 apparatus lease: 
 
FUND CHAR 1 CHAR 2 CHAR 3 CHAR 4 CHAR 5 TOTAL 
Fire General 11,300,000     11,300,000 
Fire Cumulative   1,300,000   1,300,000 
 
SECTION 3. Upon approval of this and other pending proposals, the 2024 year-end and projected 202 5 
year-end fund balances are as follows: 
 
Fund 2024 Year-End Balance Projected 2025 Year-End Balance 
Fire General 3,683,425 3,501,298 
Fire Cumulative 3,315,750 1,809,913 
 
SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-
4-14.

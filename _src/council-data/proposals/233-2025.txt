CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 75, 2025
Proposal No, 233, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Ebony Chappel to the Alcoholic Beverage Board of Marion County.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Alcoholic Beverage Board of Marion County, the Council reappoints:
Ebony Chappel

SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending September 8, 2026. The person

reappointed by this resolution shail serve at the pleasure of the Council and until his or her successor is

appointed and qualifies, unless the duration of the holdover period for this office is limited by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8th day of September, 2025, at 7:08 p.m.

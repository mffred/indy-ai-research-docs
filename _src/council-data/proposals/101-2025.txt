CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 9, 2025
Proposal Ne. 101, 2025

A GENERAL RESOLUTION designating a portion of 25 Street from North Hillside Avenue to North Dr.
Andrew J. Brown Avenue, as Pastor Frank Alexander Memorial Way.

WHEREAS, Pastor Frank Alexander was born June 21, 1943, to Elijah and Annie Alexander and was
educated at Bishop Coliege in Dallas, Texas and at The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary in
Louisville, Kentucky; and

WHEREAS, Pastor Alexander served as an instructor/counselor for Indiana’s Job Corps, as the director
of Gateway Christian Center in Muncie, Indiana, as the director of Edna Martin Christian Center, and for 40
years he was the senior Pastor of Oasis of Hope Baptist Church, a place to serve and be served; and

WHEREAS, under Pastor Alexander's leadership College Avenue Baptist Church was one of the first
Black churches in Indianapolis to out-grow its then-current facility and build a new facility named Oasis of
Hope Baptist Church; and

WHEREAS, Pastor Alexander led his church in creating two community development corporations,
purchasing a 185-unit apartment complex, building a 70-unit apartment complex for seniors and providing
social services including youth employment training, after-school care, and summer day camps; and

WHEREAS, many community and state organizations have benefited from Pastor Alexander's expertise
and faithfulness including Martindale/Brightwood Weed and Seed, Martindale/Brightwood Community
Resurrection Partnership, Jobs Partnership of Greater Indianapolis, Indiana Healthy Marriage and Family
Coalition, and Teacher Training for the Indiana Baptist State Convention; and

WHEREAS, Alexander's reach extended outside of Indianapolis with Oasis Mission for Orphans,
Disabled and Unaccompanied Children (MODUC), a nonprofit started by Alexander in 1999 to support
orphaned children after the Liberian Civil War in which he partnered with Thomas and Mary Beh, who turned
their home into an orphanage after the conflict. This mission gave children access to education and medical
care; and

WHEREAS, because of the profound affect he had on so many in this community, the city of Indianapolis
proclaimed November 28, 2015, as Pastor Frank Alexander Day; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council does hereby honor the memory of Pastor Frank
Alexander by designating that portion of 25" Street from Hillside Avenue to Dr. Andrew J. Brown Avenue,
as Pastor Frank Alexander Memorial Way.

SECTION 2. As per Revised Code Section 431-405(a) this authorization shall expire fifteen (15) years after
it is passed by the City-County Council.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC
36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5!" day of May, 2025, at 7:33 p.m.

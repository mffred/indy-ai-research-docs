COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025 
 
 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 43, 2025 
Proposal No. 71, 2025 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Candace Harris as Chief Financial Officer  (CFO) for the City-
County Council of Indianapolis, Marion County. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  As required by Section 151-98 of the Revised Code of the City of Indianapolis – Marion 
County, the City-County Council, has considered for approval the recommendation of the committee on 
rules and public policy the appointment of Candace Harris for the position of CFO for the City-County 
Council. 
 
SECTION 2. The Council hereby approves the appointment of Candace Harris as the CFO for the 
Council office. 
 
SECTION 3.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3
rd day of March, 2025, at 7:31 p.m.

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022) by transferring and appropriating a total of ten n two hundred twenty-
three thousand four hundred sixty-four dollars ($10,223,464) for the purposes of the Department of Public
Works.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2023 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter
stated for purposes of the Department of Public Works.

SECTION 2. Fhe Department of Public Works requests a transfer of appropriations totaling two million two
hundred twenty-three thousand four hundred sixty-four dollars ($2,223,464) from character three to character
four in the Stormwater General Fund, additional appropriations totaling five hundred thousand dollars
($500,000) in character three in the Transportation General Fund, and additional appropriations totaling seven
million five hundred thousand dollars ($7,500,000) in character four in the Capital Asset Development &

Lifecycle Fund. The character increases and transfers will be utilized to finance additional DPW Capita
Improvement Plan needs, contribution to a local grant match for the Federal Reconnecting Communities Pilot
Program, to afford initial design and scoping costs for the Safe Streets and Roads for All Grant Program, and
to align existing appropriations with contractual costs:

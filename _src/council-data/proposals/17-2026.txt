CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring Greg Stowers, Jr. 
 
WHEREAS, Greg Stowers Jr. has served with distinction as Chief Administrative Officer and Policy 
Director for the Indianapolis City -County Council, overseeing day -to-day operations and providing 
strategic policy analysis to all 25 councilors; and 
 
WHEREAS, Greg has consistently brought people together across differences, engaging 
communities with authenticity and compassion while amplifying the voices of those who have too often 
been overlooked, reflecting a deep commitment to equity and inclusion in public service; and  
 
WHEREAS, Greg’s leadership extended beyond policy to the people who make government work. He 
invested deeply in staff development, fostering a culture of collaboration, mentorship, and professional 
growth. His guidance and encouragement have strengthened the Council team and prepared future 
leaders for continued success; and 
 
WHEREAS, his career reflects a deep dedication to civic leadership, beginning as an intern for 
President Barack Obama’s 2008 campaign, contributing to John Gregg’s 2016 gubernatorial campaign, 
and serving in the administration of Mayor Joe Hogsett; and 
 
WHEREAS, his influence extends beyond government, including leadership roles with Hashtag 
Lunchbag Indy, the Washington Township Schools Foundation, the Mitch Daniels Leadership Foundation, 
and Indy Hub’s 1828 Leadership Project, as well as his service as co -chair of the Community 
Engagement Committee for the 2024 NBA All-Star Game; and 
 
WHEREAS, Greg has been recognized as one of the Indianapolis Business Journal’s 2023 Forty 
Under 40 , a testament to his professional excellence, community impact, and dedication to creating a 
more equitable experience for all; and 
 
WHEREAS, as a lifelong and third-generation Hoosier, his upbringing rooted in faith, family, and 
community was shaped by his parents, Kim and Gregory Stowers, alongside his siblings David, Alex, 
Wesley, and Gabrielle, instilling values that continue to guide his life and service; now, therefore:

CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
 
I, Bryce Patz, Current Planning Administrator, Department of Metropolitan 
Development, certify that at the meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on July 1, 2026, a public hearing as required by law pursuant 
to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the “Indianapolis Star” and “Court and 
Commercial Record” newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance 
amendment prayed for in petition:  
   
2026-ZON-035 7323 South Mooresville Road (Approximate Address) 
PROP NO. 265, 2026 Decatur Township, Council District #21 
R.O. No. 76, 2026 Redwood Apartment Neighborhoods, by Clark Quinn Law 
Rezoning of 21.66 acres from the D-A (FW) (FF) district to the D-6 (FW) 
(FF) district to provide for residential uses. 
 
2026-ZON-040 2422 & 2430 Prospect Street (Approximate Addresses) 
PROP NO. 266, 2026 Center Township, Council District #18 
R.O. No. 77, 2026 Terra Resources, LLC, by Antonio Amaryllis aka Jonathan Padilla 
Rezoning of 0.23 acres (total across two noncontiguous lots) from the I-3 to 
the D-5 to provide for residential uses. 
 
2026-CZN-823  4240 Bluff Road (Approximate Address) 
PROP NO. 267, 2026 Perry Township, Council District #22 
R.O. No. 78, 2026 Otis James & Tonya Haimes, by David Gilman 
Rezoning of 1.14 acres from the SU-7 district to the D-1 district to provide 
for residential uses.  
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance 
prayed for in said above-listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, by a majority of its members, adopted, approved, and 
recommended 2026-ZON-035, 2026-ZON-040, and 2026-CZN-823 to the City-County 
Council for adoption to the end that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety 
from fire, flood, and other danger may be secured, that property values may be preserved 
and that the public health, comfort, morals, and convenience and general welfare may be 
preserved.

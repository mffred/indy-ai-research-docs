CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 19, 2023
Proposal No. 134, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Haitian Association of Indiana.

WHEREAS, during May, we recognize the strong historical connections between Haiti and the United
States, which include the international celebration of Haitian Flag Day on May 18". Beyond geographical
proximity, Haiti’s influence is evidenced throughout the American timeline, beginning with the American
Revolution, where 50 heroic Haitian “gens de couleur libre’, or free men of color, volunteered to fight
alongside American troops. Additionally, Haitian-Americans have made instrumental contributions in major
U.S. cities, including Miami, New York, Boston, New Orleans, Orlando, and Chicago; and

WHEREAS, today, many Haitian-American residents maintain their rich cultural identities and native
language, Kreyol, in celebration of those original Haitian immigrants who have settled throughout the United
States in search of better lives and opportunities for their children; and

WHEREAS, more than one million individuals of Haitian descent now reside throughout the United
States, and Indianapolis is one of the fastest growing metropolitan areas in the country for Hal
populations. Hoosiers with Haitian heritage are prevalent in every sector of Marion County, including
successful educators, business owners, homeowners, healthcare professionals, laborers, engineers,
students, and religious leaders; and

WHEREAS, this week, the City of indianapolis joins the Haitian Association of Indiana in celebration of
the distinct Haitian culture and heritage that exists in residents of our great city and remains part of our
collective American history; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Haitian Association of Indiana.

SECTION 2. The Council also acknowledges the cultural contributions of Haitian-Americans in celebration
of Haitian Flag Day.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4, This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8" day of May, 2023, at 7:52 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 89, 2025
Proposal No, 321, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Leslie R. Schulte to the Board of Public Works.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Public Works, the Council reappoints:

Leslie R. Schulte
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2026. The
person reappointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council, Upon expiration of the

term, the reappointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to
exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 34 day of November, 2025, at 7:39 p.m.

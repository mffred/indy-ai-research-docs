CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 6, 2023
Proposal No. 51, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Aretha Britton, Indiana’s 2022 Elementary Principal of the Year.

WHEREAS, with a Bachelor of Science degree from Indiana State University, Aretha Britton began her
teaching career in 1997 at Benjamin Franklin Elementary School (Vigo County Schools) where she taught
fifth, third, and a split second/third grade class, as well as serving as a reading recovery teacher and a
writing coach through 2002, taking her Master of Science degree in Elementary Education/Early Childhood
in 2001; and

WHEREAS, from 2002 to 2005, Aretha served as a Title | reading teacher and reading coach at
Rhoades Elementary School (MSD of Wayne Township — Marion County). After earning both
administrative licensure and educational leadership licensure at indiana State in 2004, she became
assistant principal at Rhoades Elementary until 2007 and then at North Wayne Elementary (Wayne
Township) from 2007 to 2009. Aretha served as principal of North Wayne from 2009 to 2018 and is
currently principal of Rhoades Elementary; and

WHEREAS, the Indiana Association of School Principals ([ASP) recognized Aretha Britton, Principal at
Rhoades Elementary School in MSD of Wayne Township, as the 2022 State Elementary Principal of the
Year. Britton was chosen by her peers as one of 12 District Elementary Principals of the Year, representing
District 7, including all schools in Marion County. From these 12 district winners, Britton was honored as
the State Elementary School Principai of the Year and will be submitted for consideration to be the National
Principal of the Year; and

WHEREAS, Aretha was honored at the annual IASP awards banquet, a part of the IASP’s annual Fall
Professional Conference, in recognition of her distinguished leadership and extraordinary service; and

WHEREAS, proud of her focus on mental well-being during the pandemic, Aretha points to mental
health wellness coaching for students and a staff relaxation station, which included ten-to-twenty-
minute massages for staff, and

WHEREAS, Ms. Britton is a dedicated educator who is adored by the Rhoades community. Her
leadership and service have positively impacted the Wayne community for more than 20 years; now,
therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes Aretha Britton and congratulates
her on being chosen as 2022 Elementary Principal of the Year.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily thanks Ms. Britton for her dedication to educating students and serving
her community and the City of Indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6 day of February, 2023, at 7:15 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 1, 2023
Proposal No. 1, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the work of community partners and City agencies for their efforts
toward achieving significant violence reduction in 2022.

WHEREAS, in 2021 the Council appropriated the first tranche of American Rescue Plan funding to kick
start the Mayor’s 3-year violence reduction strategy which began in 2022; and

WHEREAS, this historic investment delivered a total of $150 million for the implementation of a data-
driven Gun Violence Reduction Strategy, the creation of the 50 person Peacemaker program, and the $45
million Elevation Grant program for grassroots organizations managed and distributed by the Indianapolis
Foundation; and

WHEREAS, through the Gun Violence Reduction Strategy, the City has been able to identify the
individuals and groups at the highest risk of being involved in gun violence and have been able to deploy
effective interventions with these individuals through the Indy Peace Fellowship; and

WHEREAS, from the Violence Interrupters responding to shooting scenes to mediate and develop
relationships with individuals in conflict, to the Outreach Workers who identify the very high risk individuals
to connect them with the Life Coaches who help the fellows develop and implement Life Plans, the City’s
Peacemakers have utilized their experience, knowledge and dedication to our communities to change the
es of our citizens; and

WHEREAS, in 2022, this team of committed individuals interrupted 654 conflicts, provided support at
310 shooting scenes, enrolled 99 individuals in the fellowship, attended over 2,000 community events, and
referred 1,254 non-fellow community members to services; and

WHEREAS, additional grassroots supports have come by the way of the Elevation Grant program, with
$14 million awarded in 2022 to over 70 organizations, with future award rounds in 2023 and 2024; and

WHEREAS, the City’s efforts extend to other 2022 Operational Budget items like the continued
investments in the $300,000 Community-Based Violence Prevention Partnership with this year's awards
going toward New B.O.Y., Brightwood Community Center, and VOICES; and

WHEREAS, as a part of the ARPA distribution, $9 million has been dedicated to the investment in
modern policing technology for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department which has seen the
successful deployment of 6 new public safety cameras that can be moved throughout the County and over
200 license plate readers; and

WHEREAS, through the steadfast efforts by the Peacemakers, law enforcement, and community
partners, our community is starting to see the benefits of these investments; and

WHEREAS, just after its first year of implementation of the 3-year strategy, the Mayor announced on
January 1 that the City has seen the largest single-year reduction in criminal homicides since the creation
of IMPD, the lowest level of deadly violence since 2019; and

WHEREAS, it’s important to recognize the work dedicated by so many and this achievement however
our efforts must not stop now—our communities deserve to see continued years of significant reduction of
violence and to be without the risk of suffering from violence; now, therefore

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council thanks the community partners, law enforcement, and City agencies
for their work and dedication to making our City a better place for all.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council remembers and grieves the losses of the individuals in our
community who have been lost due to senseless acts of violence.

SECTION 3. The City-County Council commits to continue to make investments that benefit our
communities and make them a safer, healthier place.

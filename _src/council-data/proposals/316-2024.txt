CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 69, 2024
Proposal No. 316, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Karin Sarratt to the Economic Enhancement District Board.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Economic Enhancement District Board, the Council appoints:

Karin Sarratt
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025. The person
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the
appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the

holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7th day of October, 2024, at 7:07 p.m.

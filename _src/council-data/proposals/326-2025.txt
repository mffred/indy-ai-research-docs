CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
 
I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Current Planning Administrator, Department of Metropolitan 
Development, certify that at the meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on September 17, 2025, a public hearing as required by law 
pursuant to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and 
“Court and Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, 
Indiana, said Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning 
ordinance amendment prayed for in petition:  
 
 
2025-ZON-066  6470 West 10th Street (Approximate Address)   
Prop No. 323, 2025 Wayne Township, Council District #16 
R.O. No. 110, 2025 Sangar Estate, LLC, by Russell L. Brown 
Rezoning of 0.55-acre from the D-3 (W-5) district to the C-4 (W-5) 
district to provide for community-regional commercial uses. 
 
2025-ZON-080 3350 North German Church Road (Approximate Address)   
Prop No. 324, 2025 (Amended) 
R.O. No. 111, 2025 Warren Township, Council District #15 
Hindu Temple of Central Indiana, Inc. 
Rezoning of 28.49 acres from the D-A (FF), SU-1, and SU-38 districts 
to the SU-1 (FF) district to provide for religious uses. 
 
2025-ZON-083 441 South Rural Street (Approximate Address)   
Prop No. 325, 2025 Center Township, Council District #18 
R.O. No. 112, 2025 BECA and Associates, LLC, by Josh Smith 
Rezoning of 0.108-acre from the C-4 district to the D-5 district to 
provide for a remodel and addition to an existing detached single-
family dwelling.  
 
2025-ZON-087  200 North Rural Street (Approximate Address)   
Prop No. 326, 2025 Center Township, Council District # 13 
R.O. No. 113, 2025 Englewood Community Development Corporation, by Joseph D. 
Calderon 
Rezoning of 0.22-acre from the D-5 (TOD) district to the D-9 (TOD) 
district to provide for a medium apartment development.  
 
2025-CZN-830 9110 and 9150 West 10th Street (Approximate Addresses)    
Prop No. 327, 2025 Wayne Township, Council District #16 
R.O. No. 114, 2025 Raceway Development Partners, by Misha Rabinowitch

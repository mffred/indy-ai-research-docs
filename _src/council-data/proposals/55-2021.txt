CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 2, 2021
Proposal No. 55, 2021

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION determining the need to lease approximately 40,750
square feet of space located at 3737 E. Washington St., Indianapolis, Indiana for use by Marion County for
and on behalf of the Marion County Election Board.

WHEREAS, Marion County for and on behaif of the Marion County Election Board entered into a lease
in March 2010 for certain real estate at 3737 E. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN in March 2010 (hereafter
“Lease”); and

WHEREAS, the term of the Lease is set to expire in March 2021 and the parties desire to modify the
Lease and the modifications include, among other things, an extension of the Lease for an additional four
(4) years; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council, pursuant to indiana Code § 36-1-10-7, has investigated the
conditions concerning the lease amendment and hereby determines that the lease of approximately 40,750
square feet of space for use by the Marion County Election Board, is needed.

SECTION 2. The property to be leased is approximately located at 3737 E. Washington St., Indianapolis,
Indiana and the owner pursuant to Revised Cade 151-66 is IEH Sherman Drive, LLC successor in interest
to Icahn Enterprises Holdings, L.P. d/b/a American Entertainment.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC. § 36-
3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 15" day of March, 2021, at 9:06 p.m.

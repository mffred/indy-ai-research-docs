CITY-COUNTY GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 38, 2023
Proposal No. 263, 2023

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE to add Chapter 121, Article IX, County Option Circuit
Breaker Tax Credit, to the Revised Code.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Chapter 121 of the "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” regarding taxation,
hereby is amended to add Article IX, “County Option Circuit Breaker Tax Credit,” reading as follows:

ARTICLE IX —- COUNTY OPTION CIRCUIT BREAKER TAX CREDIT
Sec. 121-901. - Purpose; Designation of Neighborhood Enhancement District(s).

(a) Indiana Code Chapter 6-1.1-49 authorizes a county fiscal body fo adopt an ordinance to provide a
County Option Circuit Breaker Tax Credit (“Tax Credit”) to certain qualified individuals, as defined by statute,
to defray a portion of the tax burden imposed by year-to-year increases in assessed value. The statute
authorizes a county fiscal body to apply the Tax Credit either in the entire county, or in one or more
“neighborhood enhancement districts.” The statute imposes certain other parameters for the Tax Credit. The
City-County Council desires to implement the Tax Credit in accordance with such statutory parameters, with
the effect of limiting year-over-year property tax increases to 4% for qualified individuals residing in the
neighborhood enhancement district defined below.

(b) The City-County Council finds that the Riverside Neighborhood is appropriate for
designation as a Neighborhood Enhancement District, in light of the impact that increases in
assessed value have had on longtime residents of the neighborhood in recent years, and so
designates it, consistent with IC 6-1.1-49-2. For purposes of this Article, the Riverside
Neighborhood shall be defined generally as the residential parcels within the boundaries of:

The White River at 16" Street north to 165; i65 east and south to the aqueduct; the
aqueduct west and south to 16" Street; and 16" Street west to the White River, as is further
depicted in the diagram on Attachment A and the parcel list on Attachment B, both
incorporated by reference herein.

(c} The Council reserves the right fo expand or reduce the neighborhood enhancement district
described in subsection (b)} above, or to designate additional neighborhood enhancement districts. Pursuant
fo IC 6-1.1-49-4(h), the same income thresholds, credit amounts, and eligibility requirements for the Tax
Credit shail apply in the neighborhood enhancement district or districts designated under this section.

Sec. 121-902. — Application; Expiration.

This Article is effective January 1, 2024. This Article expires on December 31, 2027 and the Tax Credit
described herein shall not apply for property taxes first due and payable after December 31, 2027.

Sec. 121-903. - Definitions.

Benchmark Year means the calendar year preceding by two (2) years the calendar year in which the
property taxes to which the Tax Credit would apply are first due and payable.

Homestead means a residential property qualifying for a standard deduction under IC 6-1.1-12-37.
Joint Income Cap means the dollar amount, if any, defined as such in Section 121-904(d) below.

Neighborhood Enhancement District means any geographic area so designated by the Council in
Section 121-901, consistent with IC 6-1.1-49-2.

Qualified individual means an individual who:

(1) Has received a standard deduction granted under IC 6-1.1-12-37 for the individual's Homestead
property, located in a Neighborhood Enhancement District, in the immediately preceding calendar
year (or was married at the time of death to a deceased spouse who qualified for a standard
deduction granted under IC 6-1.1-12-37 for the individual's Homestead property, located in a
Neighborhood Enhancement District, in the immediately preceding calendar year);

(2) Is receiving a standard deduction granted under IC 6-1.1-12-37 for the same Homestead property in
the current calendar year;

(3) Has lived in that Homestead for at least ten (10) years on or before December 31 of the calendar
year immediately preceding the current calendar year;

(4) Is fifty-five (55) years of age or older on or before December 31 of the calendar year preceding the
year in which the credit is claimed; and

(5) Had a gross income (as defined in Section 62 of the Internal Revenue Code), in the Benchmark
Year, not exceeding:

a. The Single Income Cap, for an individual who filed an individual/single tax return with
respect to income earned in the Benchmark Year.
b. The Joint Income Cap, for an individual who filed a tax return jointly with the individuals
spouse with respect to income earned in the Benchmark Year.
Single income Cap means the dollar amount, if any, defined as such in Section 121-904(c) below.

Tax Credit means the County Option Circuit Breaker Tax Credit described in IC Chapter 6-1.1-49, as
implemented by this Article.

Sec. 121-904. — Tax Credit Adopted.

a) Pursuant to IC 6-1.1-49-4, a Tax Credit shall be provided against a Qualified Individual’s property tax
lability, in accordance with statute and the terms and conditions stated in this Section.

(b} The percentage of increase on a Qualified Individual’s property tax liability in a particular year
compared to the prior year that is to be used in determining the amount of the Tax Credit, pursuant to the
formula set forth in IC 6-1.1-49-7, shall be four percent (4.0%).

(c) No Single Income Cap shail apply.

(d) No Joint Income Cap shall apply.

(e) To obtain the credit for a desired calendar year in which property taxes are first due and payable, the
required application must be completed and dated in the immediately preceding calendar year and filed with
the county auditor on or before January 5 of the calendar year in which the property taxes are first due and

payable.

(f} All other terms and conditions set forth in IC Chapter 6-1.1-49 shall be observed by the Auditor and
all other relevant City or County officials in implementing the Tax Credi

SECTION 2. Pursuant to IC 6-1.1-49-5, the Clerk of the Council is directed to give notice, within fifteen (15)
days after the adoption of this ordinance, to:

a. The Department of Local Government Finance, on the form and in the manner prescribed by
the Department;

b. The Auditor; and

c. The fiscal officer of each taxing unit within the Neighborhood Enhancement District defined
by this ordinance.

Such notice shall include a certified copy of the adopted ordinance.

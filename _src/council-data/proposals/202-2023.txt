CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 29, 2023
Proposal! No. 202, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve and authorize the execution of an amendment
to an existing concession agreement with a concessionaire for the operation, maintenance and
improvement of the metered parking system of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana.

WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) entered into a concession agreement with
Parkindy, LLC (the “Concessionaire”) on December 22, 2010, for the operation, maintenance and
improvement of the City’s metered parking system; and

WHEREAS, the City believes that the concession agreement with the Concessionaire (the
“Agreement’) should be amended to entitle the Concessionaire, for a trial period of approximately two years,
fo receive Parking Enforcement Revenue for citations issued by the Concessionaire in respect of the
sections of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City of Indianapolis and Marion County (the “Revised
Code”) and the State of Indiana Code (the “Indiana Code”) laid out in the table located at Section 4(b) of
Schedule 5 of the Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the City believes that the Agreement should further be amended to add a row containing
Ordinance Code 441-373 (Parking in bicycle paths or lanes) to the table of sections of the Revised Code
and the Indiana Code located at Section 4(b) of Schedule 5 of the Agreement such that Concessionaire
shall be entitled to enforce violation of said ordinance; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council desires to adopt this Resolution and authorize the appropriate
officers of the City to execute the amendment to the Agreement in the form attached hereto as “Exhibit A’;
now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. —_ The City-County Council hereby (i) approves the substantially final form of the amendment
to the Agreement in the form attached hereto as “Exhibit A’, and (ii) authorizes the appropriate officers of
the City to execute and deliver the amendment to the Agreement with such changes to “Exhibit A” as are
reasonably necessary upon the approval of the Corporation Counsel as to form and legality, and to take all
actions and to execute all documents necessary or appropriate consistent with the terms of the Agreement.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council in compliance
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14" day of August, 2023, at 8:23 p.m.

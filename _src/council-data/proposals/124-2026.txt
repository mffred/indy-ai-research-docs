Proposal No. 124, 2026 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City‑County Annual Budget for 2026 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 12, 2025) by an additional appropriation totaling forty-two thousand dollars ($42,000) for the purposes 
of the Marion County Recorder’s Office. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY‑COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County Annual Budget for 2026 is hereby amended by the character increase hereinafter stated for purposes 
of the following departments and agencies. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Marion County Recorder’s Office requests an additional appropriation of forty -two 
thousand dollars ($42,000) in the County Recorder’s Perpetuation Fund in character three to be used for 
property fraud and Veterans honors advertisement , including non- political billboards, radio, and TV 
advertisements.

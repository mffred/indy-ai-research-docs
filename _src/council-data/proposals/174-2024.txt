CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 2, 2024
Proposal No. 174, 2024

A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2024 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance
No. 14, 2023) by the transfers and additional appropriations listed herein for the purposes of those
departments and agencies listed below.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the City-
County- Annual Budget for 2024 is hereby amended by the character increases and transfers hereinafter
stated for purposes of the following departments and agencies.

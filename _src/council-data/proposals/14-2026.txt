CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 3, 2026
Proposal No. 14, 2026

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to approve and authorize the execution of an amendment
to an existing franchise agreement with a franchise holder for the operation, maintenance and improvement
of the metered parking system of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana.

WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) entered into a concession agreement with
ParkIndy, LLC (the “Concessionaire”) on December 22, 2010, for the operation, maintenance and
improvement of the City’s metered parking system; and

WHEREAS, the City previously executed a Fourth Amendment to the concession agreement with the
Concessionaire (the “Agreement”) which entitled the Concessionaire, for a trial period of approximately two
years, to receive Parking Enforcement Revenue for citations issued by the Concessionaire in respect of the
sections of the Revised Code of the Consolidated City of Indianapolis and Marion County (the “Revised
Code”) and the State of Indiana Code (the “Indiana Code”) laid out in the table located at Section 4(b) of
Schedule 5 of the Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the City believes that the Agreement should be further amended so that Concessionaire
is entitled to continue to receive Parking Enforcement Revenue for such citations issued, beyond the trial
period and for the remainder of the term of the Agreement, with such entitlement subject to cancellation
and revocation by the City in its sole discretion at any time; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council desires to adopt this Resolution and authorize the appropriate
officers of the City to execute the amendment to the Agreement in the form attached hereto as “Exhibit A”;
now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: .

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby (i) approves the substantially final form of the amendment to
the Agreement in the form attached hereto as “Exhibit A’, and (ii) authorizes the appropriate officers of the
City to execute and deliver the amendment to the Agreement with such changes to “Exhibit A” as are
reasonably necessary upon the approval of the Corporation Counsel as to form and legality, and to take all
actions and to execute all documents necessary or appropriate consistent with the terms of the Agreement.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council in compliance with
Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2nd day of February, 2026, at 7:38 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 19, 2026
Proposal No. 306, 2026

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION establishing that the City-County Council of the City of
Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana, is interested in making the purchase of specified land.

WHEREAS, the City-County Council of the: City of Indianapolis and’ Marion County, Indiana (“City-
County Council’) is the fiscal body of the City of Indianapolis pursuant to Indiana Code § 36-1-2-6; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to:Indiana Code § 36-1-10:5-1, et seq., the City of Indianapolis may purchase
land for a total price exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) only after the City-County Council,
as the fiscal body, passes a resolution to the effect that it is interested in making a purchase of specified
land and has provided a copy of both appraisals to the fiscal body; and

WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis, through its Indianapolis Fire Department (“IFD”) wishes to
purchase fee simple title to the real estate with a common address on the property record card of 5490
West Pike Plaza Road, Indianapolis, IN 46254 (parcel number 6010384) and depicted in Exhibit “A’,
attached hereto and incorporated herein (Real Estate”); and

WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis wishes to purchase the Real Estate for the construction of a new
firehouse to replace IFD Station #33; and

WHEREAS, it is anticipated that the cost of the parcel of land that the City of Indianapolis is interested
in acquiring is greater than twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000); and

WHEREAS, as per Indiana Code § 36-1-10.5(4) the Council has been provided with a copy of both
appraisals, and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council, having considered the acquisition of the Real Estate and being
duly advised, finds that the City-County Council has an interest in acquiring the Real Estate; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby establishes that it has an interest in acquiring the Real Estate
with a parcel number of 6010384 and a common address’on the property record card of 5490 West Pike

Plaza Road, Indianapolis, IN 46254. An aerial map of this acquisition is depicted: on Exhibit “A”.

SECTION 2. For purposes of § 151-66 of the Revised Code of Indianapolis and Marion County, said parcel
is owned by William B Yarmuth, John A Yarmuth, Robert N Yarmuth, and Fran Y Samuels.

SECTION 3. The City-County Council, being duly advised and pursuant to Indiana Code § 36-1-10.5-5,
hereby expresses its approval for the City to purchase said parcel of land.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption by the Council and in compliance
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 19th day of October, 2026, at 8:30 p.m.

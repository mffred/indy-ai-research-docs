CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.     , 2025 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizes the Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated, Alpha Mu Omega 
Chapter, and declares December 1, 2025 as “Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. Day” at City Hall . 
 
WHEREAS, Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated ®, was founded on January 15, 1908, with a 
mission to promote unity and friendship among college women, to study and help alleviate problems 
concerning girls and women in order to improve their social stature, to maintain a progressive interest in 
college life, and to be of “Service to All Mankind.” and,  
 
WHEREAS, in 1915, the sorority hosted its first political action conference with Illinois Congressman 
and human rights activist Martin B. Madden as the speaker; and, 
 
WHEREAS, through the years, the sorority's function has become more complex, branching out to 
engage members worldwide in advocacy and service projects to improve socioeconomic, racial, political,  
and environmental conditions; and,  
 
WHEREAS, Alpha Kappa Alpha's diverse, collaborative work throughout the United States, the 
Caribbean, Europe, Africa, and  United Arab Emirates integrates a high level of personal engagement 
with the strong bonds of sisterhood and a productive network of community partners; and,  
 
WHEREAS, on December 1, 2025, Central Indiana members of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, 
Incorporated®, will participate in “AKA Day at the City Hall ”; and, 
 
WHEREAS, the event is an opportunity for members to speak with local lawmakers, engage in the 
local City-County Council meeting, advocate on a local level for critical issues, including mental health 
and wellness, Black maternal health, infrastructure, economic development, criminal justice reform, etc.; 
and,  
 
WHEREAS, the Central Region is home to 95 empowered and engaged chapters serving Illinois, 
Indiana, Kentucky, Minnesota, Southeastern Missouri and Wisconsin; and  
 
WHEREAS, Indiana is home to 22 Alpha Kappa Alpha chapters, 8 which are located on college and 
university campuses and have 14 active alumnae groups in communities throughout the state; and

WHEREAS, there are nearly 1,000 active members of Alpha Kappa Alpha in the city of Indianapolis, 
representing the Kappa, Alpha Mu Omega, and Chi Chi Omega chapters, respectively; and   
 
WHEREAS, there are many members of Alpha Kappa Alpha currently serving in Marion County and 
Central Indiana, working for their community in local government, now, therefore,  
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  The Indianapolis City -County Council proudly recognizes the Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, 
Incorporated, Alpha Mu Omega Chapter, and declares December 1, 2025 as “Alpha Kappa Alpha 
Sorority, Inc. Day” at City Hall. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Council further thanks Alpha Kappa Alpha for its many years of service to the 
community, and directs that a copy of this resolution be transmitted to Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. ®, 
Alpha Mu Omega and Chi Chi Omega Chapters, in recognition of all Indianapolis chapters.  
 
SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.  
 
SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-4-
14.

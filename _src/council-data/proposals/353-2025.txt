CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 17, 2025
Proposal No. 353, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the 40" Anniversary of the Indianapolis Children’s Choir

WHEREAS, the Indianapolis Children’s Choir (ICC), founded in 1986 by Henry Leck, has served as a
beacon of musical excellence and youth development in Central Indiana for four decades; and

WHEREAS, under the visionary leadership of Executive Artistic Director Joshua Pedde, the ICC has
continued to nurture and inspire over 2,500 young people annually, ranging in age from 18 months to 18
years, through its diverse and inclusive music education programs; and

WHEREAS, the ICC’s programs—including early childhood, community outreach, and performing
choirs—provide transformative experiences for children across nearly 20 counties, representing over 360
schools and 56 school icts; and

WHEREAS, the ICC’s commitment to accessibility and community outreach is demonstrated through
its regional choirs in Boone, Hamilton, Hendricks, Johnson, and Marion counties, as well as its partnerships
with arts, civic, and educational institutions; and

WHEREAS, the ICC has earned national and international acclaim, performing at prestigious venues
and events including the 2024 U.S. Olympic Swimming Trials, Super Bowl XLVI, the 100th running of the
Indianapolis 500, and for dignitaries such as Nobel Peace Prize Laureate Archbishop Desmond Tutu; and

WHEREAS, the ICC has enriched the cultural fabric of Indianapolis through performances at
gubernatorial inaugurations, national conferences, and collaborations with world-renowned artists and
orchestras, while also producing award-winning recordings and broadcasts; and

WHEREAS, the ICC transforms lives through powerful choral experiences and music education,
empowering young voices to grow, connect, and lead on stage and in the community; and

WHEREAS, the 2025 season, themed “Magical Moments,” celebrates the ICC’s 40 years of artistic
excellence, community impact, and dedication to shaping the lives of young people through the power of
music; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianapolis City-County Council recognizes the 40 Anniversary of the Indianapolis
Children’s Choir.

SECTION 2. The Council celebrates the extraordinary contributions of the Indianapolis Children’s Choir to
our city, our youth, and our shared cultural heritage.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3rd day of November, 2025, at 7:11 p.m.

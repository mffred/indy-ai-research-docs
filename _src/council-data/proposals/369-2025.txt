CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 19, 2025
Proposal No. 369, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION honoring the 10th Anniversary of The Exchange at the Indianapolis Urban
League and celebrating its National Recognition at the 2025 National Urban League Conference.

WHEREAS, 2025 marks the 10th anniversary of The Exchange at the Indianapolis Urban League, the
young professional auxiliary of the Indianapolis Urban League, which for a decade has empowered young
Black professionals through civic engagement, leadership development, and service to the Indianapolis
community; and

WHEREAS, in recognition of its remarkable impact, The Exchange was honored at the 2025 National
Urban League Conference with three distinguished awards: the President of the Year Award, the Robert
Ingram National Day of Service Award, and the Hugh B. Price National Chapter of Excellence Award. These
recognitions underscore the chapter's leadership, innovation, and service; and

WHEREAS, President Kelly Doucet was named President of the Year for her strategic leadership,
mentorship, and stewardship of the Exchange Leadership Fellowship—contributing more than 680 hours
to the chapter's mission, guiding three cohorts of fellows, and inspiring excellence across the national
movement; and

WHEREAS, The Exchange earned the Robert Ingram National Day of Service Award for its impactful
2024 service initiative, which advanced youth literacy by collecting and donating over 900 books, hosting a
joyful Reading Hour with local students, and collaborating with WNBA legend Tamika Catchings to
celebrate reading as a tool of empowerment; and

WHEREAS, The Exchange was recognized with the Hugh B. Price Chapter of Excellence Award—the
highest honor for a young professionals chapter—after reaching over 700 young professionals, delivering
more than 2,000 hours of leadership development, and executing programs that expanded civic education,
policy advocacy, and community impact across Indianapolis; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly celebrates The Exchange at the Indianapolis
Urban League on its 10th anniversary and commends its national recognition at the 2025 National Urban
League Conference.

SECTION 2. The Council recognizes the extraordinary contributions of The Exchange, which stands as a
model of visionary leadership, transformative service, and the collective power of young Black professionals
to shape a more just and vibrant future for Indianapolis and beyond.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 3rd day of November, 2025, at 7:26 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 5, 2022
Proposal No. 25, 2022

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Reese Hamilton.

WHEREAS, Reese Hamilton was born on June 28, 2008, in Indianapolis. He is a student at Center for
Inquiry School 70, where he shows excellent leadership among his fellow students; and

WHEREAS, after seeing police officers in his neighborhood due to a crime committed, Reese realized
that violence was getting closer to home. He was afraid of the violence that was occurring in the city, but
at just eight years old, Reese knew that he could do something to change it; and

WHEREAS, in 2016, Reese wrote a letter to Mayor Hogsett explaining his concerns, and together, they
worked on a plan to help children feel safe; and

WHEREAS, in October 2016, Reese led his first Peace March through his neighborhood, where various
community leaders including Mayor Hogsett, joined him; and

WHEREAS, Reese’s annual efforts include the Bike Ride for Peace, two giveaways at Military Park for
those in need, the Thanksgiving giveaway, and his most recent act of kindness has been coliecting stocking
stuffers for homeless people and neighborhoods in need; and

WHEREAS, Reese has received support from his community to continue his work in the city and, most
importantly, support from his family, including his mother and his big brother; and

WHEREAS, Reese Hamilton’s involvement and dedication have been recognized by numerous news
networks and awards. Today he is celebrated for his work in the community; now therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council recognizes Reese Hamilton for his willingness to serve the city and
wishes him success in his future endeavors.

SECTION 2. The Mayor is

ited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7" day of February, 2022, at 7:20 p.m.

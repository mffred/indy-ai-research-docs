CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 5, 2026
Proposal No. 35, 2026

APROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL RESOLUTION determining the need for the Marion County Election Board
to lease approximately 57,000 square feet of office space at 51 Koweba Lane, Indianapolis, IN 46201 to
relocate the Election Service Center.

WHEREAS, the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis and Marion County, indiana (“City-
County Council’) is the fiscal body of the City of Indianapolis pursuant to Ind. Code 36-1-2-6; and

WHEREAS, the Marion County Election Board (“MCEB”) is responsible for the administration of
elections in Marion County; and

WHEREAS, the MCEB requires a facility to (1) securely store and maintain critical election systems;
(2) train poll workers; (3) distribute voting machines and supplies; and (4) manage ail voting operations for
the County; and

WHEREAS, the MCEB wishes to lease real estate (as depicted in Exhibit A, attached hereto and
incorporated herein) located at 51 Koweba Lane, Indianapolis, IN 46201 from 51 KOWEBA, LLC for the
above-stated purposes; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Ind. Code 36-1-10-7, if the annual cost of a proposed lease is $250,000 or
more, the leasing agent (the MCEB) cannot lease a structure unless it receives: (1) a petition signed by fifty
(80) or more taxpayers of Marion County; and (2) the fiscal body (the City-County Council) determines,
after investigation, that the structure is needed; and

WHEREAS, the MCEB has obtained the requisite taxpayer petition and signatures required; and

WHEREAS, the City-County Council, having considered the lease of the structure, conducting an
appropriate investigation into the need of the structure, and being duly advised, finds that the structure is
needed by the MCEB; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 14. The City-County Council hereby determines that a structure for the Marion County Election
Board’s Election Service Center is needed, that the structure located at 51 Koweba Lane, Indianapolis, IN
46201 is adequate, and approves the lease of the structure by the Marion County Election Board from 51
KOWEBA, LLC.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2nd day of March, 2026, at 7:36 p.m.

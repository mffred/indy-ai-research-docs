CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 1, 2025
Proposal No. 50, 2025

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE approving the 2025 Budget of the Downtown Economic
Enhancement District.

WHEREAS, General Ordinance No. 24, 2024 (Proposal No. 201, 2024) established the Downtown
Economic Enhancement District (EED); and

WHEREAS, Section 121-1105 of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County” provides
that the EED Board is to prepare and submit a proposed budget governing the Board’s projected
expenditures from the Economic Enhancement District project fund; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-7-40-7(a) and Section 121-1105 of the Revised Code, the Council
may approve, modify, or reject the EED Board’s proposed budget; and

WHEREAS, the EED Board has now prepared and submitted a proposed budget for calendar year
2025 to the Council; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF
THE CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY,
INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Pursuant to IC 36-7-40-7(a) and Section 121-1105 of the “Revised Code of the
Consolidated City and County,” the Downtown Economic Enhancement District Board has submitted
its proposed budget for calendar year 2025. The proposed budget is attached hereto and made a part
hereof, marked as Exhibit A. The total proposed budget is Four Million Five Hundred and Thirty
Thousand Dollars ($4,530,000).

SECTION 2. The Council hereby approves the proposed budget submitted by the Downtown
Economic Enhancement District for calendar year 2025.

SECTION 3. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with
IC 36-3-4-14.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.  , 2024 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION in support of the immediate implementation of the U.S.-backed ceasefire plan. 
 
WHEREAS, the City-County Council of Indianapolis and Marion County (Council) condemns all forms 
of antisemitism, Islamophobia, racism, and bigotry; and  
 
WHEREAS, the Council advocates for the dignity and safety of all people; and 
 
WHEREAS, since October of 2023, Indianapolis community members and IU -Indianapolis students, 
faculty and staff have called for a ceasefire in Gaza and divestment from Israel with thousands gathering 
to raise awareness of the growing death toll in Gaza; and  
 
WHEREAS, on June 10, 2024, the United Nations Security Council endorsed a U.S. -backed 
ceasefire proposal to end the war in Gaza, which included a complete ceasefire, the withdrawal of Israeli 
forces from Gaza, the release of Israeli hostages in exchange for thousands of Palestinian prisoners, a 
surge of humanitarian aid into Gaza, and the allowance of displaced Palestinians to return to their homes 
in northern Gaza; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Council condemns the widespread killing of innocent civilians by the Israeli Defense 
Forces in Gaza since the October 7, 2023, attack and notes that as of June 25, 2024, more than 38,129 
Palestinians (civilians and combatants) have been killed, and the UN reports 20,000 children are buried, 
trapped, detained or lost in Gaza; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Council opposes all violence that leads to the loss of innocent civilian lives and 
ongoing human suffering in Palestine; and  
 
WHEREAS, hundreds of thousands of civilians in Gaza lack access to food, water, medicine and 
other basic necessities of life, with the World Health Organization reporting widespread child malnutrition, 
lack of potable water, and rampant levels of disease as well as imminent famine; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA 
 
SECTION 1.  The City -County Council of Indianapolis and Marion County calls for and supports the 
immediate implementation of the U.S.-backed ceasefire plan and the withholding of all offensive weapons 
to Israel, in order to uphold our values as a peaceful democracy, and to send a clear message of support 
to the people of Indianapolis and anyone else affected by the violence overseas; and

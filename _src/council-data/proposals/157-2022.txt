boundaries.

SECTION 1. Chapter 152 of the "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County" be, and is hereby,
amended by adopting a new Chapter 152 to read as follows:

CHAPTER 152
COUNCIL DISTRICT BOUNDARIES

ARTICLE I. DISTRICTS ESTABLISHED

Sec. 152-101. Districts. Pursuant to 1C 36-3-4-2 and IC 36-3-4-3, the City-County Council consists of
twenty-five (25) members which are elected from single member districts.

Sec. 152-102. Reserved.

Sec. 152-103. Single member districts. The county is divided into twenty-five (25) single member districts,
from each of which one member shall be elected by the voters in that district. The boundaries of each
district are set forth in Secs. 152-201 through 152-225 of this chapter.

Sec. 152-104. Precincts. The references to precincts by township and precinct numbers listed in Sec, 152-
201 through Sec. 152-225 refers to those areas within the precinct boundaries described in the “ORDER
OF THE MAYOR OF INDIANAPOLIS (AS MARION COUNTY EXECUTIVE) ESTABLISHING
PRECINCTS’, signed by Mayor Joseph Hogsett on January 3, 2022 and filed with the Indiana Election
Division on January 4, 2022, which Order and its attachments are Incorporated herein by reference.

ARTICLE Il. ADOPTION AND INTERPRETATION
Sec. 152-301. Exhibits.

The map or maps filed in connection with the submission of the districting plan adopted as Article II of
this Chapter shall be marked by the clerk as “Proposal No.157 2022: Exhibit A" and retained with the
permanent records of the ordinance adopting this article.

Sec. 152-302. Rules of construction.

(a) Any discrepancies or conflicts between the geographic boundaries of the precincts and the

boundaries of census blocks and any omissions or duplications shall be resolved by reference first to the

rules of construction stated in this section and if not resolved by such reference, then to the individual district
map or maps referenced as exhibits in section 152-301.

(b) If a conflict exists between a map or maps referenced as an exhibit in Section 152-301 showing
the boundaries of a district and_a description of the boundaries of-that district set forth in the descriptions
of the plan in Article II of this Chapter, the district boundaries are the descriptions of the boundaries set
forth in the plan in Article II of this Chapter, not the boundaries shown on the map or maps referenced as
an exhibit in Section 152-301, but only to the extent there is a conflict between the description and the map
or maps.

(c) Ifaconflict exists between the overall map of the districts and any map ofa single district referenced
as exhibits in Section 152-301, the district boundaries shal! be those shown in the individual district map to
the extent there is no conflict between such map and the district plan set forth in Article Il of this Chapter.

(d) The districts in Article Il of this Chapter shall be construed so as to include all of the territory of the
county within one (1), but only one (1), of the districts established by this article.

(e) Article I! of this Chapter shall be construed so as to exclude from ail districts any territory not located
in the county.

(f) tf any territory in the county is not included in one (1) of the districts, the territory is included in the
district that (3) is contiguous to that territory and (ii) contains the least population of all districts contiguous
to that territory.

(g) If any territory in the county is included in more than one (1) of the districts, the territory is included
in the district that (i) is one (1) of the districts in which the territory is described in Article |i of this Chapter,
(i) is contiguous to that territory and (iii) contains the least population of all the districts contiguous to that
territory.

(h) Any deviation of precinct boundaries from census block boundaries shall be resolved by using the
census block boundary.

Sec. 152-303. Reservations.

The council reserves the right to amend or revise the descriptions of the districts set forth in Articie il of
this Chapter to correct any errors, duplications or omissions in the descriptions, to conform to any
corrections or revisions of the census data hereafter made by the U.S. Bureau of Census, to comply with
any valid court order respecting this article, or for any other lawful purpose.

Sec. 152-304.
The districts established in Sec. 152-201 through 152-225 shall be in effect for elections to be held in 2023

and thereafter.

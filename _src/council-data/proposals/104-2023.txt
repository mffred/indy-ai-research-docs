CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 15, 2023
Proposal No. 104, 2023

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to authorize the city controller to make a public purpose
grant to the Arts Council of Indianapolis for the public art grants program pursuant to Revised Code Sec.
271-106.

WHEREAS, the City-County Annual Budget for 2023 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022),
Section 4.01(c), provides that any expenditures in 2023 for public purpose grants other than those enumerated
therein must receive Council approval by a resolution that identifies the amount and recipient of the grant; and

WHEREAS, Revised Code Section 271-106 provides that the Council may appropriate money from the
public art for neighborhoods fund for the purpose of awarding sub-grants for public art and public space
beautification through the public art grants program, and that such program shall be administered by the
Arts Council of Indianapolis;

WHEREAS, the Council desires to make a public purpose local grant for the public art grants program
in compliance with Fiscal Ordinance No. 18, 2022 and Revised Code Sec. 271-106; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby finds that a public purpose grant for the support of the public
arts grant program, as established by Rev. Code Sec. 271-106, is necessary and desirable in the
public interest and is in furtherance of a purpose for which the controller is otherwise authorized to
expend public moneys.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council hereby authorizes the city controller to make a public purpose local
grant to the Arts Council of Indianapolis, for purposes of the public arts progtam, in the amount of One
Hundred Seven Thousand Six Hundred Fifty dollars ($107,650). Such grant shall be in addition to, and
authorized on the same basis as, the public purpose locai grants enumerated in Fiscal Ordinance No. 18,
2022, Section 4.01(c).

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the City-County Council and
compliance with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14. .

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8" day of May, 2023, at 8:55 p.m.

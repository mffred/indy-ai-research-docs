CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 53, 2025
Proposal No. 105, 2025

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION renominating Charles Garcia for appointment to the Economic Development
Commission.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Economic Development Commission, the Council renominates:

Charles Garcia
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending May 5, 2029. The person
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the
appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the

holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5th day of May, 2025, at 7:18 p.m.

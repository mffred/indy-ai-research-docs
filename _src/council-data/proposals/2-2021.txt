CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 10, 2021
Proposal No. 2, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Taylor Schaffer as the Chief Deputy Mayor
for a term ending December 31, 2021, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 201-4 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and
County,” a mayoral appointment of Chief Deputy Mayor is subject to the approval of the City-County Council:
and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Taylor Schaffer
to serve as the Chief Deputy Mayor at his pleasure for a term ending December 31, 2021: now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Taylor Schaffer is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as Chief Deputy
Mayor for a term ending December 31, 2021, and until a successor is appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1*t day of February, 2021, at 7:57 p.m.

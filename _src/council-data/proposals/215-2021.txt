CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 80, 2021
Proposal No. 215, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Abel Contreras to the Indianapolis-Marion County Building Authority
Board of Trustees.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis-Marion County Building Authority Board of Trustees, the
Council appoints:

Abel Contreras
SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a termy ending June 30, 2023. The person
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the

appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the
holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 12 day of July, 2021, at 7:52 p.m.

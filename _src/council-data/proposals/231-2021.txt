CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
             
I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of Metropolitan 
Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on June 2, 2021, a public hearing as required by law pursuant to 
required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and Commercial 
Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan Development 
Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment prayed for in petition:  
 
2021-ZON-022 2153 NORTH CAPITOL AVENUE (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 231, 2021 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #11 
R.O. No. 62, 2021 HOOT ENTERPRISES INC., by David Kingen and Schyler Sullivan 
 Rezoning of 0.14 acre from the HD-2 (W-5) district to the D-8 (W-5) district. 
 
2021-ZON-029 6016 SOUTH BELMONT AVENUE (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 232, 2021 PERRY TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #20 
R.O. No. 63, 2021 EARL E. WHITE, by Schyler Sullivan and David Kingen 
 Rezoning of 0.94 acre from the D-A (FF) district to the C-7 (FF) district. 
  
2021-ZON-031 2366 AND 2372 WINTHROP AVENUE; 919 EAST 24TH STREET 
Prop No. 233, 2021 (APPROXIMATE ADDRESSES) 
R.O. No. 64, 2021 CENTER TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #17 
 ANGELA AND KENDRICK DAVIS, by Sharmin Frye 
 Rezoning of 0.22 acre from the I-2 district to the D-8 district.  
 
2021-ZON-035 1815 STOP 10 ROAD (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 234, 2021 PERRY TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #24 
R.O. No. 65, 2021 WILLIAM G. JONES, SHIRLEY M. JONES AND DONALD R. JONES, by David 
Kingen

2021-ZON-038 3325 LOWRY ROAD (APPROXIMATE ADDRESS) 
Prop No. 235, 2021 WAYNE TOWNSHIP, COUNCIL DISTRICT #10 
R.O. No. 66, 2021 LITTLE SCHOLARS CHILDCARE LLC, by Shuntel Robinson  
 Rezoning of one acre from the SU-37 (FF) district to the C-1 (FF) district.  
 
I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance prayed for in 
said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, 
by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended 2021-ZON-022, 2021-ZON-029, 
2021-ZON-031, 2021-ZON-035, and 2021-ZON-038 to the City-County Council for adoption to the end 
that adequate light, air, convenience of access and safety from fire, flood, and other danger may be 
secured, that property values may be preserved and that the public health, comfort, morals and 
convenience and general welfare may be preserved.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO. 13, 2025
Proposal No, 281, 2025

A SPECIAL ORDINANCE approving the appropriation of proceeds and investment earnings thereon of
“Indianapolis-Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term
Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project), Series 2025” in an original aggregate principal
amount not to exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000), for the purpose of financing all or any portion
of the 2025-2026 Muliti-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project.

WHEREAS, on July 28, 2025, the Library Board (the “Library Board”) of the Indianapolis-Marion County
Public Library, Indiana (the “Public Library”), adopted Resolution 25-2025 (the “2025-2026 Multi-Facility
Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Resolution’) authorizing the issuance of
one or more series of general obligation bonds of the Public Library to be designated as the “Indianapolis-
Marion County Public Library General Obligation Bonds (2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital
Maintenance and Equipment Update Project), Series 2025” (collectively, the “2025-2026 Multi-Facility
Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds’), in an original aggregate principal
amount not to exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000), for the purpose of procuring funds (a) to pay
for the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project (as defined
in the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Resolution)
and costs related thereto, and (b) to pay the costs of issuance of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term
Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds; and

WHEREAS, on July 28, 2025, the Library Board adopted Resolution 26-2025, appropriating a sum not
to exceed Nineteen Million Eight Hundred Thousand Dollars ($19,800,000), out of the proceeds of the 2025-
2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds, together with all
investment earnings thereon, for the purpose of paying all or a portion of the costs of the 2025-2026 Multi-
Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project; and

WHEREAS, the Library Board has requested the approval of the City-County Council, pursuant to
Indiana Code § 6-1.1-18-5(]), as amended, of the approval of the additional appropriation of the proceeds
of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds and
investment earnings thereon in an amount not to exceed $19,800,000 for the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-
Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project, and the City-County Council now finds that such
appropriation should be approved; and

WHEREAS, notice of a hearing on said appropriations has been published as required by law; and

WHEREAS, such public hearing on said appropriations has been held on the date hereof at this location
and prior to consideration of this Ordinance at which all taxpayers and interested persons had an
opportunity to appear and express their views as to such additional appropriations; now, therefore:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The City-County Council does hereby approve the appropriation of a sum not to exceed
Nineteen Million Eight Hundred Thousand Dollars ($19,800,000) out of the proceeds of the 2025-2026
Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project Bonds together with all
investment earnings (the “2025-2026 Muliti-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update
Project Appropriation”), all of which sum shall be for the use of the Public Library in paying all or a portion
of the costs of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project.

SECTION 2. The 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project
Appropriation shail be in addition to all appropriations provided for in the existing budget and levy of the
Public Library, and shall continue in effect until the completion of the 2025-2026 Multi-Facility Long-Term
Capital Maintenance and Equipment Update Project. Any surplus of such proceeds shall be credited to the
proper fund as required by law.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 9, 2024
Proposal No. 35, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Natalia Rodriguez-Hilt to the Metropolitan Board of Zoning Appeals,
Division |.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Metropolitan Board of Zoning Appeals, Division I, the Council appoints:
Natalia Rodriguez-Hilt

SECTION 2. The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2024. The

person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term,

the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed
the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5 day of February, 2024, at 8:01 p.m.

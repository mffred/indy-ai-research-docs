COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2022 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 63, 2022 
Proposal No. 134, 2022 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION re appointing Lori Kaplan to the Indianapolis Greenways Development 
Committee. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1. As a member of the Indianapolis Greenways Development Committee, the Council reappoints:  
 
Lori Kaplan 
 
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025.  The 
person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council.  Upon expiration of the term, 
the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed 
the holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8
th day of August, 2022, at 8:52 p.m.

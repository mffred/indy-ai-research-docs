COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2022 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 70, 2022 
Proposal No. 251, 2022 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION appointing Damon Richards to Fatal Crash Review Team. 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.  As a member of the Fatal Crash Review Team, the Council appoints:  
 
Damon Richards 
 
SECTION 2.  The appointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2023.  The person 
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term, the 
appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed the 
holdover duration set by statute. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8th day of August, 2022, at 8:56 p.m.

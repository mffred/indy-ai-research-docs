CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 3, 2023
Proposal No. 20, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Venture Christian Church’s Harvest Thanksgiving.

WHEREAS, each year at the end of October, the food collection begins. A few weeks later, a large box
truck filled with groceries on Tuesday before Thanksgiving shows up at Amber Woods Apartments. It’s
Harvest Thanksgiving; and

WHEREAS, after the truck and caravan of church members arrive, the group circles up to pray and
thank God for his many blessings, and for two decades, Venture Christian Church of Carmel, Indiana, has
been collecting bags of groceries and delivering them to dear friends in Indianapolis; and

WHEREAS, this ministry has blessed thousands of families. Each family receives five bags containing
items for breakfast, lunch, dinner, a Thanksgiving meal, a wellness bag, and a $10 gift card to Kroger. Over
the past 20 years, this ministry has graciously donated an estimated $300,000-$500,000; and

WHEREAS, in the past few years, the ministry has expanded to include Agape Christian Church of
Indianapolis and Clarksville Christian Church of Noblesville. With their help, the Harvest Thanksgiving
ministry can bless families in Noblesville as well; and

WHEREAS, the successful organization of this ministry goes to Jenny and Jason Belpulsi, who
volunteer their time as its dedicated leaders. They work in conjunction with Jon Vance, Outreach Minister
at Venture Christian Church; and

WHEREAS, in a three-way partnership with Venture Christian Church, Indianapolis Councilwoman La
Keisha Jackson, the not-for-profit, Pathway Resource Center, and Flaherty & Collins Properties as support
volunteers to coordinates the recruitment and registration of the residents at Amber Woods Apartment;
now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. City-County Councillor La Keisha Jackson recognizes the commitment made by Venture
Christian Church to be a connector for residents who otherwise will go without.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily recognizes Venture Christian Church for their shared vision and
commitment to nourishing and sustaining residents in Indianapolis communities.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 9 day of January, 2023, at 7:45 p.m.

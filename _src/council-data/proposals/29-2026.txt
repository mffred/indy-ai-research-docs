CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO.      , 2026 
 
A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Don Colvin for over 35 years of service to the City of Indianapolis 
with Indy Parks. 
 
WHEREAS, Don Colvin has served the City of Indianapolis for over 35 years with great distinction, 
during which time his name has become synonymous with Indy Parks.  Don has helped to transform the 
Indy Parks system and the city’s greenways into the amenities our residents enjoy today. As Deputy 
Director, Don has brought over $150 million in park projects into reality, transforming our city’s park 
system for generations to come; and 
 
WHEREAS, Don’s experience as a landscape architect brought him to the city first as an intern, then 
as a planner, administrator, and Deputy Director over the course of three decades. His impact on the Indy 
Parks system is unrivaled. Don saw countless ideas start out in park master plan meetings and 
shepherded them from aspirations into real amenities; and 
 
WHEREAS, Don’s stewardship and leadership positioned Indy Parks to execute historic projects over 
the last five years. He oversaw the planning and construction of five transformational projects as a result 
of the Circle City Forward Initiative, headlined by the long -awaited Frederick Douglass Park Family 
Center. Don’s knowledge of over 200 parks was crucial when the city allocated over $20 million in 
American Rescue Plan funds to transform some of the city’s smallest parks in some of the most 
distressed areas of the city. Despite the $80 million Lilly Endowment Grant to Indy Parks being over 14 
times the annual budget that Don’s planning and construction team generally oversees, he led that team 
to move over 40 projects to construction in less than three years; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City County Council of Indianapolis celebrates Don Colvin and expresses sincere 
gratitude for his public service to our city. Every visitor to our parks benefits from Don’s knowledge and 
decades of service. His incredible expertise ranges from horticulture to landscape architecture to public 
planning and community outreach, and much more. While there is no way for any one person to compete 
with his immense knowledge, he has passed on so much to Indy Parks staff which will serve the city well 
for decades to come; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

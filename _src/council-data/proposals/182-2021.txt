CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO. 15, 2021
Proposal No. 182, 2021

PROPOSAL FOR A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2021 (City-County
Fiscal Ordinance No. 20, 2020) by the addition of appropriations totaling Three Million Three Hundred Eleven
Thousand Three Hundred Seventy-One dollars ($3,311,371) to support a collective community approach to
public safety.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. To provide for expenditures, the necessity for which has arisen since the adoption, the
City-County Annual Budget for 2021 is hereby amended to reflect additional appropriations hereinafter stated
for purposes of the Information Services Agency, Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department and the Office
of Public Health and Safety.

SECTION 2. The Information Services Agency requests additional appropriations totaling Seven Hundred
Thirty Thousand dollars ($730,000) in the Information Services Agency Fund in character three for the
purpose of funding public safety technology infrastructure and hardware investments.

WHEREAS, Indianapolis has been home to many notable Irish immigrants, people of Irish ancestry,
and Irish organizations including: Thomas A. Taggart of County Monaghan, Ireland who was elected Mayor
of Indianapolis in 1895, 1897 and 1899; Sculptor John Hawkins Mahoney whose bronze statues of George
Rogers Clark, William Henry Harrison and James Whitcomb adorn the Soldiers’ and Sailors’ Monument;
and the Ancient Order of Hibernians whose motto of “Friendship, Unity and Christian Charity” has long
promoted Irish culture and philanthropy in the Indianapolis community; and

WHEREAS, the month of March has been recognized as Irish American Heritage Month since 1991,
providing an opportunity to celebrate the achievements and contributions of Irish Americans; and

WHEREAS, Indianapolis has a rich history of Irish American immigration, with many Irish Americans
settling in the city during the 19th and 20th centuries, and continuing to make their mark in various fields
such as agriculture, education, and politics; and

WHEREAS, the Irish American community in Indianapolis has preserved and shared its unique cultural
heritage through various activities and events, including music, dance, sport, language, and food, now,
therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proclaims March as Irish American Heritage Month in
Indianapolis and encourages all citizens to honor and recognize the significant contributions of Irish
Americans to our city, state and nation.

SECTION 2. The Council recognizes the importance of preserving and promoting the cultural heritage of
the trish American community in Indianapolis and encourages individuals and organizations to participate
in activities and events that celebrate and highlight the contributions of this important community.

SECTION 3. The Council expresses their appreciation and gratitude to the Irish American communi
Indianapolis for their many contributions to our city, and encourages all residents of indianapolis to join in
celebrating Irish American Heritage.

SECTION 4. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 5. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
44.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 4" day of March, 2024, at 7:31 p.m.

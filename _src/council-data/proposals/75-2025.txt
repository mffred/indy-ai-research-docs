CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 8, 2025
Proposal No. 75, 2025

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to authorize the city controller to take necessary steps to
permit non-elective contributions to be made to the City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana
Deferred Compensation Plan (“Plan”).

WHEREAS, the Plan currently does not permit non-elective contributions to be made on behalf of
Plan participants; and

WHEREAS, certain elected officials are entitled to contributions to be made by the City-County on
their behalf to the Plan; and

WHEREAS, the current process requires an election to be made through manual entry in order to
process these contributions, which increases the administrative burden and the risk of errors; and

WHEREAS, the city controller desires to automate the process and permit non-elective contributions
to the Plan; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby authorizes the city controller to take all necessary steps to
permit non-elective contributions to the City of Indianapolis and Marion County, Indiana Deferred
Compensation Plan.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the City-County Council and
compliance with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7" day of April, 2025, at 7:53 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 9, 2024
Proposal No. 79, 2024

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the 2024 Barbasol Horizon League Basketball Championships.

WHEREAS, the 45-year-old Horizon League is an NCAA Division | athletics conference comprised of
11 member institutions located throughout the Midwest's major metropolitan cities. Headquartered in
Indianapolis, the League and its members strive to be highly competitive athletically while committing to the
total development of its student-athletes and creating a lasting impact on campus and in the community;
and

WHEREAS, the Horizon League will host, for the fifth-straight year, the 2024 Barbasol Horizon League
Women’s and Men’s Championships at the historic Indiana Farmers Coliseum highlighting student-athletes,
Indianapolis and Midtown in front of a national television audience on ESPN linear and digital platforms.
The semifinals will occur March 11 with the finals on March 12". and

WHEREAS, the Horizon League, in conjunction with the championships, will host amplifying community
events to further impact our city, including the KeyBank international Women’s Day Celebration, Officially
Human “What does it take to become a high level official?” event to support and develop referees; and the
Valeo Fan Zone, providing fans from all over an enhanced experience outside of the action on the
hardwood; and

WHEREAS, the Horizon League — under the leadership of Commissioner Julie Roe Lach — its Local
Organizing Committee, neighborhood associations, civic leaders and neighbors are working to create a
long-term home. for this event in Indianapolis and invites our community to the Indiana Farmers Coliseum
March 11 and 12 for affordable family fun as #MarchStartsHere...in Midtown; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes and celebrates the Horizon League
on their fifth straight year hosting the Barbasol Horizon League Basketball Championships.

SECTION 2. The Council further recognizes the Horizon League’s commitment to college athletics,
Indianapolis, and the Midtown neighborhood; and wishes them continued success with this event and other
endeavors for many years to come.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 4" day of March, 2024, at 7:35 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO, 35, 2023
Proposal No, 12, 2023

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Joe Glass as the Director of the Office of
Audit and Performance for a term ending December 31, 2023.

WHEREAS, pursuant Section 202-302 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City and County,” a
mayoral appointment naming the Director of the Office of Audit and Performance is subject to the approval of
the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Joe Glass to
serve as the Director of the Office of Audit and Performance at his pleasure for a term ending December 31,
2023; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Joe Glass is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as the Director of the
Office of Audit and Performance for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is appointed
and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 6 day of February, 2023, at 8:00 p.m.

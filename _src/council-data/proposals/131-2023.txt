CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 16, 2023
Proposal No. 131, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the Chapelwood Elementary School Robotics team.

WHEREAS, the Chapelwood Elementary School Robotics team won the 2023 Indiana Robotics
Championship. The students on that team are Jacob Modesto and Miguel Quinones-Mora. By placing first
at three highly competitive city competitions, Jacob and Miguel earned a bid to the Indiana State Robotics
Championship at Lucas Oil Stadium; and

WHEREAS, at the state competition on March 25. the boys performed well throughout the eight
qualification rounds. They went into finals ranked 20" out of the top 130 Indiana elementary teams. Making
the finals was not enough for these young men. During the final sixty-second round, they achieved their
highest scoring round ever. They outscored ail other alliances and were declared the 2023 Indiana Vex
Robotics State Champions; and

WHEREAS, these robotics competitions require quick thinking, alliance work, strong work ethic, and
nerves of steel. Jacob and Miguel demonstrate these skills both in and out of the classroom. By winning
the state event, Jacob Modesto and Miguel Quinones-Mora will continue their robotics journey at the 2023
VEX World Championship. Jacob Modesto also represented the Chapelwood 2022 Robotics State
Championship Team; therefore becoming a back-to-back state champion; and

WHEREAS, STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) continues to be a strong focus for
Wayne Township, as they have more STEM-certified schools than any other district in the state. That focus
is paying huge dividends, as is evidenced by their strong showing in the state’s robotics competition; and

WHEREAS, this year’s state competition was held on March 25, 2023, at Lucas Oil Stadium. This
competition was the largest State Championship in the country, gathering over 350 robotics teams from
elementary, middie and high schools from across the state to compete. The upcoming elementary worid
competition will be held in Dallas, Texas from May 2-4, 2023. now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the Chapelwood Elementary
Robotics team for their achievements in STEM-based robotics.

SECTION 2. The Council heartily congratulates the Chapelwood Elementary team and wishes them much
success in future endeavors.

SECTION 3. The Mayor i ited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 8 day of May, 2023, at 7:26 p.m.

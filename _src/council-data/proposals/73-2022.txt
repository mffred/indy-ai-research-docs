CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 24, 2022
Proposal No. 73, 2022

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Rev. T.D. Robinson to the Indianapolis-Marion County Public
Library Board of Trustees.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the indianapolis-Marion County Public Library Board of Trustees, the Council
reappoints:

Rev. T.D. Robinson

SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2025. The
person appointed by this resolution shail serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the
term, the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to
exceed the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7 day of March, 2022, at 7:46 p.m.

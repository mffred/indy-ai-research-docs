CITY-COUNTY GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 67, 2022
Proposal No. 410, 2022

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL ORDINANCE amending the Revised Code of the Consolidated City and
County and establishing speed along Mann Road from Southport Road to the Johnson County Line.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County," specifically, Sec. 441-323,
Alteration of prima facie speed limits, be and the same is hereby amended by the deletion of the following,
to wit

Mann Road from Southport Road to the Johnson County Line; 40 mph.
SECTION 2. The "Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County," specifically, Sec. 441-323,
Alteration of prima facie speed limits, be and the same is hereby amended by the addition of the following,
to wit:

Mann Road from Southport Road to the Johnson County Line; 30 mph.

SECTION 2. This ordinance shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC
36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5'" day of December, 2022, at 8:13 p.m.

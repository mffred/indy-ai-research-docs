CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 8, 2024
Proposal No. 34, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Roderick Bohannan to the Metropolitan Board of Zoning Appeals,
Division Il.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Metropolitan Board of Zoning Appeals, Division Ill, the Council reappoints:
Roderick Bohannan

SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2024. The

person appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council. Upon expiration of the term,

the appointee may serve until his or her successor is appointed and qualifies, for a period not to exceed

the holdover duration set by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 5 day of February, 2024, at 8:00 p.m.

CITY-COUNTY FISCAL ORDINANCE NO.     , 2025 
 
A FISCAL ORDINANCE amending the City-County Annual Budget for 2025 (City-County Fiscal Ordinance 
No. 6, 2024) by an additional appropriation of six million eight hundred thirty thousand six hundred fifty 
dollars ($6,830,650), a  transfer of appropriation of one million two hundred thirty-six thousand dollars 
($1,236,000), and a reduction of appropriation of six hundred thirty thousand six hundred fifty dollars 
($630,650) for the purposes of the Marion County Sheriff. 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:  
 
SECTION 1.  To provide for expenditures the necessity for which has arisen since its adoption, the 
City-County Annual Budget for 20 25 is hereby amended by the  character increases, decreases, and 
transfers hereinafter stated for purposes of the Marion County Sheriff. 
 
SECTION 2.  The Marion County Sheriff  requests a n additional appropriation totaling six million eight 
hundred thirty thousand six hundred fifty dollars ($6,830,650)  in character one, a transfer of appropriation 
totaling nine hundred thirty-six thousand dollars ($936,000) from character three to character one, a transfer 
of appropriation totaling three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000) from character two to character four  
in the County General Fund , and a reduction of appropriation totaling six hundred thirty thousand six 
hundred fifty dollars ($630,650) in the Medical Care for Inmates Fund in character three for the purposes of 
funding the Indiana Fraternal Order of Police Labor Council, Inc.  collective bargaining agreement and 
equipment replacement plan:

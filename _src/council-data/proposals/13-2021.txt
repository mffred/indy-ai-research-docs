CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 41, 2021
Proposal No. 13, 2021

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor's appointment of Randal Taylor as the Chief of Police for
the indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department for a term ending December 31, 2021, and until a successor
is appointed and confirmed.

WHEREAS, pursuant to IC 36-3-5-2 and Section 279-221 of the “Revised code of the Consolidated City
and County,” a mayoral appointment of the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department
is subject fo the approval of the City-County Council; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted fo this Council the name of Randal Taylor
to serve as the Chief of Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department at his pleasure for a term
ending December 31, 2021; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Randal Taylor is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as the Chief of
Police for the Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department for a term ending December 31, 2021, and until
a successor is appointed and confirmed.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 22"4 day of February, 2021, at 7:58 p.m.

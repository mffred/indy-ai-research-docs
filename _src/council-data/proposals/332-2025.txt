CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL ORDINANCE NO.     , 2025 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A SPECIAL ORDINANCE  accepting the county recorder’s sworn statement and 
approving the county recorder’s request to use money in the perpetuation fund to pay for other office 
expenses during the 2026 fiscal year.   
 
WHEREAS, Indiana Code (IC) Section 36 -2-7-10(f) directs the county treasurer to establish a county 
recorder’s record Perpetuation Fund (Fund); and 
 
WHEREAS, that same section of the Indiana Code further states that, other than as allowed in IC 36-
2-7-10.2, the county recorder may only use the money in the Fund for the preservation of records and for 
the improvement of record keeping systems and equipment; and 
 
WHEREAS, IC 36-2-7-10.2 authorizes the use of the  money in the Fund to pay all or a portion of the  
office expenses for the following calendar year  if, and only if, the county recorder provides a sworn 
statement and the council adopts that statement by ordinance; now, therefore:   
  
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:  
  
SECTION 1.  In compliance with IC 36-2-7-10.2 the Marion County Recorder hereby submits in, Exhibit A, 
a sworn statement indicating that  
 
(A) the current revenue to the Perpetuation Fund is sufficient to fulfill the statutory purpose of the 
Fund,  
(B) the technology of the recorder’s office is updated and at a level to sufficiently meet the 
statutory purposes of the Fund and the county recorder’s office,  
(C) the Fund has a sufficient reserve, consistent with the recorder’s plan, to capitalize the next 
technology or other records management upgrade necessary to fulfill the statutory  purpose of 
the Fund and the county recorder’s office, and  
(D) the county recorder specifically requests that a specific portion of the Fund be used to pay the 
expenses of the office for the following calendar year.

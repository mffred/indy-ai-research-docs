CERTIFICATION AND REPORT OF ZONING PETITIONS     ) 
 
I, Edward D. Honea, Jr., Interim Current Planning Administrator, Department of Metropolitan 
Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan Development Commission of 
Marion County, Indiana, held on September 18, 2024, a public hearing as required by law pursuant 
to required statutory legal notice hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and 
Commercial Record" newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said 
Metropolitan Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment 
prayed for in petition:  
 
2024-ZON-027  1101 and 1117 South Sherman Drive (Approximate Addresses) 
PROP NO. 326, 2024 Center Township, Council District #19 
R.O. No. 107, 2024 Liberty Commercial Investors, LLC., by Donald W. Fisher 
Rezoning of 0.68-acre from the D-5 and C-3 districts to the C-5 district to provide for 
automobile sales. 
 
2024-ZON-081  4310 North Carroll Road (Approximate Address) 
PROP NO. 327, 2024 Lawrence Township, Council District #15 
R.O. No. 108, 2024 Lennar Homes of Indiana, LLC, by Brian J. Tuohy 
Rezoning of 30.85 acres from the D-P (FF) district to the D-P (FF) district to 
provide for a single-family residential development, consisting of 51 dwelling units 
and amenities, including open space and a nature trail. 
 
2024-ZON-084  3124 West 16th Street (Approximate Address) 
PROP NO. 328, 2024 Wayne Township, Council District #11 
R.O. No. 109, 2024 Marcos Perera 
Rezoning of 0.83-acre from the D-5 and C-1 districts to the C-3 district to provide for 
commercial uses. 
 
2024-ZON-088  8101 and 8105 West Washington Street (Approximate Addresses) 
PROP NO. 329, 2024 Wayne Township, Council District #17 
R.O. No. 110, 2024 Indy Aerospace, Inc., by Brian J. Touhy 
Rezoning of 4.77 acres from the SU-46 district to the 1-3 district to build a 30,000-
square-foot building. 
 
2024-ZON-089  3527 North College Avenue (Approximate Address) 
PROP NO. 330, 2024 Center Township, Council District # 8 
R.O. No. 111, 2024 Walvan Investments, LLC, by David Gilman 
Rezoning of 0.22 acre from the D-5 district to the D-8 district to provide for a (6-unit) 
small apartment use. 
 
2024-ZON-091  6530 and 6650 Bluff Road (Approximate Addresses) 
PROP NO. 331, 2024 Perry Township, Council District #22 
R.O. No. 112, 2024 Linda Group Indy, LLC, by David Kingen 
Rezoning of 10.57 acres from the SU-16 district to the SU-1 district to provide for 
religious uses.

I FURTHER CERTIFY that the attached ordinance is a true copy of the ordinance prayed for 
in said above listed petitions, and the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, 
Indiana, by a majority of its members adopted, approved, and recommended 2024-ZON-027, 2024-
ZON-081, 2024-ZON-084, 2024-ZON-088, and 2024-ZON-089, 2024-ZON-091, and 2024-CZN-820

that the public health, comfort, morals, and convenience and general welfare may be preserved.

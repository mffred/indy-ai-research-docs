CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 11, 2025
Proposal No. 176, 2025

A GENERAL RESOLUTION of the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis and of Marion County,
Indiana (the "City-County Council’), approving (i) a Declaratory Resolution and Economic Development
Plan of the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, acting as the
Redevelopment Commission of the City of Indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) and serving as the governing
body of the City of Indianapolis Redevelopment District (the “Commission”) under indiana Code 36-7-15.1,
et seq. (the “Act’), (i) the removal of certain parcels from the Fall Creek/Citizens Consolidated
Redevelopment Area (the “Fall Creek Redevelopment Area”) and the Fall Creek/Citizens Consolidated
HOTIF Area (the “Fall Creek Allocation Area’), (ili) the creation of the Oxford Row Economic Development
Area (the “Oxford Row Economic Development Area”) and the Oxford Row Allocation Area (the “Oxford
Row Allocation Area”) and (iil) amendments to the Fall Creek Redevelopment Area, the Fall Creek
Allocation Area and the Fall Creek/Citizens Consolidated Redevelopment Area Plan (the “Fall Creek
Plan’).

WHEREAS, Commission has previously adopted and confirmed resolutions (as amended from time
to time, collectively, the “Fall Creek Declaratory Resolution”) which established Fall Creek Redevelopment
Area, approved the Fall Creek Plan, and designated the Fall Creek Allocation Area as an allocation area;
and

WHEREAS, on May 21, 2025, the Commission adopted a declaratory resolution No. 2025-E-012 (the
“Declaratory Resolution”) which (a) initially approved an Economic Development Pian (the “Oxford Row
Plan”) for the Oxford Row Economic Development Area; (b) removed certain parcels from the Fall Creek
Allocation Area and created a new economic development area to be designated Oxford Row Economic
Development Area and also established such new Economic Development Area as a new allocation area
as the Oxford Row Allocation Area, and (c) amended the Fall Creek Redevelopment Area, the Fall Creek
Allocation Area and the Fall Creek Plan, as needed, pursuant to the Act; and

WHEREAS, the Act requires approval of the Declaratory Resolution, the Oxford Row Plan, the Oxford
Row Economic Development Area, the Oxford Row Allocation Area and the amendments to the Fall Creek
Redevelopment Area, the Fall Creek Allocation Area and the Fall Creek Plan, by the City-County Council
of the City of Indianapolis and of Marion County, Indiana (the “Council’); and

WHEREAS, the Declaratory Resolution, the Oxford Row Plan, the Oxford Row Economic
Development Area, the Oxford Row Allocation Area and the amendments to the Fall Creek Redevelopment
Area, the Fall Creek Allocation Area and the Fall Creek Plan have been submitted to this Council; now,
therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Declaratory Resolution, the Oxford Row Plan, the Oxford Row Economic Development
Area, the Oxford Row Allocation Area and the amendments to the Fall Creek Redevelopment Area, the
Fall Creek Allocation Area and the Fall Creek Plan, as needed, are in all respects approved, ratified and
confirmed by the Council.

SECTION 2. The removal of certain parcels from the Fall Creek Allocation Area and the determination of
the Commission that the Oxford Row Allocation Area described in the Declaratory Resolution is an
allocation area pursuant to the Act is in all respects approved, ratified and confirmed by the Council.

SECTION 3. This resolution shail be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code §§ 36-3-4-14, 36-3-4-15 and 36-3-4-16.

GENERAL RESOLUTION RECORD 2025

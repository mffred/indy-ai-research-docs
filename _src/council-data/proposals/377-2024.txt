CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO.  , 2024 
 
PROPOSAL FOR A COUNCIL RESOLUTION establishing a study commission to evaluate intellectual 
and developmental disability service providers and funding allocations in Marion County. 
WHEREAS, the Consolidated City of Indianapolis believes in empowering individuals with disabilities 
and their families; and 
WHEREAS, Indianapolis and Marion County have provided public support to service providers for 
decades through various funding streams, including through the authority of Indiana Code 12 -29 et seq.; 
and 
WHEREAS, it is the Council’s opinion that future funding allocations for public support should be 
made based on shared, measurable outcomes; now, therefore:  
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE  
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY , INDIANA: 
 
Section 1. Purpose:  A study commission is herby established under the authority of the council to review 
and analyze the historical use of funds collected under Indiana Code 12 -29 et seq., to consult with 
experts to consider the current method of fund distribution, any future fund distribution methodology, and 
to develop recommendations on how to create a fund distribution model that evaluates nonprofit 
intellectual and developmental disability (I/DD) service providers and funding allocations in Marion 
County.  The commission shall hold public meetings and report its findings and recommendations to the 
full council.  Public comment shall be allowed under any rules devised by the Commission.  
 
Section 2. Name: The commission shall be named the “Commission on Nonprofit Intellectual and 
Development Disability Provider Support” (Commission).” 
 
Section 3. Scope: The Commission shall consider the following in carrying out its purpose:  
(1) best practices and recommendations from industry experts (e.g. the Agency for Healthcare 
Research and Quality (AHRQ) and the National Quality Forum);

(2) identification and measurement of need, services currently provided and impact, gaps in services, 
populations currently served/unserved/underserved;  
(3) maintaining current practices  that do not disrupt access to low -barrier or additional services that 
may not be covered through Medicaid;  
(4) methods to equitably meet the needs of individuals with an I/DD in Marion County;  
(5) historical revenues collected by the Consolidated City – Marion County through Indiana Code 12 -
29-, the funds distributed, and an assessment of how these funds have been used, consideration 
of increasing the current property tax collection;  
(6) alternative funding allocations and a timeline for any changes that prioritize continuity of services 
and overall impact, including through the creation of a new, separate fund, authorized and 
permitted under Indiana Code, including a grantmaking process and administrative structure, for 
service gaps in this space; and  
(7) the efficacy of allocations related to an organization’s dependence on City funds for its operations 
and programming.  
 
Section 4. Members: The Commission shall be composed of seven (7) members all appointed by the 
Council President.  The President shall take into consideration recommendations from the Commission 
Chair and others when appointing members.  The Commission members shall consist of:  
• four (4) Councilors,  
o (1) of whom shall be designated as the chair of the Commission,  
o at least two (2) must represent different nonprofit I/DD service providers,  
o and at least one (1) with personal or family lived experience with I/DD;  
o one (1) Councilor from the Minority Caucus;  
• one (1) advocate with personal or family lived experience with I/DD;  
• one (1) expert in the I/DD field; and  
• one (1) industry professional that represents an organization with experience employing 
individuals with I/DD or working with nonprofit I/DD service providers. 
 
Section 5. Quorum and Vacancies: The Commission shall meet upon the call of its chair or a majority of 
its members.  Four (4) members of the Commission shall constitute a quorum.  Any findings or reports of 
the Commission shall only be approved by the vote of four (4) members.  Any vacancy on the 
Commission shall be filled in the same manner that the original appointment was made.  
 
Section 6. Staffing: The Commission will receive staff support from the Council staff, as needed. All 
meetings shall be subject to the Indiana Access to Public Records Act (IC 5 -14-3) and the Indiana Open 
Door Law (IC 5-14-1.5).  
 
Section 7. Information Sharing:  City and County departments and agencies shall share information and 
cooperate with the Commission including, without limitation, permitting personnel to meet with 
Commission members and testify before the Commission. 
 
Section 8. Timeline: The Commission shall hold its first meeting by January 31, 2025, and establish a 
regular schedule for future meetings. The Commission shall meet and gather all needed research and 
findings with an initial draft of the below-described report by April 30, 2025.  
 
Section 9. Report: The Commission shall present its final report to the Council by the June 2025 meeting 
of the full council. The final report shall be posted on the Council’s website. 
 
Section 10. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36 -3-4-
14.

CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 33, 2022
Proposal No. 380, 2022

A GENERAL RESOLUTION approving a declaratory resolution and redevelopment plan for the
Consolidated Redevelopment Area in the City of Indianapolis, Indiana.

WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana (the “Commission’),
serves as the Redevelopment Commission of the City of indianapolis, Indiana (the “City”) under Indiana
Code 36-7-15.1, ef seq. (the “Act”), and, in that capacity, the Commission serves as the governing body of
the City of Indianapolis Redevelopment District; and

WHEREAS, the Commission has previously adopted and confirmed resolutions (as amended from time
to time, collectively, the “Declaratory Resolution”) which (i) declared an area of the City, known as the
“Consolidated/Harding Street Redevelopment Area” (the “Consolidated Redevelopment Area”), to be a
“redevelopment project area” within the meaning of the Act, (ii) designated a portion of the Consolidated
Redevelopment Area, known as the “Consolidated Allocation Area,” as an “allocation area’ for the purposes
of Section 26 of the Act (the “Consolidated Allocation Area”), and approved the “Consolidated/Harding
Street Redevelopment Area Plan” for the Consolidated Redevelopment Area (the “Consolidated Area
Plan’); and

WHEREAS, the Commission, pursuant to the Act, adopted Resolution No. 2022-E-041 amending the
Declaratory Resolution and the Consolidated Area Plan to: (i} remove certain parcels of property from the
Consolidated Allocation Area; (ii) designate the “Cole Motor Allocation Area” as a separate allocation area
within the Consolidated Redevelopment Area for purposes of Section 26 of the Act; and adopt a
supplement to the Consolidated Redevelopment Plan (the "Plan Supplement”) (collectively, the “2022
Amendments”); now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. Pursuant to Section 9 of the Act, the City-County Council of the City of Indianapolis and
Marion County, Indiana (the “City-County Council”) hereby approves the amendment to the Declaratory
Resolution and the 2022 Amendments.

SECTION 2. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon its adoption and compliance with Indiana
Code 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was. passed by the City-County Council this 5" day of December, 2022, at 8:02 p.m.

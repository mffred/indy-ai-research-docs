CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 34, 2024
Proposal No. 17, 2024

A COUNCIL RESOLUTION reappointing Terry A. Webster, Jr. to the Board of Public Health and Safety.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. As a member of the Board of Public Health and Safety, the Council reappoints:

Terry A. Webster, Jr.
SECTION 2. The reappointment made by this resolution is for a term ending December 31, 2024. The person
appointed by this resolution shall serve at the pleasure of the Council and until his or her successor is

appointed and qualifies, unless the duration of the holdover period for this office is limited by statute.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 1st day of April, 2024, at 7:56 p.m.

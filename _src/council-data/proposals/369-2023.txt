 I, MATT PLEASANT, Administrator, Division of Current Planning, Department of 
Metropolitan Development, certify that at a regular meeting of the Metropolitan 
Development Commission of Marion County, Indiana, held on October 18, 2023, 
following a public hearing as required by law pursuant to required statutory legal notice 
hereof published in the "Indianapolis Star” and “Court and Commercial Record" 
newspapers of general circulation in Marion County, Indiana, said Metropolitan 
Development Commission acted upon the proposed zoning ordinance amendment 
prayed for in petition: 
 
2023-ZON-083 2991 East Troy Avenue (Approximate Address) 
Prop No. 369, 2023 Perry Township, Council District #21

provide for a food pantry ministry.

ZON-083 to the City-County Council.

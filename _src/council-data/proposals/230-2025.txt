CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 16, 2025
Proposal No. 230, 2025

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing "Knozone Action Days” and commending the ongoing cooperation
between the State of Indiana and the City of Indianapolis to protect public health and improve air quality.

WHEREAS, the City of Indianapolis partners with the State of Indiana to support Knozone, a 365-day-
ion i ive aimed at improving air quality, protecting public health, and reducing
harmful emissions across Central Indiana; and

WHEREAS, on days when ground-level ozone and/or fine particulate matter (PM2.5) reach unhealthy
levels for sensitive groups or higher, a Knozone Action Day is declared, prompting the public to take
precautionary steps to limit exposure; and

WHEREAS, Knozone Action Days are especially important for sensitive individuals, such as active
children, older adults, and people with asthma, lung disease, or other serious health conditions, who are
urged to reduce outdoor activity during these times; and

WHEREAS, on days when the air is deemed unhealthy for all groups, very unhealthy, or hazardous, ali
residents are advised to avoid spending time outdoors and follow recommended health precautions; and

WHEREAS, the collaborative efforts between the State and City on Knozone Action Days exemplify a
proactive, science-based approach to public health, environmental stewardship, and intergovernmental
cooperation; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Council formally recognizes Knozone Action Days as a critical public health tool that
raises awareness and helps protect residents from harmful air pollutants.

SECTION 2. The Council encourages continued public engagement, education, and participation in air
quality improvement strategies to ensure a healthier future for all who live, work, and visit Indianapolis.

SECTION 3. The Mayor

invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 7 day of July, 2025, at 7:34 p.m.

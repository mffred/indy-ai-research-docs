CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 4, 2022
Proposal No. 26, 2022

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing the extraordinary life of Al Unser, Sr. and his contributions to auto
racing, a sport vital to local tourism, the economy, and the identity of the City of Indianapolis.

WHEREAS, the Indianapolis Motor Speedway is Indianapolis’ most recognizable landmark; and

WHEREAS, in 2019, the Indianapolis 500 race at the motor speedway drew 5.5 million television

viewers and over 300,000 in-person attendees; and

WHEREAS, in 2021 the Indianapolis Motor Speedway led the way for sports to return to normal amidst
a global pandemic; and

WHEREAS, the world had the pleasure of witnessing Al Unser, Sr. become one of four drivers to win
the Indianapolis 500 four times; and

WHEREAS, along with many of his incredible achievements, Al Unser, Sr. was the oldest driver to ever
take the checkered flag at the Indianapolis 500, showing that age is only a state of mind; and

WHEREAS, Unser finished his career with 39 IndyCar wins, sixth on the all-time list, and he won
IndyCar season championships in 1970, 1983, and 1985; and

WHEREAS, according to Mario Andretti, “Al Unser, Sr. was one of the smartest drivers I've ever raced
against,” and he often said, “I wished | could have had some of his patience;” and

WHEREAS, Al Unser, Sr. is one of the most iconic names in Indianapolis 500 race history, which has
contributed immensely fo the legacy of the Indianapolis Motor Speedway; and

WHEREAS, Unser was inducted into the Indianapolis Motor Speedway Hall of Fame in 1986 and the
International Motorsports Hall of Fame in 1998; and

WHEREAS, Al Unser, Sr.’s remarkable life and dedication to Indianapolis, the Indianapolis 500, and
auto racing will always be remembered by the City of indianapolis and all race fans; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianap City-County Council proudly recognizes the extraordinary life and
contributions to the City made by Al Unser, Sr. who passed away on December 9, 2021 after battling cancer
for seventeen years.

SECTION 2. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 10" day of January, 2022, at 8:01 p.m.

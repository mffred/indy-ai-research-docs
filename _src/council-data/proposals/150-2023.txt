COUNCIL RESOLUTION RECORD 2023 
CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL RESOLUTION NO. 61, 2023 
Proposal No. 150, 2023 
 
A COUNCIL RESOLUTION approving the Mayor’s appointment of Rusty Carr as the Director of the 
Department of Metropolitan Development for a term ending December 31, 2023. 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Ind. Code § 36- 3-5-2 and Section 231- 211 of the “Revised code of the 
Consolidated City and County,” a mayoral appointment naming the Director of the Department of Metropolitan 
Development is subject to the approval of the City-County Council; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Mayor of the City of Indianapolis has submitted to this Council the name of Rusty Carr to 
serve as the Director of the Department of Metropolitan Development at his pleasure for a term ending 
December 31, 2023; now, therefore: 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE 
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA: 
 
SECTION 1.   Rusty Carr is approved and confirmed by the City-County Council to serve as the Director of the 
Department of Metropolitan Development for a term ending December 31, 2023, and until a successor is 
appointed and confirmed. 
 
SECTION 2.  This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC § 36-3-4-
14. 
 
The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 14
th day of August, 2023, at 8:03 p.m.

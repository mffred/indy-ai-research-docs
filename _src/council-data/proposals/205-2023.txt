CITY-COUNTY SPECIAL RESOLUTION NO. 28, 2023
Proposal No. 205, 2023

A SPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing Moms Demand Action and Students Demand Action as two
organizations that have demonstrated an unwavering commitment to advocating for common-sense gun
legislation and ending the epidemic of gun violence in our communities.

WHEREAS, following the Sandy Hook tragedy in 2012, where a gunman entered an elementary school
and took the lives of twenty children and six educators, Shannon Watts founded Moms Demand Action as
a grassroots movement to empower and mobilize individuals to fight for stronger gun laws and safer
communities; and

WHEREAS, with chapters in every state and more than 10 million supporters, Moms Demand Action,
through its advocacy efforts, has been instrumental in securing important victories at the state and federal
levels, including the passage of red flag laws and expanding background checks; and

WHEREAS, Students Demand Action is a student-led initiative that is part of Moms Demand Action
and works te empower young people to take action on gun violence prevention; and

WHEREAS, both Moms Demand Action and Students Demand Action have successfully organized
rallies and events across the country and have been a powerful voice in the fight for gun reform, and

WHEREAS, estimates show that 4.6 million American children live in a home where at least one gun
is kept loaded and unlocked; and

WHEREAS, today’s youth have grown up with lockdown drills and safety seminars, resulting in
lifelong psychological trauma;

WHEREAS, guns are the leading cause of death among children, teens, and college-aged youth in the
United States; and

WHEREAS, in 2022 alone, 43,000 students were exposed to an active shooter event, in which a total
of 34 people, including children and adults, died from gunshot wounds, and

WHEREAS, the tireless efforts of Moms Demand Action and Students Demand Action have helped
shine a light on the devastating toll that gun violence takes on our communities, and, by speaking out and
demanding action, these organizations have inspired others to get involved in protecting their communities;
and

WHEREAS, given the rising trend in gun-related deaths in Indiana and the current state laws that allow
such deadly weapons to proliferate unimpeded, the Indiana chapters of Moms Demand Action and Students
Demand Action play a critical role in advocating for legislative reform to ensure that young Hoosiers are
secure in their homes and their schools;

Now, therefore,

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The Indianapolis City-County Council recognizes and commends Moms Demand Action and
Students Demand Action for their outstanding work and advocacy efforts to end gun violence in our
communities.

SECTION 2. The Council extends its deepest gratitude to Moms Demand Action and Students Demand
Action for their tireless efforts to effect change and their continued pursuit of a future free from gun violence.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with iC 36-3-4-
14.

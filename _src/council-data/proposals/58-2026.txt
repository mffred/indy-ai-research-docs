ASPECIAL RESOLUTION recognizing 40 years of dedicated service by Indianapolis Fire Department (IFD)
Chief Ernest Malone.

WHEREAS, Ernest V. Malone was born and raised in Indianapolis as the middle c
to Ernest and Mary Malone, living his entire life within the IFD service district, attending Indianapolis Public
Schools, graduating from Arlington High School in 1981, and achieving an Associate’s Degree in Fire
Science, a Bachelor’s Degree in Business Management, a Master's Degree in Management, as well as
being a graduate of the National Fire Academy; and,

WHEREAS, Emest and his wife Tammy have three grown sons and st

WHEREAS, while working as a loss prevention manager at Target at 23 years of age, “Ernie” was
recruited during a walk through the Indiana Black Expo by IFD Ladder 19B Lieutenant Eddie Rogers, whose
excitement and passion for firefighting inspired Ernest to apply for a position with IFD; and

WHEREAS, Firefighter Malone was hired by the Indianapolis Fire Department in 1986. Over his 40-
year career, Chief Malone has held every promotional rank possible on the department, including
Lieutenant, Captain, Battalion Chief, Fire Marshall and Assistant Chief. He was promoted to serve as the
42"4 Chief of the indianapolis Fire Department by Mayor Greg Ballard in July, 2014, and re-appointed by
Mayor Joe Hogsett in 2016, serving continuously and diligently in that top position for over 11 years; and

WHEREAS, Chief Malone has completed numerous certifications, including EMT, Basic Emergency
Rescue Technician (BERT), Haz Mat Technician, Top Water Rescue, Fire Inspector and Investigator,
Incident Commander, and Master Scuba Diver, which he considers to have been one of the most
challenging. Among his impressive array of credentials and qualifications, Chief Malone has also received
an Executive Fire Officer Certification from the National Fire Academy and is the first Indianapolis Firefighter
ever to receive the designation of Chief Fire Officer from the Center for Public Safety Excellence; as well
as completing the Indiana University’s Randall L. Tobias Center for Leadership Development program, after
which he was subsequently awarded the designation as a Hoosier Fellow; and

WHEREAS, highly decorated during his years of service, some notable commendations include:
Rookie of the Year, American Red Cross Heroes Hall of Fame; Administrative Officer of the Year; Firefighter
of the Year, Service Firefighter of the Year, Professional Excellence Award, Medal of Bravery, Community
Service Officer of the Year, Medal of Merit and many more; and

WHEREAS, known among firefighters to be tactically knowledgeable and tenacious about getting the
job done right and safely, Chief Ernest Malone has proven himself to be a leader who values the traditions
of the past, while effectively addressing the issues of the present and embracing the challenges of the
future; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianapolis City-County Council proudly recognizes the 40 years of dedicated service
by Indianapolis Fire Department (IFD) Chief Ernest Malone.

SECTION 2. The Council looks forward to continuing to work alongside Chief Malone to create an even
safer community, and wishes him continued success in all future endeavors.

SECTION 3. The Mayor is invited to join in this resolution by affixing his signature hereto.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall be in full force and effect upon adoption and compliance with IC 36-3-4-
14,

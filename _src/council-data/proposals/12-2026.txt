CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. 2, 2026
Proposal No. 12, 2026

A PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION determining the need for the Indianapolis Metropolitan
Police Department, (IMPD) to lease approximately 27,010 square feet of office space at 4140 North
Keystone Ave Indianapolis, IN 46205

WHEREAS, IMPD currently leases occupies 27,010 square feet of office space at 4140 North Keystone
Avenue, Indianapolis, IN 46205; and

WHEREAS, the current Lease expires on August 31%, 2028; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Ind. Code 36-1-10-7, if the annual cost of a proposed lease is $250,000 or
more, the leasing agent (IMPD) cannot lease a structure unless it receives: (1) a petition signed by fifty (50)
or more taxpayers of Marion County; and (2) the fiscal body (the City-County Council) determines, after
investigation, that the structure is needed.

WHEREAS, IMPD wishes to enter into a new lease to supersede current lease, fo extend the term
through January 315, 2031.

WHEREAS, IMPD wishes to continue to occupy 4140 N Keystone Ave to accommodate the following
IMPD units: domestic violence, sex crimes, child abuse, missing persons, pawn, and fraud. The housing of
the units together creates greater ease of communication between the units and reduces the need for
additional leases.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council, pursuant to IC 36-1-10-7, has investigated conditions as to the need
for the subject office space lease with a term of 5 years with one (1) renewal options which lease consisting
of approximately twenty-seven thousand and ten (27,010) square feet for use by the Indianapolis
Metropolitan Police Department, and hereby determines the lease is needed.

SECTION 2. The property to be lease is located at 4140 N. Keystone Ave. and is owned by GIP Indy
Keystone 4150, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall be in effect from and after its passage by the Council and compliance
with Indiana Code § 36-3-4-14.

The foregoing was passed by the City-County Council this 2nd day of February, 2026, at 7:29 p.m.

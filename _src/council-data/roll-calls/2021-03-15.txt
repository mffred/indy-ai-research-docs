MOTION TO AMEND 
Proposal No. 106, 2021 
 
 
Mr. President, 
 
 I move to amend Section 1 of Proposal No. 106, 2021 by deleting the language that is stricken -
through and adding the underlined language in the highlighted portions, to read as follows: 
 
 
 ___________________________ 
 Councillor Barth 
 
SECTION 1.  Title IV of the “Revised Code of the Consolidated City and County,” regarding business 
and commercial regulations and licenses, hereby is amended by the addition of a NEW Chapter 812 to 
read as follows: 
 
Sec. 812-101.  Purpose and application. 
 
This chapter is adopted for the purpose of establishing a temporary limit on the charges and fees 
imposed by Third -Party Delivery Services on Retail Food Establishments during the COVID -19 
pandemic public health emergencies  and preventing those charges from being borne by customers 
and drivers for Third-Party Delivery Services. 
 
This chapter shall not be construed or interpreted to interfere with or impair any contracts 
between retail food establishments and third-party food delivery services that exist as of the effective 
date of this chapter. This chapter applies to all contracts that begin on or after the effective date of 
this chapter and any renewals or extensions of contracts when those renewals or extensions occur 
during the effective period of this cha pter, even if the extension or renewal is on the same terms as 
or authorized by the original contracts. 
 
This chapter shall be in effect only when a Marion County Public Health Order or applicable 
State mandate limits the capacity of indoor dining and for up to 90 days after the expiration or 
rescission of all local or state restrictions limiting indoor dining capacity. 
 
Sec. 812-102.  Definitions.  
 
As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this 
section: 
 
Retail Food Establishment shall have the meaning ascribed thereto in Indiana Administrative 
Code under 410 IAC 7 -24-79, including but not limited to  a restaurant, a catering operation, a 
grocery store, a tavern, or a food bank.  
 
Third-Party Food Delivery Serv ice means any website, mobile application, or other internet 
service that offers or arranges for the sale of food and beverages prepared by, and the delivery or 
pick-up of food and beverages from, no fewer than 20 Retail Food Establishments located in the 
Consolidate City-County that are each owned and operated by different persons.  
 
Delivery Fee means a fee charged by a  Third-Party Food Delivery Service for providing a  Retail 
Food Establishment  with a service that delivers food from such establishment to customers. The 
term does not include any other fee that may be charged by a  Third-Party Food Delivery Service to 
a Retail Food Establishment, such as fees for listing or advertising the  Retail Food Establishment on 
the Third-Party Food Delivery Service  platform or fees related to processing the online 
order, including, but not limited to, service fees, fees for facilitating Online Orders for pick -up, and 
credit card processing fees.

Online Order means an order placed by a customer through or with the assi stance of a platform 
provided by a Third -Party Food Delivery Service, including a telephone order, for delivery or pick -up 
within the City.  
 
Purchase Price means the price, as listed on the menu, for the items contained in an Online 
Order, minus any applicable coupon or promotional discount provided to the customer  by the Retail 
Food Establishment through the Third -Party Food Delivery Service. This definition does not include 
taxes, gratuities, and any other fees or costs that may make up the total amount charged to the 
customer of an Online Order.  
 
Sec. 812-103. Prohibited Actions.  
 
It shall be unlawful for a Third-Party Food Delivery Service to:  
 
(1) Charge a Retail Food Establishment a Delivery Fee that totals more than  15 percent of the 
Purchase Price of each Online Order on an individual or cumulative basis;  
 
(2) Charge a Retail Food Establishment any fee or fees other than a Delivery Fee for the use of 
their service greater than 5 percent of the Purchase Price of each Online Order. Any fees 
from a Third -Party Food Delivery Service to a Retail Food Establishment beyond such 
maximum 5 percent, and a Delivery Fee collected pursuant to  subdivision (I) of this section 
are unlawful;  
 
(3) Charge a customer any price for a food or beverage item that is higher than the  
Purchase Price; or  
 
(4) Reduce the compensation rates paid to the Third-Party Delivery Service drivers or to garnish 
gratuities, as a result of any fee limitations instituted by this section.  
  
Sec. 812-104. Enforcement and Penalties.  
  
(a) This chapter sh all be enforced by the department of business and neighborhood services or 
its designee.  
 
(b) A Third -Party Food Delivery Service which fails to comply with the provisions of this chapter 
shall be subject to admission of violation and payment of the desig nated civil penalty as provided in 
chapter 103-52 of this Code.  
 
(c) Each day on which a violation of this chapter occurs shall be considered a separate and 
distinct violation.  
 
(d) Violations under this subchapter shall accrue daily for each day and for  each Retail Food 
Establishment charged a fee in violation of the subchapter of any rule promulgated pursuant to this 
subchapter.  
  
Sec. 812-105. Sunset.  
 
This article shall sunset 90 days after  the Health and Hospital Corporation of Marion County’s 
Public Health  2020 Order limiting capacity of indoor dining at Retail Food Establishments 
is either rescinded or expires,  and Retail Food Establishments can operate at 100% 
capacity indoors.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL MOTION
Proposal No. 192, 2026

Madam President:

Proposal No. 192, 2026 revises certain provisions of Chapter 121 of the Code to amend

the county's excise and wheel taxes and to add a new Division 12 entitled State and

Local Road Funding. The Administration and Finance and Public Works Committees

heard presentations in meetings held in different parts of the County, with final

recommendation taken by the Rules and Public Policy Committee on June 16, 2026 to

recommend the Proposal for adoption by a vote of 7-3. The full Council adopted the
Proposal as General Ordinance No. 44, 2026 on July 6, 2026 by a vote of 14-10.
Subsequently, the Mayor vetoed Proposal No. 192, 2026 (General Ordinance No. 44,
2026) on July 16, 2026. Sec. 451-78 of the Council Rules lays out the process for

reconsideration of a veto; which reads as follows:

“If the mayor shall veto any ordinance of general resolution of a council, upon receipt of

the communication from the mayor so informing the council and at the next succeeding

regular meeting of the council (which the Clerk read at the beginning of the meeting

under Official Communications), the ordinance or general resolution shall be made a

special order of unfinished business. Upon coming to that order of business, the

the question: "Shall the ordinance or resolution be effective

president shall state
If the question receives the statutory majority of two-thirds

notwithstanding the veto?"
(%) of the members of the council (which would require 17 votes of the Council:body),

the ordinance shall be in effect.”

Therefore, Madam President, | would move that the ordinance be effective

notwithstanding the veto, and that the veto be overturned by a vote of this body.

Councilor Nielsen

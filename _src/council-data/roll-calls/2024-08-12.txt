CITY-COUNTY GENERAL RESOLUTION NO. ___, 2024
PROPOSAL NO. ___ 2024

PROPOSAL FOR A GENERAL RESOLUTION to authorize the Rules Public Policy Committee to review
the city’s current processes and procedures associated with sexual harassment of city/county employees
and the training to prevent such.

WHEREAS, the city county council is committed to working to determine the necessary changes to the
city’s policies and systems to ensure a safe work environment for all employees, now and into the future; and

WHEREAS, the council's priority is to create a workplace where every employee feels secure, valued,
and supported; and

WHEREAS, the council is focused on implementing improvements that will bring greater accountability
for all leaders, whether elected, appointed, or hired; now, therefore:

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby authorizes the Rules and Public Policy Committee (Rules)
to investigate the need for separate branch of Human Resources from the Administration that explicitly
addresses sexual harassment and other discrimination related complaints.

SECTION 2. The Rules Committee is authorized to investigate the need for a separate investigative
commission/committee that will engage subject matter experts to evaluate the city’s current policies, identify
gaps, and implement best practices that will foster a culture of safety, respect, and accountability. This
includes, but not limited to, the use of the Employee Assistance Program and/or the evaluation of past
actions.

SECTION 3. The Rules Committee is authorized to propose a further General Ordinance modifying Revised
Code Section 293-304 to include all city county employees in an annual mandatory sexual harassment
training.

SECTION 4. The Rules Committee is authorized to take such further action as needed to ensure that all city
employees are working in a safe work environment which is foundational fo the well-being and productivity.

City-County Resolution No. 2024

A RESOLUTION forming a Committee to investigate allegations against the Mayor’s
Office concerning the conduct of former Chief of Staff Thomas Cook

WHEREAS, the City-County Council has the authority to create Investigative Committees
for any lawful purpose pursuant to Indianapolis-Marion County Municipal Code Sec. 151-
33; and

WHEREAS, Thomas Cook was a high-ranking city official, serving as Chief of Staff for Mayor
Joe Hogsett for several years; and

WHEREAS, in recent months, numerous women have come forward with allegations that
Thomas Cook engaged in inappropriate behavior, including sexual harassment,
manipulation, grooming, and creating a hostile work environment during his tenure as Chief
of Staff; and

WHEREAS, it has been alleged that individuals informed the Mayor’s Office, including Mayor
Joe Hogsett personally, about Thomas Cook’s inappropriate behavior, and that the Mayor’s
Office failed to take appropriate action, thereby contributing to a hostile work environment;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
INDIANAPOLIS AND OF MARION COUNTY, INDIANA:

SECTION 1. The indianapolis City-County Council hereby creates an Investigative
Committee to examine the allegations made by any employees of the City of Indianapolis
and/or Marion County and the handling of these allegations by the Mayor and the Mayor’s
Office. The purpose of the committee is to review the allegations and determine if any
wrongdoing has been committed by either the Mayor or the Mayor’s office and limit any
future instances of sexual harassment and hostile workplace environment within the city-
county government.

SECTION 2. The investigative Committee will be composed of six (6) from the majority party
and three (3) members from the minority party. The members of the committee shall be
appointed by the Committee on Committees by August 19, 2024. The chair of the
Investigation Committee shall be appointed by the Committee on Committees.

SECTION 3. The Investigative Committee shall meet at least once a month, on the third
Tuesday of each month, until its investigation is concluded.

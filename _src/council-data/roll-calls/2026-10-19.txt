CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL MOTION
Proposal No. 264, 2026

Madam President

In order to permit some Councilors with personal interests in portions of Proposal
No. 264, 2026 to abstain on those portions, | move to divide the question on the
adoption of that proposal as follows:

Question 1 - The appropriations for community development grants set forth in
Section 4.01 (b), and the appropriations for crime prevention grants set forth in
Section 4.01 (d) of Proposal No. 264, 2026 (Lewis, Carlino)

Question 2 — The appropriations for public purpose grants set forth in Section
4.01 (c) of Proposal No. 264, 2026. (Lewis, Annee, Dilk)

Question 3 - The appropriations for the Department of Metropolitan Development
set forth in Section 1.017 (c), Character 03 of Proposal No. 264, 2026. (Boots)

Question 4 — The appropriations for the Office of Public Health and Safety set
forth in Section 1.01 (f), Character 03 of Proposal No. 264, 2026. (Osili)

Question 5 - The appropriations for the Marion County Auditor set forth in Section
1.04 (b), Character 03; the appropriations for the Election Board set forth in
Section 1.05 (a), Character 03; the appropriations for the Office of the Marion
County Public Defender set forth in Section 1.06 (b), Character 03; and the
appropriations for the Office of Corporation Counsel set forth in Section 1.04 (b)
(3), Character 03, of Proposal No. 264, 2026. (Delaney)

Question 6 - The balance of Proposal No. 264, 2026.

Councilor Barth

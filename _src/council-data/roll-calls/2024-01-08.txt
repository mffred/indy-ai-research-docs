=== 2024-01-08 roll call, page 11 of 15 ===
ANNOUNCEMENT OF A PUBLIC HEARING
ON A PETITION FOR REZONING

Mr. President:

This Council will hold a public hearing on Rezoning Petition No. 2023-ZON-
094, Council Proposal No. 30, 2024, at its next regular meeting on February 5,
2024, such meeting to convene at 7:00 p.m. in these Council Chambers in the
City-County Building in Indianapolis. This petition proposes to rezone 21.44 acres
at 6729 Westfield Boulevard from the SU-34 (FF) and D-P (FF) Districts to the D-P

(FF) classification to provide for a multi-family and townhome development

Written objections that are filed with the Clerk of the Council shall be heard at

such time, or the hearing may be continued from time to time as found necessary

by the Council.

CITY-COUNTY COUNCIL MOTION
Proposal No. 190, 2026

Madam President:

At the Public Safety and Criminal Justice Committee hearing on June 10, 2026; the committee
voted to divide the question with regard to Proposal No. 190, 2026. I would like to again move to
divide the question regarding Proposal No. 190, 2026 into two separate votes as follows:

Question 1 - Approving Sections 1-4 of Proposal No. 190, 2026, which reads as follows:

SECTION 1. The City-County Council hereby calls upon the director of OPHS to create a centralized system for
tracking and managing contracts and invoices or otherwise utilize currently-existing processes or systerns of the
City-County enterprise. Additionally, the Council hereby calls for ongoing training for all staff of the office fo ensure
a general understanding of how to comply with the duties given to the OPHS. If the director does not comply with
these requirements, the City-County Council will call upon the Mayor's office to replace the director.

SECTION 2. The City-County Council hereby calls upon the Office of the Corporate Counsel to provide in-person
ethics training for the staff of OPHS, within the next six months, followed by monitoring OPHS to ensure all current
and new employees comply with the current online training requirements.

SECTION 3. The City-County Council hereby calls upon the Mayor's office and the Office of Corporation Counsel
fo modify the ordinance to increase the training of ethical requirements for all of city and county departments and
agencies.

SECTION 4, The City-County Council hereby calls upon the Office of Finance and Management to establish a

monitoring system for OPHS that will ensure compliance with both city and state accounting policies and
procedures.

Question 2 - Approving Section 5 of Proposal No. 190, 2026, which reads as follows:

SECTION 5. The City-County Council hereby calls upon the OPHS to take the any and all steps needed to
address the findings made in the Audit Report and to fully cooperate with the other departments and agencies
listed herein. Until this is done, the City-County Council will freeze any additional funding that goes to OPHS.

| so move to divide the question, Madam President.

Councilor Hart

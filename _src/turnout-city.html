How Indianapolis votes

    Notes on this data

    • Official results, as published. Every count comes from the Marion County Election Board's precinct-level result files. Nothing here is a projection or estimate. District and county totals are sums of the precincts.
    • Turnout is ballots cast divided by registered voters in that precinct for that election. Registration changes between elections, so each election uses its own count.
    • Party margin compares the Democratic and Republican candidates in the race named under each election, counting only their two vote totals (other candidates are left out but shown in the precinct details). "D+20" means the Democrat got 20 points more of those votes than the Republican (60% to 40%); "R+20" is the reverse. In primaries it compares how many voters chose each party's side: votes in each party's mayor primary in 2023, party ballots in 2024, and votes for each party's unopposed prosecutor nominee in 2026. Indiana primaries are open, so a voter picks a party's ballot on Election Day.
    • 2023 mayor shows every candidate for Mayor of Indianapolis in the November 2023 general and in each party's May 2023 primary, colored by who got the most votes in each district or precinct. A primary map counts only that party's voters; a shade's strength is the winner's share of the votes in that race.
    • 2024 primary ballots count Democratic and Republican ballots. A few voters took a nonpartisan ballot where there was a local question; those aren't in the 2024 primary turnout here.
    • The May 2026 primary has turnout for the county only. The city published candidate votes for that primary but not registered voters or ballots cast by precinct, so districts and precincts show only the party split. The county figure (99,923 voters of 632,168 registered, 15.81%) is from the Election Board's turnout page. A records request to the Election Board could fill this in.
    • Precincts and districts. The same 621 precincts were used in all six elections. Each precinct's council district comes from the 2023 council races, which list every precinct by district. Council districts were last redrawn in 2022. Three precincts with no registered voters (PE007, WY043, WY056) are blank.
    • Cities and towns. A precinct is labeled Beech Grove, Lawrence, Southport or Speedway if it had votes in that city's or town's own 2023 race, so a precinct partly inside a city carries its name.
    • The map draws the city's precinct and district boundaries, lightly simplified to keep the page fast, with major roads from the city's street centerlines. Zoomed in, the street map is OpenStreetMap and the aerial photo is the city's spring 2024 flight.
